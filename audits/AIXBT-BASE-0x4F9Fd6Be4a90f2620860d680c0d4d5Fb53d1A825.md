## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A 1 billion supply AI research agent token" / Reality: the identity and near full circulation are confirmed on chain. The item most listings omit is the live 1 percent tax on both buys and sells, which the contract confirms, and the owner key can adjust template parameters such as tax within template limits.

The project and aggregator descriptions present AIXBT as a one billion supply Base token powering an AI driven crypto research agent, and the public data supports this. On chain total supply reads about 998.9 million tokens, which is close to a one billion cap, the tiny shortfall consistent with a small burn rather than any inflation. Circulating figures cluster near 990 million, so the token is nearly fully circulating. The main mismatch is emphasis rather than fact, because many marketing and listing pages do not mention that the contract charges a live one percent tax on both buys and sells, which the RPC confirms with buy and sell tax basis points both equal to one hundred.
