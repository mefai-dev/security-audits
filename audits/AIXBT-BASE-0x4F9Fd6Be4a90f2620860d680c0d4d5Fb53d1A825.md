## Why This Report Exists

aixbt by Virtuals is one of the most recognized AI agents in crypto, at its peak the mindshare leader among Virtuals Protocol agents. The pitch is simple and seductive: an autonomous AI that reads the market better than you, and a token that lets you buy into that edge and unlock its terminal. The token is real and live. The product is real and live. The question this report answers is narrower and harder: does the code actually connect the token to the agent, is the tax what it claims to be, and is any of the AI verifiable. The answer is that the plumbing is a clean and standard Virtuals token, the tax is exactly 1 percent and provably routed, but the thing you are actually paying for, the AI edge, is a closed box that no on chain or public artifact can confirm, and the token captures almost none of the product's value on chain.

## Claim 1: "An autonomous AI agent that delivers real market intelligence"

**CLAIM.** aixbt is marketed as an autonomous AI crypto market intelligence agent, and the token is positioned as a way to own exposure to that intelligence.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED. Core AI closed and unverifiable.** The agent model, its training, its data pipeline, and its methodology are entirely proprietary. There is no public repository, no published weights, no disclosed prompt or ranking algorithm, and no on chain attestation of any agent output. The public site (aixbt.tech) renders a real time ranking of the 25 most discussed crypto topics with source category tags, but the page discloses nothing about how the ranking is computed. Nothing verifiable distinguishes an autonomous large language model agent from a curated aggregation feed. Per audit policy, the model's claimed signal or trading performance is given no credit whatsoever, because none of it can be independently reproduced or checked.

**EVIDENCE.** No GitHub source exists for the agent. aixbt.tech exposes output only, no methodology. There is no on chain hook that records or proves agent inference.

**IMPACT.** The entire value thesis of the token rests on an unauditable black box. This is a first order transparency finding, not a code bug: a buyer cannot verify the product they are pricing.

## Claim 2: "Hold AIXBT to unlock the terminal (600,000 AIXBT for access)"

**CLAIM.** Holding a threshold of AIXBT (widely cited as 600,000 tokens) unlocks the advanced aixbt terminal; the token is framed as a utility and access token.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED. The gate is real but enforced entirely off chain.** The `AgentToken` contract contains no staking, no lock, and no access control tied to balance. Direct selector probes against the live token confirm the absence: `stake(uint256)`, `stakedBalance(address)`, and `hasTerminalAccess(address)` all revert (function not present). Terminal gating, if it exists, is a server side balance check performed by aixbt's own infrastructure, which can be changed, waived, or removed at any time with zero on chain trace. There is no smart contract that binds token ownership to terminal access.

**EVIDENCE.** `AgentToken` ABI (Sourcify verified) exposes only ERC20 transfer, tax, and liquidity functions. On chain calls to `stake`, `stakedBalance`, and `hasTerminalAccess` on `0x4F9Fd6Be4a90f2620860d680c0d4d5Fb53d1A825` revert. The public homepage displays no token requirement.

**IMPACT.** The token's headline utility has no on chain enforcement. Access is a promise kept in a private database, not a property of the token. If the team changes the policy, holders have no contractual recourse.

## Claim 3: "A 1 percent transfer tax funds the project"

**CLAIM.** Prior coverage and the token page note a 1 percent transfer tax.

**REALITY: CONFIRMED IN CODE, with one labeling correction.** The rate is exactly 1 percent and it is a buy and sell tax, not a tax on ordinary wallet to wallet transfers. In `AgentToken._taxProcessing`, tax is applied only when the counterparty is a registered liquidity pool: `if (isLiquidityPool(from_) ...)` on buys and `if (isLiquidityPool(to_) ...)` on sells, computing `projectTax = (sentAmount * projectSellTaxBasisPoints) / BP_DENOM`. Live reads confirm `projectBuyTaxBasisPoints == 100` and `projectSellTaxBasisPoints == 100` (100 basis points is 1.00 percent). Accrued tax sits as `projectTaxPendingSwap` (currently 9,019.70 AIXBT) until swapped.

The destination is fully traceable on chain and is a standard Virtuals pipeline, not a wallet:

1. Tax accrues inside the `AgentToken` and is pulled by `distributeTaxTokens()`.
2. `projectTaxRecipient` is the verified `TaxSwapper` contract `0x8e0253da409faf5918fe2a15979fd878f4495d0e`. Its `swapTax` function calls `uniswapRouter.swapExactTokensForTokensSupportingFeeOnTransferTokens(..., taxRecipient, ...)`, converting the AIXBT tax into the asset token.
3. The asset token is `0x0b3e328455c4059EEb9e3f84b5543F74E24e7E1b`, confirmed on chain as `Virtual Protocol` (symbol `VIRTUAL`).
4. TaxSwapper's `taxRecipient` is `0x7E26173192D72fd6D75A759F888d61c2cdbB64B1`, a `TransparentUpgradeableProxy` whose implementation is `AgentTax` at `0xB7A6C94B6C388E885B148dA4b505aB2997bad72B`. It currently holds about 51,034 VIRTUAL.

So the true tax flow is: 1 percent of each DEX trade in AIXBT, collected as AIXBT, swapped to VIRTUAL, forwarded to the Virtuals `AgentTax` distributor.

**EVIDENCE.** Selectors and reads above; `TaxSwapper` and `AgentToken` verified source on Blockscout; asset token symbol read live as VIRTUAL.

**IMPACT.** The tax is genuine, modest, and routed to real Virtuals infrastructure rather than straight to the owner EOA (which holds 0 AIXBT). The only labeling issue is that calling it a "transfer tax" overstates its reach: sending AIXBT between wallets is untaxed. Note the destination `AgentTax` is upgradeable, so the ultimate distribution rules can be changed by whoever controls that proxy admin.

## Claim 4: "On chain mechanism ties AIXBT to agent revenue"

**CLAIM.** The token is presented as capturing the agent's revenue and success, via the Virtuals bonding curve launch and ongoing tokenomics.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED to FALSE, depending on the sub claim.** The Virtuals bonding curve launch was real (aixbt graduated to a Uniswap pair long ago), and the 1 percent trade tax to VIRTUAL described in Claim 3 is the one real, recurring on chain value flow. But there is no mechanism that returns any of this to AIXBT holders. There is no staking reward, no dividend, no fee share, and no buyback and burn contract wired to holders. The tax funds a Virtuals side `AgentTax` recipient (project and ecosystem side), not the wallets of token holders. The claim that holding AIXBT accrues agent revenue to the holder is FALSE at the contract level: holders benefit only if speculative price rises. The 4,055,863 AIXBT sitting at the `0x…dead` burn address and the roughly 1.08 million net reduction in supply are the only deflationary signal, and neither is a promised, enforced, holder facing reward.

**EVIDENCE.** No `stake`, `claim`, `distribute to holders`, or `buyback` function on the token (probes revert). Tax terminates at the Virtuals `AgentTax` proxy, not at holder addresses.

**IMPACT.** The economic link between "the agent does well" and "my AIXBT is worth more" is entirely reflexive and off chain. On chain, holders own a claim to nothing except transfer rights.

## Claim 5: Centralization and owner powers

**REALITY: MIXED.** The good news dominates the malicious vectors: there is no external `mint` (only an internal `_mint` reachable at initialize, so the owner cannot inflate supply), the contract is not `Pausable`, and there is no blacklist (`blacklist` and `isBlacklisted` probes revert). Owner cannot freeze, seize, or dilute. The concern is `setProjectTaxRates`, which is `onlyOwnerOrFactory` and enforces no maximum cap: the parameters are raw `uint16`, so the owner EOA or the Virtuals factory can raise the buy or sell tax with no ceiling checked in the setter and no timelock. In practice a hostile owner could push the sell tax high enough to function as a soft honeypot on exits. The owner is an unrenounced externally owned account (`0xe220329659d41b2a9f26e83816b424bdacf62567`), not a multisig, and `setProjectTaxRecipient` lets that owner redirect the tax stream. Ownership uses `Ownable2Step`, which is safer than single step transfer.

**IMPACT.** No rug via mint, freeze, or blacklist. Residual risk is the uncapped, owner adjustable trade tax plus the upgradeable downstream collector, both requiring trust in the key holder and in Virtuals.

## Positive Findings (Credited)

- **No inflation vector.** Supply is not owner mintable; only an internal `_mint` at initialization exists. Confirmed in verified source.
- **No freeze, no blacklist, not pausable.** Transfers cannot be halted or addresses censored. Selector probes for pause and blacklist revert.
- **Verified, reused implementation.** The token is a Sourcify verified Virtuals `AgentToken` minimal proxy (Solidity 0.8.20), not a bespoke unaudited contract.
- **Real, live product.** aixbt.tech serves a live, minutes fresh ranking feed. This is not vaporware; the flagship product exists and updates.
- **Clean tax routing.** The 1 percent tax flows to a real Virtuals `TaxSwapper` then `AgentTax` distributor and is converted to VIRTUAL, not siphoned to the owner EOA (which holds 0 AIXBT).
- **Modest, honest tax rate.** 1 percent buy and 1 percent sell is low by memecoin standards and matches the stated figure.

## Conclusion

aixbt is a genuine, well known product wrapped around a technically clean, standard Virtuals token. On the contract axis it scores well: no mint, no pause, no blacklist, verified code, and a fully traceable 1 percent buy and sell tax that routes to real Virtuals infrastructure rather than to a private wallet. Those facts limit the contract level risk. What holds the score down is the gap between the token and the thing it supposedly represents. The core AI is a closed, unverifiable box whose performance cannot be credited. The headline utility, terminal access at 600,000 AIXBT, is enforced off chain with zero contract backing. There is no on chain revenue share, staking reward, or buyback that returns the agent's success to holders, so the token captures almost none of the product's value on chain. Layer on an unrenounced EOA owner who can raise the trade tax with no coded cap, an upgradeable downstream tax collector, and a price sitting about 98 percent below its January 2025 all time high of $0.9426 (roughly $0.0173, market cap near $17.3 million, 24 hour volume near $3.8 million), and the picture is a real project with a real product whose token is a largely speculative, off chain gated claim on an unverifiable AI. Flagged, because the uncapped, owner settable trade tax with a redirectable recipient held by an unrenounced single key is a latent soft honeypot: benign at 1 percent today, but a single transaction can raise the sell tax toward 99 percent or block sells entirely, so it clears to Passed only on a renounced owner, a coded tax cap, or a multisig and timelock owner.
