## Executive Summary

Claim vs reality audit of Cudos (CUDOS). A decentralized compute network whose Ethereum token is a fixed 10 billion, non mintable, non upgradeable, fee free asset with permanently enabled transfers, whose main consideration is deprecation rather than contract risk, since it is actively migrating into the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance FET token.

Cudos is a decentralized compute and cloud network that markets GPU and cloud infrastructure for AI workloads. It runs a native Cosmos based Cudos chain and issued the CUDOS token as an Ethereum ERC20 at 0x817bbDbC3e8A1204f3691d14bB44992841e3dB35. In October 2024 Cudos joined the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance as a compute partner, and an official migration now converts CUDOS into FET at a rate of about 112 CUDOS to one FET, so the ERC20 is being wound down in favor of the Alliance token.

## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A fixed 10 billion supply compute token" / Reality: confirmed exactly on chain with no mint path. The project has genuinely joined the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance and runs a live migration converting CUDOS to FET, so the Ethereum token is now a legacy asset the team directs holders to migrate.

Official documentation states a fixed total supply of ten billion CUDOS minted once at genesis, and public chain data confirms this precisely, because total supply reads ten billion tokens at eighteen decimals and no mint function exists in the verified source. Market trackers historically reported circulating supply near ninety nine percent of the cap, but the live migration is steadily reducing the effective ERC20 float as holders convert to FET, and current tracker figures for the remaining circulating amount diverge widely. Holders should treat the ERC20 as a legacy asset that the team openly directs users to migrate.
