## Executive Summary

Cudos presents itself as a decentralized cloud and compute network for AI workloads, and at the contract level the CUDOS token is one of the cleaner ERC 20 tokens MEFAI has reviewed. The problem for anyone assessing CUDOS as a standalone project and token is not safety, it is continuity: the project has been absorbed into the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance and the token is being wound down in favor of FET.

1. **The token contract is genuinely clean.** MEFAI's onchain read confirms a fixed supply of ten billion CUDOS minted once at genesis, no reachable mint path, no proxy, no fee on transfer, and transfers permanently enabled. Ownership is not the standard pattern and the residual admin cannot mint, seize, or restrict transfers. Contract level risk is low.
2. **The standalone project is in migration and wind down.** In October 2024 Cudos joined the ASI Alliance as a compute partner, the native Cudos Cosmos mainnet has been halted, and an official swap converts CUDOS into FET at a rate of about 112.427 to 1, adjusted to roughly 118.344 after a 5 percent merger fee. The Ethereum ERC 20 is explicitly a legacy asset the team directs holders to migrate.
3. **The compute product survives, but it is no longer Cudos's own.** The GPU and cloud infrastructure now ships as ASI:Cloud under the ASI Alliance and Fetch.ai, and FET, not CUDOS, is the payment and utility token of that product. The cudos.org homepage itself now headlines ASI:Cloud rather than a standalone CUDOS token economy.
4. **Token utility and traction are transitional to nil.** CUDOS was delisted or paused at major venues during the merge, including Binance.US and KuCoin in September 2024, ERC 20 liquidity is thin, and remaining circulating figures diverge across trackers as holders convert. The token no longer fuels its own product.

The contract is not a scam and the product is not broken. This is instead a clean, honestly signposted migration of a project that, as a standalone token and standalone product, is effectively being retired. That lands Cudos at 50 out of 100, Flagged, on deprecation and absence of independent utility rather than on any contract defect.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the CUDOS contract on Ethereum returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | CudosToken, symbol CUDOS, 18 decimals, verified |
| Max supply | 10,000,000,000, minted once in the constructor, matches the onchain total |
| Mint authority | None reachable after deployment; supply is fixed and immutable |
| Pause authority | No live pause; a one time transfers enabled gate exists and now reads true, so transfers are permanently open and cannot be frozen |
| Upgradeable | No, the implementation storage slot reads empty and the contract is not a proxy (Solidity 0.6.12) |
| Transfer fee | None |
| Ownership | Not the standard Ownable pattern; residual admin via CudosAccessControls cannot mint, seize, or restrict transfers |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level CUDOS is among the safer ERC 20 tokens MEFAI reviews: a hard fixed supply with no emission, no proxy, no fee, no live pause, and no privileged party able to mint, seize, or freeze balances. Verified open source is published under the CudoVentures GitHub. The residual risk here is not a contract exploit and not centralization of balances; it is that the token itself is being deprecated. The real concerns for this project sit at the project, product, and continuity level below, not in the token contract.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Supercharge your AI development with ASI:Cloud"

> Site: cudos.org presents Cudos as a decentralized cloud and compute network for AI, headlined around ASI:Cloud, GPU provisioning, and model inference.

**Reality: the product is real, but it is no longer a standalone Cudos product and it does not run on CUDOS.** The compute infrastructure genuinely exists and has processed billions of inference tokens, but it now ships as ASI:Cloud under the ASI Alliance and Fetch.ai, with FET as the payment and utility token for compute and inference. The Cudos brand today is effectively the compute layer of another network rather than an independent product with its own token economy. A reader following the CUDOS token expecting it to fuel the compute shown on the homepage will find that the settlement and utility asset is FET, not CUDOS.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: "Cudos runs its own chain and token" (ASI migration and token absorption)

> Site and docs: historically Cudos ran a native Cosmos based chain and issued CUDOS as its token, and public trackers still list CUDOS as a tradable asset.

**Reality: the standalone chain is halted and the token is being absorbed into FET.** In October 2024 Cudos joined the ASI Alliance, the native Cudos Cosmos mainnet was halted, and an official governance approved swap converts CUDOS into FET at about 112.427 to 1, adjusted to roughly 118.344 after a 5 percent merger fee, with vesting spread over several months. The team openly describes the merger as complete and directs holders to migrate. The Ethereum ERC 20 at `0x817bbDbC3e8A1204f3691d14bB44992841e3dB35` therefore functions as a legacy migration rail into FET rather than as the live token of an operating standalone network. This is the central fact of the audit: CUDOS as an independent token has no forward roadmap of its own, only a conversion path into FET.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: CUDOS Utility, Liquidity, and Traction

> Implication: CUDOS is a live, tradable, and useful token backing a growing compute network.

**Reality: transitional to nil utility, thinning liquidity, and delistings.** CUDOS does not pay for ASI:Cloud compute or inference, since FET performs that role, so the token has no product utility in the product it is marketed alongside. During the merge, major venues delisted or paused CUDOS, including Binance.US on September 29, 2024 and KuCoin from late September 2024, and remaining ERC 20 liquidity is thin. Circulating figures now diverge widely across trackers because the float is being reduced as holders convert to FET. What activity the compute network generates is counted under the ASI Alliance and denominated in FET, not in CUDOS. The token is a legacy asset in orderly wind down, not the fuel of a thriving standalone economy.

---

## 6. Claim vs Reality: Migration Safety and User Guidance

> Site: the project directs holders to migrate CUDOS to FET.

**Reality: the migration itself is handled responsibly, which is a genuine positive.** MEFAI's frontend review found the cudos.org marketing site to be a plain content site with no Web3 attack surface: no wallet connect flow, no signature or approval prompt, and no contract or wallet address embedded in the HTML or JavaScript, so there is no on page vector for a migrate your CUDOS to FET drainer or a lookalike address swap. External scripts fall into ordinary categories such as cookie consent, email signup, analytics, image CDN, and official ASI Alliance partner domains, with no obfuscated or remote crypto payloads. The real migration is intentionally hosted off the marketing site on the channel the ASI Alliance publicly designates as the sole official migration page. The main caveat is access, since the live domain sits behind bot protection, so this rests on a recent archived snapshot and the core bundles. Users should still treat any third party site promising CUDOS to FET migration with suspicion and verify against the official ASI channels.

## 7. Positive Findings (Credited)

- The CUDOS token contract is clean: a hard fixed ten billion supply minted once, no reachable mint path, no proxy, no fee on transfer, and no live pause.
- No privileged party can mint, seize, or freeze balances; the residual admin behind CudosAccessControls cannot restrict transfers, which now read permanently enabled.
- The contract is verified open source under the CudoVentures GitHub, and the implementation slot confirms it is non upgradeable.
- The token migration is transparent and well signposted, routed through official ASI Alliance channels, and the marketing site carries no wallet interaction or drainer surface.
- The underlying compute product is real and operating, now as ASI:Cloud within the ASI Alliance.

## 11. Conclusion

Cudos is the unusual case of a clean contract attached to a project that is being retired. At the token level CUDOS is genuinely low risk: a fixed ten billion supply minted once, no mint path, no proxy, no fee, no live pause, and no party able to mint, seize, or freeze balances, all confirmed onchain and backed by verified open source. What pulls the score down is not safety but continuity and utility. The standalone Cudos Cosmos chain is halted, the token is being converted into FET at about 112.427 to 1 under the ASI Alliance, the compute product now ships as ASI:Cloud on FET rather than on CUDOS, and the token has been delisted or paused at major venues with liquidity thinning as holders migrate. As a standalone project and token, CUDOS has no independent product and effectively no forward utility; it is an orderly, transparent wind down into FET. That is why Cudos scores 50 out of 100 and is Flagged, on deprecation and absence of standalone utility rather than on any contract or product exploit.

---

## 12. Recommendations

**For the Cudos and ASI teams:**
- Continue to state plainly, on cudos.org and on trackers, that CUDOS is a legacy asset whose forward path is conversion to FET, so buyers do not mistake it for a live standalone token.
- Publish a clear end state for the ERC 20, including any migration deadline and the treatment of unconverted balances.
- Keep the official migration page the single canonical destination and actively warn against lookalike migration sites.

**For users:**
- Treat CUDOS as a migration asset, not a standalone investment; its value rests on completing the FET conversion, and the standalone network and product no longer run on it.
- Migrate only through the official ASI Alliance channel and verify addresses independently, since a token in a public migration is a natural phishing target.
- Note that ASI:Cloud compute is paid in FET, so holding CUDOS grants no product utility.
