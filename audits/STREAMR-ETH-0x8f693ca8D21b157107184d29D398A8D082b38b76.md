## Executive Summary

Streamr is one of the older names in the DePIN category, a decentralized real time data network that has been building since 2017, and unlike many projects that sell a future, its product is genuinely live and its token is genuinely used. The audit finds an honest, transparent project whose main open item sits in the supply mechanics of the token rather than in any broken promise.

1. **The DePIN is real and DATA is actually used.** The Hub application on Polygon manages Operators and Sponsorships in production. The project's own Q1 2026 transparency report shows tens of millions of DATA locked in Operators and Sponsorships, sponsorship rewards funded in DATA, and network protocol fees paid in DATA. This is a working economy, not a slogan.

2. **The contract and the marketing agree.** DATAv2 is a clean, immutable OpenZeppelin style ERC 20 with no transfer fee, no pause switch and no upgrade proxy, and the documentation candidly states each of these facts. Every claim MEFAI checked against the live contract matched with no contradiction. The public site is a static marketing page that fetches its network figures from the project's own live metrics API rather than faking them, and its audit links point to genuine Cyfrin and Isentropy reports hosted on the official Streamr storage bucket.

3. **The one material risk is uncapped minting.** DATA is mintable with no hardcoded cap. A MINTER_ROLE can mint at will and an active DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE controls the minter set. The two billion max supply figure shown on market trackers is a market convention, not a coded ceiling, so DATA carries a real inflation vector that is governed only socially through Streamr Improvement Proposals.

4. **Traction is real but softening.** Active staked operators fell from 159 to 93 across Q1 2026, roughly a 41 percent drop, and DATA locked in Operators and Sponsorships declined about 20 percent to 74.3M. The network runs and settles value in its own token, but the measured usage is modest and trending down.

The contract is not a scam and the project is not aspirational vaporware; the product works and the disclosures are honest. Streamr passes at 65 out of 100, with the uncapped inflation vector and traction that is genuine but thin and declining as the cautions that keep it out of the higher tier.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the DATAv2 contract on Ethereum returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | Streamr, DATA, 18 decimals, verified |
| Total supply | 1,337,523,352 DATA, matches CoinGecko total and circulating (about 100 percent circulating, no locked vesting) |
| Mint authority | Present and uncapped; MINTER_ROLE mints at will, admin adds and removes minters |
| Pause authority | None; the paused call reverts |
| Upgradeable | No; both EIP 1967 proxy storage slots are zero, immutable bytecode |
| Ownership model | Not Ownable; AccessControl with DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE active and not renounced |
| Transfer fee | None |
| Standards | ERC 20, ERC 677 transferAndCall, ERC 2612 permit, burnable |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level DATAv2 is clean and well built: an immutable, non pausable, fee free ERC 20 with modern extras like ERC 677 and permit, and verified open source at `streamr-dev/DATAv2`. The residual contract risk is not centralization of transfers or an upgrade backdoor, since neither exists, but supply. Minting is possible through the minter role with the admin able to change the minter set and no ceiling enforced in code. That single fact, a real inflation vector under an active admin, is the primary reason the token sits in the medium tier despite otherwise transparent and battle tested mechanics. The exact holders of the admin and minter roles are not enumerable on chain; the documentation attributes them to a Streamr controlled multisig.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The decentralized real time data network"

> Site: Streamr presents itself as the decentralized real time data network, a serverless data streaming layer where Operators run nodes to secure and relay traffic.

**Reality: the DePIN is real and in production.** The Hub application, hosted separately on Polygon, is the live control plane where Operators and Sponsorships are created and managed, and the project has been shipping in this category since 2017. The Q1 2026 transparency report documents an active operator set, tens of millions of DATA locked in Operators and Sponsorships, and 399,792 DATA generated in network protocol fees over the quarter. This is a functioning network settling real economic activity in its own token, which is a materially different posture from a project whose flagship product returns an error page. The claim is supported by the evidence.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: "Operators earn yield on staked DATA, funded by Sponsorships"

> Site: Operators secure the network and earn yield on staked $DATA, Delegators share in that revenue, and Sponsors fund Sponsorships that distribute DATA to staked Operators.

**Reality: the staking economy works, but it is modest and softening.** Sponsorships are real smart contracts that pay DATA to staked Operators, and the Streamr application now generates dynamic short duration sponsorships as users stream, with an autostaker that lets Operators capture them. So DATA staking and sponsorships are genuine token utility, not decoration. The scale, however, is small and trending down: active staked operators fell from 159 at the start of Q1 2026 to 93 at the end, about a 41 percent drop, and DATA locked in Operators and Sponsorships fell from 93.4M to 74.3M, roughly 20 percent. The site's live nodes and APY figures hydrate from the project's own metrics API rather than being hardcoded, so the numbers shown are honest, but a security minded reader should note the usage is thin and declining.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: "DATAv2 is mintable, non upgradeable, non pausable and fee free"

> Site and docs: Streamr openly documents DATAv2 as role based mintable, non upgradeable, non pausable and free of transfer fees.

**Reality: every word matches the chain, and that transparency includes an uncapped mint.** MEFAI verified each property directly. The owner call reverts while the default admin role resolves, confirming AccessControl rather than simple ownership. Both proxy slots are empty, confirming a direct non proxy deployment. The paused call reverts, so there is no pause module, and a valid permit domain separator is present. The token also enforces no fee on transfer. The important nuance is that the same honest documentation confirms there is no fixed cap: any two billion max supply figure on market trackers is a convention, not a coded limit, and minting can push supply beyond its current level. The disclosure is exemplary; the underlying inflation risk is still real.

## 6. Claim vs Reality: "Audited"

> Site: Streamr links to independent smart contract audits of its token and network contracts.

**Reality: the audit claims are genuine.** The linked reports actually download from the official Streamr storage bucket, including the Isentropy DATAv2 token audit and Cyfrin network and staking contract reports. These are real third party reviews rather than fabricated badges, which is consistent with the honest posture of the rest of the site. The public frontend was independently assessed as clean: the only blockchain address in its code is the genuine DATA contract, appearing purely as the target of a normal Uniswap buy link, the page never asks visitors to connect a wallet or sign anything, and all scripts load from the project's own content network. The audit claim holds.

## 11. Conclusion

Streamr is a genuine, long running decentralized real time data network with a clean and immutable token, a working Hub application, and real DATA utility through operator staking and stream sponsorships, which keeps most of its risk profile low and clearly separates it from projects whose products do not run. As a project it scores 65 out of 100 and is classified Passed. Two cautions keep it out of the higher tier, and both are about supply and scale rather than deception. First, DATA is mintable with no on chain cap under an active admin role, a real inflation vector where the widely cited two billion max supply is a market convention and not a coded ceiling. Second, the measurable traction, while real and denominated in the token itself, is modest and declined through Q1 2026, with active operators down about 41 percent and locked DATA down about 20 percent. The honest framing of the project, disclosures that match the chain, live rather than faked metrics, and genuine audits, is credited throughout, and the result is a pass with a clearly stated supply caveat.

## 12. Recommendations

**For the Streamr team:**
- Cap DATA supply on chain, or place minting behind a timelock with published parameters, so the inflation vector is bounded in code rather than only in governance.
- Publish the current holders of the admin and minter roles so the multisig attribution can be verified on chain.
- Keep publishing live operator, sponsorship and locked supply metrics, and address the declining operator count directly in reporting.
- Keep the audit links current and clearly labeled with scope and date, since the reports are genuine and worth surfacing.

**For users:**
- Understand that DATA has no hard cap and can be minted under an active admin role, so the two billion figure is a convention rather than a guarantee.
- Recognize that the network and token utility are real but the scale is modest and was declining in Q1 2026; verify current operator counts, APY and staked totals on the Hub before committing.
- Judge any wallet signing behavior on the separate Hub application rather than on the landing page, which never requests a connection or signature.
