## Executive Summary

CARV markets itself as the largest modular data layer for gaming and AI, and now as the chain powering the rise of sovereign AI beings. The audit finds a genuine operating project underneath the branding, with the token contract and the core products both real, but with headline user metrics that inflate over time and a token whose deepest utility is still ahead of it.

1. **The token contract is clean and conservative.** CARV is a LayerZero omnichain fungible token with a fixed global maximum of one billion, the same address across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Arbitrum One. There is no public mint path, no proxy, no pause switch and no transfer fee, and the Ethereum owner is a Gnosis Safe multisig rather than a plain wallet.
2. **The core products are verifiably live.** CARV Play runs as a gaming super app, CARV ID is a working onchain identity built on the ERC 7231 standard, the verifier node program raised roughly 35 million dollars with node NFTs now tradable, and a live Protocol Explorer surfaces attestations. This is not vaporware.
3. **Self reported user counts are inflated and inconsistent.** Across CARV's own materials the figures move from about 2.5 million registered users and 900 thousand CARV ID holders to 15 million registered, 9.5 million Play users and more than 8 million CARV IDs, against roughly 2.1 million active wallets that are actually observable onchain. Much of this was driven by multi season airdrop quests, so the top line reads as farming vanity rather than durable adoption.
4. **Token utility is real but limited, and the newest layers are nascent.** CARV backs veCARV governance and node rewards today, but its intended roles as SVM chain gas, data payment currency and agent token liquidity depend on a chain that is still testnet and a data economy that is still early.

The contract is sound and the project is real, which keeps this out of scam territory. The gap between the marketing metrics and the verifiable ones, and the distance between the AI data layer branding and what is actually shipped, is what holds it back. This lands CARV at 62 out of 100, Passed with a Medium overall risk.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the CARV contract on Ethereum via public RPC returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | CARV, 18 decimals, verified |
| Global maximum | 1,000,000,000 CARV, effectively fixed; no public mint path in the bytecode |
| Ethereum balance | About 262,382,849 CARV held on this chain at review time |
| Cross chain supply | About 557,550,269 on Base and about 172,755,323 on Arbitrum One; the three EVM chains sum to roughly 992.7 million, with the remainder on BNB Chain and Solana |
| Mint authority | No public mint function (selectors `0x40c10f19` and `0xa0712d68` absent); units appear on a chain only when an equal amount is burned elsewhere during bridging |
| Owner (Ethereum) | Gnosis Safe multisig `0xb73123882bcbf3399a115203ddb1d880f20214a9`; Arbitrum owner is a distinct delegate `0xdea3309572c85e47aecd2deb97e9ef102ffb786e` |
| Upgradeable | No, both EIP 1967 implementation and admin slots are zero |
| Pausable | No, `paused()` reverts |
| Transfer fee | None |
| LayerZero methods | `setPeer` and `enforcedOptions` present, confirming an OFT |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level CARV is clean. It is a hard capped, non upgradeable, non pausable ERC 20 OFT with no transfer fee, and supply is conserved globally so no chain can mint tokens out of thin air. The only meaningful privilege is the standard LayerZero owner ability to configure trusted peers and the delegate, which every OFT shares. Because that power sits behind a Gnosis Safe multisig rather than a single externally owned key, the residual risk is a well understood bridge trust surface rather than an open backdoor. MEFAI's separate review of carv.io found a clean first party single page application whose sole write transaction is a bounded ERC 20 approve to CARV's own bridge contract, with no unlimited approval, no Permit2 or `signTypedData` harvesting and no `setApprovalForAll`, so the front end is not a drainer. The concerns for this project therefore sit at the metrics and product claim level below, not in the token contract.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Powering the Rise of Sovereign AI Beings" and "the largest modular data layer for Gaming and AI"

> Site: The homepage now leads with "Powering the Rise of Sovereign AI Beings," while the documentation and marketing describe CARV as the largest modular data layer for gaming and AI, built on the D.A.T.A. framework, community verifier nodes and a custom CARV SVM chain.

**Reality: a real stack whose top layers are still nascent versus the branding.** The base is genuine. CARV Play, CARV ID, the verifier program and the Protocol Explorer are all live. The pieces the newest branding leans on hardest are the least mature. The CARV SVM chain, a Solana Virtual Machine based chain intended to carry the agentic and data economy narrative, is still testnet and evolving toward mainnet, and the actual modular data layer value loop, meaningful verification workload paired with real data payments, is early. The project has also cycled its story from a gaming data layer, to an AI chain, to sovereign AI beings, tracking each cycle's dominant narrative. The claim is not false, but the largest data layer and AI beings framing describes an ambition and a roadmap more than a running economy.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: "over 8 million CARV IDs and 60,000 plus verifier nodes"

> Site: CARV promotes large adoption figures, including more than 8 million CARV IDs, more than 60,000 verifier nodes, and elsewhere headline counts of up to 15 million registered users and 9.5 million Play users, alongside claims of hundreds of gaming and enterprise integrations.

**Reality: inflated and inconsistent metrics that read as airdrop farming vanity.** The promoted counts are not reconciled and grow across CARV's own materials over time, moving from about 2.5 million registered users and 900 thousand CARV ID holders to 15 million registered, 9.5 million Play users and more than 8 million CARV IDs. Against that, the figures that are actually checkable onchain are far smaller, on the order of 2.1 million unique active wallets, and the ERC 7231 CARV ID and the verifier NFTs are the parts that hold up. Season 1 through Season 3 airdrops and quests drove much of the registration, and the repeated delay of the Season 3 airdrop into May 2026 keeps farmers engaged, so the wide gap between registered users, onchain identities and active wallets is best read as farming inflation rather than durable adoption. Selecting the largest available number and presenting it as user base is a transparency concern even for an otherwise real project.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: CARV Token Utility

> Site: CARV documentation describes the token as a gas token used to pay the network computing costs of nodes on the chain, as CARV Protocol governance through veCARV at a one to one staking ratio, as the pairing asset for AI agent token liquidity pools, and as the incentive that bootstraps the modular data layer for data owners, providers and consumers.

**Reality: real but limited utility, with the biggest roles still aspirational.** Today the concrete uses are governance and rewards. Node rewards are distributed in veCARV, and holders can stake and lock CARV into veCARV for voting and delegation. A staking boost for node operators is promised but not yet live. The larger roles the documentation advertises, gas on the CARV SVM chain, data payment currency and agent token liquidity pairing, all depend on the still nascent chain and the still early data economy, so much of the described utility is forward looking rather than in use. Market context underlines the point: CARV trades around 0.027 dollars for roughly an 18 million dollar market cap, down about 98 percent from its November 2024 high. The token is transferable and governance bearing, but its deep economic utility is a future the chain has not yet delivered.

---

## 6. Claim vs Reality: D.A.T.A. Framework and Partnerships

> Site: CARV presents the D.A.T.A. framework (Data Authentication, Trust and Attestation) as an industry first way to feed verified onchain and offchain data to AI agents, and cites relationships with large names such as EA, Netmarble and AliCloud as proof of reach.

**Reality: an open flagship that has gone quiet, and partnerships of unconfirmed depth.** The D.A.T.A. framework is real code and was open sourced, but the public repository has been largely dormant since March 2025 and carries only about 31 stars, which is thin traction for something marketed as an industry first. The marquee partnerships with EA, Netmarble and AliCloud are cited without independent confirmation of their depth or of shipped, production integrations, so they should be read as reach claims until corroborated. None of this is fabricated, but the distance between the industry first framing and the actual repository activity, and between named partners and verifiable integration, is a recurring delivery and transparency gap.

## 7. Positive Findings (Credited)

- The token contract is a clean, hard capped, non upgradeable and non pausable ERC 20 OFT with no transfer fee and no public mint path, so global supply is conserved.
- Privileged control sits behind a Gnosis Safe multisig rather than a single externally owned key, and the only material power is the routine LayerZero peer and delegate configuration.
- The core products are verifiably live: CARV Play as a gaming super app, CARV ID as an onchain identity on the ERC 7231 standard with a public SDK, verifier nodes backed by a roughly 35 million dollar node sale with tradable node NFTs, and a live Protocol Explorer that surfaces attestations.
- The carv.io front end is a clean first party application; its only write transaction is a bounded bridge approve, with no unlimited approval or signature harvesting, so it is not a drainer.

## 11. Conclusion

CARV is a real, operating project with a clean token, which keeps its contract level risk low. As a project it scores 62 out of 100 and is Passed with a Medium overall risk. The token is a hard capped, non upgradeable, non pausable omnichain OFT with no public mint path, the front end is clean, and the core products, CARV Play, CARV ID, verifier nodes and the Protocol Explorer, are verifiably live rather than vaporware. What holds the score in the middle is the gap between marketing and reality: self reported user counts that inflate and disagree across CARV's own materials and read as airdrop farming vanity, a token whose deepest utility still depends on a testnet chain and an early data economy, and a data layer and sovereign AI beings narrative that runs ahead of what is shipped. The caution here is not a contract exploit or a broken product, it is a genuine project whose metrics and branding should be read with discipline.

---

## 12. Recommendations

**For the CARV team:**
- Publish one reconciled, methodology backed set of adoption metrics and stop citing divergent registered user, Play user and CARV ID counts across materials.
- Separate onchain, verifiable figures (CARV IDs minted, active wallets, node NFTs) from quest and registration totals so readers can see durable adoption apart from airdrop farming.
- Move the advertised token roles from roadmap to reality: ship the SVM chain gas usage, the node staking boost and the data payment loop, or label them clearly as forthcoming.
- Revive or clearly status the D.A.T.A. repository, and label partnerships as intent versus shipped, production integration.

**For users:**
- Treat the largest data layer and sovereign AI beings framing as forward looking; the base products are live but the chain and data economy are still nascent.
- Discount the headline user counts and rely on onchain observable figures, since much of the registration was airdrop and quest driven.
- Understand that CARV utility today is mainly veCARV governance and node rewards, that a multisig can configure LayerZero peers, and verify any claimed metric or partnership independently before relying on it.
