## Executive Summary

CARV is a real and correctly identified token. Live public RPC calls on Ethereum confirm name CARV, symbol CARV, 18 decimals, and a standard ERC20 that is actually a LayerZero omnichain fungible token deployed at the same address on Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Arbitrum One. The global maximum is one billion tokens and this is effectively fixed because the bytecode exposes no public mint function; new units appear on a chain only when an equal amount is burned on another chain during bridging. On Ethereum the contract holds about 262 million tokens right now, the three main EVM chains together hold roughly 993 million, and the owner key is a Gnosis Safe multisig rather than a plain wallet. Marketing claims about a modular data layer, the D.A.T.A. framework and verifier nodes describe offchain and separate onchain systems, not properties of this simple token contract.

## 1. Project and Product Reality

The core products are genuinely live and usable, not vaporware. CARV Play runs at play.carv.io as an AI powered gaming super app with hundreds of games and soulbound achievement credentials, plus a published mobile app, and CARV ID is a working onchain identity built on the ERC 7231 standard with a public SDK on GitHub. Verifier nodes are real: a node sale raised roughly 35 million dollars, node NFTs became transferable and tradable on OpenSea in October 2025, verifier and contracts code sits in active public repos, and a live Protocol Explorer at explorer.carv.io surfaces attestations. The weaker parts are the newer headline layers: the SVM Chain is testnet and evolving toward an agentic mainnet rather than a mature high throughput chain, and the actual modular data layer value loop (meaningful verification workload plus data payments) is early, so the whole stack reads as partial rather than fully realized.

**Project level flags:** inconsistent and inflating self reported user counts (2.5M to 15M registered, 900k to 8M CARV IDs); airdrop farming vanity metrics with Season 3 airdrop repeatedly delayed to May 2026; wide gap between registered users, onchain IDs, and roughly 2.1M active wallets; big name partnerships (EA, Netmarble, AliCloud) cited without independent confirmation of depth; flagship D.A.T.A. open source repo dormant since March 2025 with only about 31 stars despite industry first marketing; repeated narrative pivots from gaming data layer to AI chain to AI Beings chasing trends; token down about 98 percent from all time high

## 2. Website and Frontend Integrity

VERDICT: CLEAN | CONFIDENCE: high
The carv.io homepage is a standard Vite built React single page app that loads only first party asset chunks plus Google Fonts and a jsDelivr bundle pinned to CARV's own GitHub repo (carv protocol/cdn@1.0.3), with no eval, atob, base64 obfuscation, or untrusted remote drainer hosts. The only EVM contract address embedded in the code config is the correct official OFT token 0xc08Cd26474722cE93F4D0c34D16201461c10AA8C, used for the mainnet oft_arb_contract and oft_bsc_contract entries, while the remaining addresses are CARV's own bridge contracts and testnet or UAT variants gated behind a mainnet flag, so no lookalike or substituted address was found. Wallet connect is present and multichain (RainbowKit and wagmi for EVM plus Solana adapters, WalletConnect, and a Binance connector), and the sole write transaction observed is a bounded ERC20 approve whose spender is CARV's own bridge contract for the exact bridging amount after an allowance check, with no unlimited MaxUint256 approval, no Permit2 or signTypedData harvesting, and no setApprovalForAll, so it is not a drainer. Metrics and data come from live first party APIs (protocol.carv.io, public.carv.io, interface.carv.io) alongside genuine chain RPCs and the LayerZero API rather than hardcoded fakes. No audit badge or audit claim appears on the reviewed surface, so there is nothing unbacked to flag.

## 3. Traction and Claims

Some figures are hard and independently checkable while the marquee user numbers are not. The 35 million dollar node raise, the ERC 7231 CARV ID NFTs, and roughly 2.1 million unique active wallets are grounded and onchain observable. The promoted figures are not reconciled and inflate over time across CARV's own materials, moving from 2.5 million registered users and 900k CARV ID holders to 15 million registered users, 9.5 million Play users, and 8 million plus CARV IDs, with 60k plus verifier nodes and claims of 780 to 1000 gaming and enterprise integrations. Season 1 to 3 airdrops and quests drove much of the registration, and the delay of the Season 3 airdrop to May 2026 keeps farmers engaged, so the top line counts carry meaningful vanity and farming inflation.

## 4. Token Utility and Economics

CARV has real but limited utility rather than pure speculation. Node rewards are distributed in veCARV, holders can stake and lock CARV into veCARV for governance voting and delegation, and a staking boost for node operators is promised but not yet live. Its intended roles as SVM Chain gas token, liquidity pairing for agent tokens, and data payment currency depend on the still nascent chain and data economy, so much of the utility is aspirational. Market context is weak: about 0.027 dollars, roughly 18 million dollar market cap, and down about 98 percent from the November 2024 high.

The public site now leads with the tagline Powering the Rise of Sovereign AI Beings, while the documentation and third party research describe CARV as the largest modular data layer for gaming and AI. The headline product pieces are the D.A.T.A. framework for feeding verified data to AI agents, a network of community verifier nodes, and a custom CARV SVM chain based on the Solana Virtual Machine. These are ambitious platform claims about offchain services and a separate chain rather than features of the token itself. They are consistent with an operating project that concluded its token generation event in October 2024, but they cannot be confirmed from the ERC20 contract and should be treated as product marketing.

## 5. Claim versus Reality

- "The largest modular data layer for Gaming and AI, over 8 million CARV IDs and 60,000 plus verifier nodes" / Reality: an offchain adoption and infrastructure claim; not something the token contract can prove. The token itself is a plain omnichain OFT and carries none of this logic.
- "D.A.T.A. framework (Data Authentication, Trust and Attestation)" / Reality: a software plugin for web3 agent frameworks that feeds AI agents onchain and offchain data. It lives in CARV product code and the CARV SVM chain, not in this ERC20.
- "Verifier nodes validate ZK proofs and TEE attestations to secure the network" / Reality: verifier participation is gated by separate NFT license contracts and the CARV SVM chain, with CARV used as gas and staked to veCARV. The verifier system is real but distinct from this token contract.
- "CARV token utility: gas on CARV SVM, governance via veCARV at one to one, liquidity pairing" / Reality: consistent with a standard transferable ERC20 OFT; veCARV staking and governance are handled by external contracts.

## 6. Contract Security Check (supporting)

- Global maximum one billion CARV, effectively fixed; Ethereum totalSupply about 262,382,849, Base about 557,550,269, Arbitrum about 172,755,323, summing to about 992.7 million across three EVM chains with the remainder on BNB Chain and Solana. Per chain supply floats with bridging.
- Owner on Ethereum is 0xb73123882bcbf3399a115203ddb1d880f20214a9, and its onchain code matches a Gnosis Safe multisig proxy. Arbitrum owner is a different key 0xdea3309572c85e47aecd2deb97e9ef102ffb786e, consistent with per chain LayerZero delegates.
- No public mint function in the bytecode (mint selectors 0x40c10f19 and 0xa0712d68 absent). Minting occurs only internally on receipt of a valid cross chain message from a trusted peer. setPeer and enforcedOptions selectors present, confirming a LayerZero OFT.
- Not upgradeable: both EIP1967 implementation and admin storage slots are zero, so no proxy. Not pausable: paused() reverts. No fee on transfer and standard 18 decimals. Owner can configure LayerZero peers and delegate, the sole meaningful privilege.

Live reads on Ethereum via public RPC return name CARV, symbol CARV and 18 decimals, with a current onchain balance of about 262 million tokens on this chain. The bytecode carries LayerZero OFT methods including setPeer and enforcedOptions and exposes no public mint entry point, so supply is conserved globally and only moves between chains by burn and mint during bridging. Neither EIP1967 proxy slot is set, paused reverts, and there is no fee logic, confirming a non upgradeable, non pausable, fee free token. Ownership on Ethereum resolves to a Gnosis Safe multisig, whose only material power is configuring LayerZero peers and the delegate. Summing the three main EVM chains gives about 992.7 million tokens, in line with the stated one billion maximum once BNB Chain and Solana balances are included.
