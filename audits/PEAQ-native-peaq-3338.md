## Executive Summary

peaq (PEAQ) is a real, functioning Layer 1 for DePIN and the machine economy, with credible venture backing and genuine partners. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects a heavy unlock, thin fee, self reported reality behind the machine economy branding:

1. **The chain and backing are real:** MEFAI confirms a live Polkadot SDK Layer 1, EVM compatible at chain id 3338, with the PEAQ native token and credible venture backing.
2. **But the token is inflationary and heavily locked.** Supply is inflationary via staking emissions, insiders hold roughly 42 percent, and more than half of supply is still locked, unlocking on schedules that run to 2035.
3. **The machine counts are self reported and soft.** Marketed millions of machines largely reflect consumer app installs and wallets counted as machines rather than sustained on chain machine activity.
4. **Real fee revenue is thin** relative to the valuation, and usage is heavily campaign and incentive driven.

The chain and backing are real; the caution is a heavy unlock overhang to 2035, inflation, self reported machine metrics, thin fees and a near total drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The Layer 1 for DePIN and the Machine Economy"

> Site: the Layer 1 for DePIN and the Economy of Things, citing millions of machines and many DePIN projects building on peaq.

**Reality: real chain, soft machine metrics, thin fees.** peaq is a genuinely live Polkadot SDK Layer 1 with EVM compatibility, credible venture backing and real named DePIN partners, which distinguishes it from typical projects. But the marketed machine counts are soft, self reported figures: much of the reported growth comes from a handful of consumer browser and mobile apps whose installs and wallets are counted as machines rather than autonomous physical infrastructure with sustained on chain activity. Real protocol fee revenue is thin (a small figure per quarter) relative to the valuation, and usage has been heavily driven by incentive campaigns, contracting sharply once those campaigns paused.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Decentralization and Value

- **Heavy unlock overhang:** roughly 42 percent to insiders with more than half of supply still locked, vesting on cliffs to 2035, a persistent structural sell side overhang.
- **Inflationary supply:** staking emissions grow supply toward a modeled long term figure rather than a hard cap.
- **Limited validator decentralization:** block production is a small active collator set, though finality is inherited from the Polkadot relay chain (a genuine security strength), and the split of independent versus foundation affiliated collators is not clearly disclosed.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 98 percent from its late 2024 peak, near all time lows.

## 8. Conclusion

peaq (PEAQ) is a real, functioning Polkadot SDK Layer 1 for DePIN and the machine economy, EVM compatible at chain id 3338, with credible venture backing and genuine partners, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 56/100. It is held back because supply is inflationary with roughly 42 percent held by insiders and more than half of supply still locked vesting to 2035, because the marketed machine counts are soft, self reported figures (app installs and wallets counted as machines), because real fee revenue is thin and campaign driven, and because it is down roughly 98 percent from its late 2024 peak. The chain and backing are real; the caution is the heavy unlock overhang, inflation, self reported metrics and the drawdown.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the peaq team:**
- Present the insider allocation and the unlock schedule to 2035 prominently, and report inflation clearly against the modeled long term supply.
- Distinguish autonomous machine activity from consumer app installs and wallets in the machine counts.
- Disclose the independent versus foundation affiliated collator split, and report real fees separately from incentive campaigns.

**For users:**
- Understand supply is inflationary with a heavy insider unlock overhang running to 2035, and that machine counts are soft and self reported.
- Value the real chain and backing on their merits, and model the overhang, the inflation and the drawdown.
