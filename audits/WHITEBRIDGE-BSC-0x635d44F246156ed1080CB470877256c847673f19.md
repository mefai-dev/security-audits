## Executive Summary

WhiteBridge AI is unusual: the **contract is clean** (ownership renounced, no mint function, zero tax, not a honeypot) and the underlying product (a paid people-and-data search tool) is real. The MEDIUM-to-HIGH rating is driven by market structure and marketing, not contract mechanics:

1. **On-chain DEX liquidity is effectively negligible** (roughly 1,758 dollars total, confirmed two ways by MEFAI). Real trading volume is on centralized exchanges, so there is essentially no on-chain exit path and any on-chain sell of size would face catastrophic slippage.
2. The token has fallen far from launch, and the large majority of supply is non-circulating / project-held.
3. The "decentralized on-chain AI / node network" framing is aspirational: the AI runs **off-chain and closed-source**, and the public code is a single unrelated fork.

The contract will not rug you at the code level; the risk is that you may not be able to exit on-chain and the "decentralized AI" narrative is unsupported.

---

## 3. Critical Finding: Negligible On-chain DEX Liquidity

MEFAI verified, by two independent methods (a liquidity read of the pools plus a direct on-chain balance read of the main pool contract), that total on-chain DEX liquidity is approximately **1,758 dollars** across three small pools. These are concentrated-liquidity pools with no fungible LP token to lock or burn.

Reported daily volume (a low-six-figure figure) is **almost entirely on second-tier centralized exchanges**. The practical consequence: there is essentially **no on-chain exit path**. Any attempt to sell a meaningful amount on-chain would move the price catastrophically. This is the single most important risk for an on-chain trader and is why the rating sits at MEDIUM-to-HIGH despite the clean contract.

## 5. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized On-chain AI / Node Network"

> Marketing: "sophisticated AI agents ... querying a decentralized network of global data providers".

**Reality:** the underlying product is a real, paid, **off-chain** people-search tool (with no crypto dependency in the product itself). The AI agents run off-chain over third-party data APIs and an **undisclosed commercial model**, and they are **closed-source**. The project's public code organization has a single public repository, a fork with no original AI or node code. There is no live on-chain node economy. The "decentralized on-chain AI" framing is aspirational, not delivered.

---

## 6. Claim vs Reality: External Score and Incubation

The project advertises an external monitoring score and an incubation relationship with a Web3 AI incubator. The incubation relationship is genuine. The external monitoring score is an ongoing reputation figure, **not a full published code audit**; it should not be read as an assurance of the token's market safety, which (per Section 3) is poor on-chain. Self-reported business metrics (revenue, client counts, record counts) are UNVERIFIED and drift across sources.

## 10. Conclusion

WhiteBridge AI has a genuinely clean contract and a real underlying product, which is why it is not in the CRITICAL band. But for an on-chain trader the token is close to unswappable: on-chain DEX liquidity is roughly 1,758 dollars, so there is effectively no on-chain exit, and the "decentralized on-chain AI" narrative is unsupported by the off-chain, closed-source reality. A score of 50/100 (MEDIUM-to-HIGH) balances the clean code against the severe on-chain liquidity and marketing risks.

---

## 11. Recommendations

**For the WhiteBridge team:**
- Provide meaningful, lockable on-chain liquidity, or clearly state that the token is CEX-traded and on-chain exit is not viable.
- Stop describing the AI as "decentralized" and "on-chain" while it is off-chain and closed-source; publish the node/agent code or drop the claim.
- Publish audited, sourced business metrics rather than self-reported figures.

**For users:**
- Do not attempt to exit size on-chain: liquidity is ~1,758 dollars.
- The contract is clean, but treat this as a CEX-liquidity token with an aspirational on-chain narrative.
