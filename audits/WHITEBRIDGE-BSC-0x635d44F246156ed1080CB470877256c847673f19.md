## Summary

The contract is clean and the brand, incubator and CertiK score are real and verifiable. The main concern is market structure and marketing spin: onchain DEX liquidity is negligible (real trading volume is on centralized exchanges), the token is far below launch, most supply is project held, and the AI agents run offchain and closed source.

## Website claim versus reality
- "sophisticated AI agents to conduct extensive people and data research" / Real product, overstated decentralization. whitebridge.ai is a genuine paid people search SaaS with no mention of crypto. The AI is a real offchain product, but the decentralized node network and onchain data service contracts are aspirational, with no live onchain node economy.
- "incubated by ChainGPT" / TRUE. Confirmed by ChainGPT Labs own blog and the ChainGPT Pad IDO.
- "CertiK Skynet score about 90" / TRUE with nuance. Skynet reports 90.39, AA, rank number 2. Skynet is ongoing monitoring plus KYC, not a published full code audit.
- "querying a decentralized network of global data providers" as onchain AI / Reality is offchain and closed source. The GitHub org has one public repo, a fork, with no original AI or node code and no recent commits. The agents run offchain over third party data broker APIs and an undisclosed commercial LLM.
- Business metrics (4.7 million dollars ARR, 60,000 clients, 3.59 billion records) / UNVERIFIED. Self reported and drifting across sources.

### Onchain contract audit
- Ownership RENOUNCED (owner equals zero address). No mint function (is_mintable 0, fixed 1 billion supply). Buy tax 0, sell tax 0. Not a honeypot (GoPlus is_honeypot 0, sells clear onchain). is_open_source 1.
- CRITICAL market risk: onchain DEX liquidity about 1,758 dollars total (verified two ways: DexScreener API and direct RPC balanceOf). These are concentrated liquidity pools with no LP token to lock or burn. Reported daily volume is almost entirely on second tier centralized exchanges, leaving essentially no onchain exit path.
- About 62 percent of supply is non circulating and project held. Down about 78 percent from a 0.008 dollar launch (100 percent unlocked at listing) and about 98 percent from the all time high. Exact top 10 holders UNVERIFIED (explorer gated).
