## Executive Summary

Oraichain (ORAI) is a real, long running Cosmos Layer 1 with genuine tooling, but the token has a material supply reporting discrepancy across chains and marketing that outruns thin on chain usage. It is not a scam. The rating is driven by that transparency gap and the usage reality, not a malicious contract:

1. **The contract is legitimate but the Ethereum figure is misleading.** The Ethereum representation still reports roughly 86 million, but the authoritative canonical supply on the native Oraichain is only about 19.78 million maximum (roughly 19.4 million circulating), because a large burn and a native chain launch superseded the old Ethereum total. Using the Ethereum figure overstates market capitalization several times over.
2. **The chain and tooling are real,** a five year old Cosmos Layer 1 with real deployed products.
3. **But the AI Layer 1 framing outruns reality.** On chain usage is thin, and the flagship verifiable AI inference claim is largely aspirational, with newer products relying on external, off chain models.
4. **Decentralization is foundation influenced,** with a small validator set and an active foundation delegation program.

The chain and tooling are real; the caution, and the HIGH severity transparency finding, is the cross chain supply discrepancy, thin usage versus the AI framing, foundation influence and a near total drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The AI Layer 1"

> Site: the AI Layer 1, with AI powered oracles, an AI and model marketplace, verifiable AI inference, and newer AI agent and Bitcoin DeFi initiatives.

**Reality: real chain, thin AI usage, aspirational verifiable inference.** Oraichain is a genuine, independent Cosmos Layer 1 with IBC, real deployed products and a five year track record, which distinguishes it from typical projects. But the AI Layer 1 branding outruns measurable on chain usage: on chain economic activity and liquidity are small, there is little evidence of large scale third party contracts consuming AI oracle feeds in production, and the flagship verifiable AI inference claim is largely aspirational, with the project's own newer products relying on external, off chain models rather than trustless on chain inference. The direction has also pivoted repeatedly (from oracles to an AI marketplace to AI agents to Bitcoin DeFi), a focus and execution consideration.

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Decentralization and Value

- **Cross chain supply discrepancy:** the authoritative native supply is about 19.78 million, while the Ethereum representation still reports roughly 86 million, so market capitalization computed from the Ethereum figure is several times too high, a real holder confusion risk.
- **Nearly fully diluted (positive):** market capitalization is close to fully diluted valuation on the native figure, so there is no large unlock overhang.
- **Foundation influenced decentralization:** a small validator set with an active foundation delegation program means meaningful foundation influence over the validator set.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99.7 percent from its 2021 peak, a micro capitalization with minimal liquidity.

## 8. Conclusion

Oraichain (ORAI) is a real, long running Cosmos Layer 1 with genuine tooling and a nearly fully diluted supply, which keeps it just above the passing line at 52/100. It is held back most by a cross chain supply discrepancy (the Ethereum representation reports roughly 86 million while the authoritative native supply is about 19.78 million, so market capitalization from the Ethereum figure is several times too high), by thin on chain usage against an AI Layer 1 framing whose verifiable inference claim is largely aspirational, by foundation influenced decentralization with a small validator set, and by a roughly 99.7 percent drawdown. The chain and tooling are real; the caution is the supply transparency gap, thin usage, foundation influence and the drawdown.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Oraichain team:**
- Reconcile or clearly annotate the Ethereum representation so holders do not compute market capitalization from the legacy roughly 86 million figure; state the authoritative native supply prominently.
- Report real AI oracle usage and revenue on chain, and describe the verifiable inference roadmap honestly where it relies on external models.
- Publish validator decentralization and reduce foundation delegation influence over time.

**For users:**
- Use the authoritative native supply of about 19.78 million, never the Ethereum representation's roughly 86 million, when assessing market capitalization.
- Value the real chain and tooling on their merits, and model the thin usage, the foundation influence and the drawdown.
