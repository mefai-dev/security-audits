## Executive Summary

WeatherXM is a weather station DePIN that rewards hardware owners in WXM for contributing weather data and licenses that data to businesses. Unlike many projects in this category, most of the story checks out. The token contract is one of the cleaner designs MEFAI has reviewed, the reward system is genuinely settled on chain, and the physical network of weather stations is real and growing. The single meaningful transparency issue sits in the marketing layer, not the product.

1. **The token contract is clean and immutable.** MEFAI's direct read of the Ethereum L1 contract found a hard capped, non upgradeable, ownerless ERC 20 with no mint function, no pause switch, and no transfer fee. Total supply equals the cap at exactly 100,000,000 WXM. There is no privileged key that can inflate, freeze, or tax holders.

2. **The DePIN network and its rewards are real and verifiable.** WeatherXM operates a live public Explorer and rewards station owners daily through an on chain RewardPool contract. A daily reward algorithm scores each station against Proof of Location and Data Quality thresholds, builds a Merkle tree, and submits the root hash to the RewardPool from which owners withdraw WXM at will. This is functioning on chain economic activity, not a promise.

3. **WXM has genuine, live utility.** The token is described as WeatherXM Network's governance and data licensing token, and both uses are operational: station owners earn WXM for data, and businesses purchase weather data through the WeatherXM Pro B2B API. The token is the medium of its own product, which is the exact test Vanar failed.

4. **The homepage traction counters are marketing, not data.** MEFAI's frontend review confirmed that the headline station and user counters are hardcoded animated values that disagree with one another and with the body copy. The homepage simultaneously advertises 9500+ stations and over 7,000 stations, the Pro page cites 8,500+, and the animated STATIONS MANUFACTURED and STATIONS DEPLOYED counters render as 0. These figures should be read as promotional and verified against the live Explorer.

The contract is safe, the network is real, and the token is actually used. The only caution is presentational: inconsistent, hardcoded headline numbers. This lands WeatherXM at 79 out of 100, Passed.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the WXM contract on Ethereum L1 returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | WeatherXM, WXM, 18 decimals, verified |
| Max supply | 100,000,000, matches the onchain hard cap and total supply |
| Mint authority | None. No mint selector and no minter role exist in the runtime bytecode |
| Owner / admin | None. The owner call reverts; no Ownable and no AccessControl roles |
| Pause authority | None. The contract is not pausable |
| Upgradeable | No. There is no proxy implementation slot |
| Transfer fee | None. No fee on transfer and no blacklist |
| Standard | OpenZeppelin ERC20Capped plus ERC20Burnable (burn and burnFrom present) |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level WXM is close to a best case design for this category. Every token was minted at deployment, the supply is hard capped, and there is no privileged function to change supply, fees, or transferability. Because there is no owner, no minter, and no proxy, the usual centralization concerns do not apply. Holders retain burn and burnFrom, so the supply can only stay flat or fall. The reward emission the project advertises is a release of preminted tokens from a fixed allocation, not inflation, which the absence of any mint path confirms. The residual risk is not in the contract at all; it is ordinary secondary market liquidity risk, since circulating supply is thin and concentrated relative to the cap.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "9500+ live weather stations" and a real DePIN

> Site: WeatherXM presents itself as a community powered weather network with thousands of live physical weather stations deployed globally, an interactive Explorer, and a B2B data product.

**Reality: the network and hardware are genuinely real.** WeatherXM manufactures and ships physical weather stations, offers free shipping to 25 countries, and runs a live public Explorer that maps active stations worldwide. Independent DePIN trackers list the project, and the reward architecture requires each station to pass Proof of Location and Data Quality checks before it can earn, which only functioning hardware reporting real readings can satisfy. This is a delivered, operating DePIN rather than a concept. The fair conclusion is that the physical network is one of the stronger parts of the project, and MEFAI credits it as such. The caveat is only about the specific headline numbers, addressed in the next section.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: hardcoded and inconsistent headline counters

> Site: The homepage displays headline counters for the number of weather stations and community members, animated to convey live network scale.

**Reality: the headline counters are hardcoded marketing values that contradict each other.** MEFAI's frontend review found that the station and user counters on the homepage are hardcoded animated figures, not a live feed. The page advertises 9500+ live weather stations in one place and over 7,000 weather stations in another, the WeatherXM Pro page cites 8,500+, and the animated STATIONS MANUFACTURED and STATIONS DEPLOYED counters render as 0. Three different station totals on the same property, plus a headline counter stuck at zero, mean the numbers cannot all be true and none should be taken at face value. The underlying network is real, so this is not fabrication of the product, but presenting inconsistent hardcoded values as live traction is a transparency weakness. Readers should rely on the live Explorer and the project's Dune Analytics dashboard for actual station and reward figures.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: WXM utility, station rewards, and data licensing

> Site: WXM is described as WeatherXM Network's governance and data licensing token; station owners earn WXM for their data and businesses buy weather data with WXM.

**Reality: the token is actually used, and its rewards settle on chain.** This is where WeatherXM clearly separates from speculative peers. Every day the reward algorithm scores all stations, bundles the results with owner wallet addresses into a Merkle tree, and submits the root hash to the RewardPool smart contract, from which owners withdraw WXM directly and without time limits. A companion RewardVault funds the daily emissions and a BusinessDevelopmentPool handles boost allocations. On the demand side, the WeatherXM Pro B2B API sells hyperlocal weather data to developers, researchers, and businesses, and WXM is positioned as the data licensing token for that access. A further 3,000,000 WXM was reserved to reward stations that participated before token launch, distributed over 425 days. The token is therefore the working medium of both sides of the network, earning on the supply side and licensing on the demand side, which is the utility test many projects claim and few pass.

## 6. Claim vs Reality: canonical chain and the Arbitrum presentation

> Site and docs: WXM is commonly presented and traded through its Arbitrum address, while the canonical token lives on Ethereum L1.

**Reality: a minor presentational nuance, fully reconciled on chain.** The tradeable venue most users encounter is the Arbitrum bridged copy, and the docs foreground it, while the canonical supply and all contract controls sit on the Ethereum L1 token at `0xde654f497A563dd7A121c176a125dD2F11F13a83`. MEFAI confirmed the relationship directly: the Arbitrum token's l1Address resolves back to the L1 contract, so the two are the same asset and there is no duplicate or conflicting supply. This is a labeling nuance rather than a risk, and it is noted only for completeness. The MEFAI frontend review separately confirmed the marketing site is a clean WordPress and WooCommerce build with no wallet drainer, no address swap, and no clipboard hijack; the single onchain address on the homepage is the genuine bridged WXM token inside a real Uniswap link, and the Solana mint appears only in a real Orca pool link.

## 11. Conclusion

WeatherXM earns a score of 79 out of 100 and a Passed verdict. The token contract is among the cleaner designs in the DePIN category: hard capped at 100,000,000 WXM, fully minted at deployment, ownerless, non upgradeable, non pausable, and free of transfer fees, so no privileged key can inflate, freeze, or tax holders. The network behind it is genuinely real, with physical weather stations, a live Explorer, and a reward system that settles on chain every day through a Merkle root submitted to the RewardPool after Proof of Location and Data Quality checks. WXM is the working medium of both sides of that network, earned by station owners and used to license data through the WeatherXM Pro API. The one blemish is presentational rather than structural: the homepage headline counters are hardcoded animated values that contradict each other and the body copy, so those specific numbers should be treated as marketing and verified against the live Explorer. The caution here is a marketing transparency note on a fundamentally sound project, not a contract or product risk.

## 12. Recommendations

**For the WeatherXM team:**
- Replace the hardcoded homepage station and user counters with values pulled from the live Explorer or the on chain reward data, and reconcile the conflicting 9500+, over 7,000, and 8,500+ figures to a single sourced number.
- Fix or remove the animated STATIONS MANUFACTURED and STATIONS DEPLOYED counters that render as 0.
- State clearly on the token and docs pages that the Ethereum L1 contract is canonical and the Arbitrum address is a bridged copy, so buyers verify the right asset.

**For users:**
- Trust the live Explorer and the project's Dune Analytics dashboard for real station and reward figures, not the animated homepage counters.
- Note that the token contract itself is clean, immutable, and ownerless, so the main residual risk is thin secondary market liquidity, not the contract.
- Verify you are transacting the intended token: the L1 canonical contract is `0xde654f497A563dd7A121c176a125dD2F11F13a83`, and the Arbitrum copy reconciles to it via l1Address.
