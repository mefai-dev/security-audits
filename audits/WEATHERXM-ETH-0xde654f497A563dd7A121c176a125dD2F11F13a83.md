## Why This Report Exists

WeatherXM markets itself as a decentralized weather station DePIN that pays station owners in WXM for contributing real weather data, gated by a proof of data quality and a location integrity mechanism. Buyers and station operators need to know three things that marketing rarely separates cleanly. First, whether the quality and location mechanisms are real code or slogans. Second, whether reward computation happens trustlessly on chain or is decided by a central server that merely posts a result. Third, whether the network traction is genuine or inflated. This report answers each by reading the actual public Solidity and Python and by querying live chain state and the project API, then labeling every flagship claim against the evidence.

## Method

1. Queried the Ethereum mainnet token and the Arbitrum One token over public RPC for name, symbol, decimals, total supply, cap, owner, and a bytecode selector scan to prove which privileged functions exist.
2. Downloaded and read the real source of `weatherxm-network/smart-contracts` (`develop` branch) and `weatherxm-network/qod` (`main`), plus the `WeatherXM/docs` source, from GitHub tarballs rather than trusting rendered pages.
3. Traced the full reward lifecycle across `WeatherXM.sol`, `RewardsVault.sol`, and `RewardPool.sol`, and cross checked the on chain emission constant against the documented 52M over 10 years schedule.
4. Confirmed the data quality algorithm exists and produces the score that controls rewards by reading `obc_sqc_driver.py` and `initial_params.py`.
5. Pulled live network statistics from the project public API endpoint `api.weatherxm.com/api/v1/network/stats`.

All file and line references below point to the repositories named above.

## The Foundation: how a reward actually reaches a station

The lifecycle in code is precise and worth stating plainly, because it is the crux of every claim.

1. WeatherXM is a fixed supply ERC20. The constructor mints the entire 100M to the deployer at launch and there is no other mint path. See `smart-contracts/src/0.8.20/WeatherXM.sol:11` (`maxSupply = 1e8 * 10 ** 18`) and `WeatherXM.sol:18-19` (constructor calls `_mint(_msgSender(), maxSupply)`), with `_mint` overridden as internal only at `WeatherXM.sol:31-33`.

2. A pre funded `RewardsVault` releases a hard capped, flat daily emission. `RewardsVault.sol:25` sets `maxDailyEmission = 14246 * 10 ** 18` and `RewardsVault.sol:26` sets `rewardDistributionPeriod = 1440 minutes`. The function `pullDailyEmissions()` at `RewardsVault.sol:78-84` sends at most 14,246 WXM per day to the reward distributor and reverts if called again inside 24 hours. This is the on chain emission schedule, and it is a flat linear drip with no halving.

3. An off chain server, referred to in the docs as the Reward Mechanism, computes every station reward for the day using Quality of Data, Proof of Location, Hardware Class weighting, and Cell Capacity limits, then builds a Merkle tree of cumulative balances per wallet.

4. Only the Merkle root is written on chain. `RewardPool.sol:117-121` exposes `submitMerkleRoot(...)` gated `onlyRole(DISTRIBUTOR_ROLE)` and rate limited to once per `merkleRootSubmissionPeriod = 1440 minutes` (`RewardPool.sol:57`).

5. Station owners pull their own rewards trustlessly. `RewardPool.sol:210` (`claim`) verifies a Merkle proof against the stored root for the cycle at `RewardPool.sol:196-198` and transfers WXM, tracking cumulative claims so nothing can be double claimed.

The important structural truth: the chain enforces the supply cap and the emission ceiling and the honesty of each withdrawal against a posted root, but the chain does not compute or verify the reward amounts themselves. Those are the output of a trusted off chain oracle.

## Claim 1: There is a genuine proof of data quality that rewards stations for real weather data

CLAIM. From the QoD README: "WeatherXM's QoD (Quality-of-Data) is the algorithm that evaluates the quality of weather data provided by the network's weather stations ... The QoD score of each station is an important parameter that controls its daily rewards."

REALITY. Confirmed and stronger than most DePIN peers. The QoD algorithm is real, open source under MIT, and substantial. It is not a stub. It performs out of bounds checks, self quality checks, spike and dip detection, constant value detection, per parameter availability thresholds, and minute and hour averaging, with separate parameterization per station model (WS1000, WS2000, Helium, M5). It emits a single `qod_score` in the range 0 to 1 that feeds the reward formula.

EVIDENCE.
- Algorithm size and structure: `qod/src/obc_sqc/model/` totals 3,177 lines (for example `constant_data_check.py` 660 lines, `minute_averaging.py` 667 lines, `raw_data_check.py` 292 lines).
- Per model thresholds and limits: `qod/src/obc_sqc/model/initial_params.py:1-277` (out of bounds limits `obc_limits`, availability thresholds, jump thresholds, per parameter averaging windows).
- The score that controls rewards: `qod/src/obc_sqc/model/obc_sqc_driver.py:236-241` assigns `result_df["qod_score"] = total_rewards`, and `obc_sqc_driver.py:364-388` computes `calculate_daily_score` as the mean of per parameter adjusted daily rewards.
- The reward formula that consumes it: `docs/.../reward-mechanism.mdx` states `reward_score = pol_score * data_quality_score` and `actual_reward = reward_score * max_reward_per_hardware_class`.

IMPACT. Positive. A buyer can independently run the same algorithm the network uses to score data. This is the most credible, verifiable part of the project. Note the caveat carried into Claim 3: the algorithm is open, but the network runs it off chain and only the resulting Merkle root is anchored.

Label: CONFIRMED IN CODE.

## Claim 2: There is a Proof of Backhaul mechanism

CLAIM. The audit brief asked specifically whether WeatherXM has a "proof of backhaul" that rewards stations for real connectivity.

REALITY. False as a WeatherXM claim. WeatherXM has no proof of backhaul mechanism. There is no `proof-of-backhaul` repository under either the `WeatherXM` or `weatherxm-network` organizations (the URL returns HTTP 404), and no such concept appears in the docs or the reward code. The only repositories on GitHub named for proof of backhaul belong to unrelated third parties (for example `youyou301/Proof-of-Backhaul`, a decentralized speed test, not connected to WeatherXM). WeatherXM's location and continuity mechanism is Proof of Location (PoL), which is a distinct concept: it scores whether a station is where its owner says it is and whether it has stayed put, not backhaul bandwidth.

EVIDENCE.
- `docs/.../proof-of-location.mdx` describes PoL as a score in [0,1] recalculated daily from Location Packets, with relocation resetting the score to 0 and resetting seniority.
- The reward pseudocode in `reward-mechanism.mdx` uses `pol_score` and `data_quality_score` only. There is no backhaul term.
- No backhaul source exists in `qod` or `smart-contracts`.

IMPACT. Anyone citing a WeatherXM proof of backhaul is describing something that does not exist. The correct integrity mechanism to evaluate is Proof of Location, covered in Claim 4.

Label: FALSE (as a WeatherXM claim).

## Claim 3: Rewards are an on chain WXM emission, decentralized and trustless end to end

CLAIM. Marketing framing of DePINs typically implies that tokens are emitted on chain to participants according to on chain rules. The token doc says the network "introduce[s] a smart contract that distributes a fixed budget of tokens ... to station owners ... daily."

REALITY. Overstated on the decentralization axis, accurate on the fixed budget axis. Three facts must be separated.

First, there is no on chain emission by minting. The token cannot mint (Claim on the token, proven by bytecode and by `WeatherXM.sol`). Rewards are paid from a pre minted vault, and the vault caps the daily release at 14,246 WXM on chain.

Second, the per station reward computation is a fully centralized off chain oracle. The docs are candid about this: "Every day, the reward algorithm assesses the performance of all weather stations ... and creates a Merkle Tree, the root hash of which is submitted to the RewardPool." Only the root is on chain.

Third, a single privileged role posts that root, and the chain does not check that the root reflects an honest QoD or PoL computation. `RewardPool.sol:53` defines `DISTRIBUTOR_ROLE`; `RewardPool.sol:117-121` lets that role submit any root once per day; and `RewardPool.sol:82-90` grants `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`, `UPGRADER_ROLE`, and `DISTRIBUTOR_ROLE` all to the deployer at initialization. The RewardPool is UUPS upgradeable (`RewardPool.sol:341`, `_authorizeUpgrade` gated by `UPGRADER_ROLE`) and pausable by the admin. So the operator can, within the tokens available to the pool and the vault drip, allocate rewards to any wallets it chooses, and can upgrade the distribution logic.

EVIDENCE.
- Off chain computation and root submission: `docs/.../reward-mechanism.mdx` (high level flow and pseudocode ending in `submit(tree.root_hash())`).
- Central role and upgradeability: `smart-contracts/src/0.8.25/RewardPool.sol:53`, `:57`, `:82-90`, `:117-121`, `:341`.
- Emission cap enforced on chain: `smart-contracts/src/0.8.25/RewardsVault.sol:25-26`, `:78-84`.

IMPACT. Medium. The honest description is a centrally computed reward with an on chain supply cap, an on chain emission ceiling, and trustless withdrawal against a posted result. It is not a trustless, decentralized reward calculation. Users must trust the operator's off chain math and its control of the distributor and upgrader keys. The mitigating design point is real: the vault caps daily emission at 14,246 WXM regardless of what the operator does, so the operator cannot inflate total emission beyond schedule, only misallocate within it.

Label: OVERSTATED.

## Claim 4: Reward distribution is transparent and verifiable via a Merkle tree, and Proof of Location protects against spoofed placement

CLAIM. The docs state that v2.0 uses "a Merkle Tree structure for transparent reward distribution" and that PoL "generates a score which is a confidence indicator about the station's location."

REALITY. Partly confirmed. The withdrawal path is genuinely trustless and verifiable: a user can reconstruct their leaf and verify their proof against the on chain root, and cannot claim more than allocated or claim twice. That part is confirmed in code. The transparency claim is narrower than it sounds, because only the distribution of a posted result is verifiable, not the computation that produced it (Claim 3). The Proof of Location mechanism is real as a concept and documented, but two limitations matter. It runs off chain, and unlike the Data Quality algorithm its source is not public. The open `qod` repository contains only the data quality package (`obc_sqc`); there is no PoL implementation in any public WeatherXM repository. PoL is also a heuristic based on Location Packets and station continuity, not a cryptographic proof of physical location.

EVIDENCE.
- Verifiable withdrawal: `RewardPool.sol:196-198` (`MerkleProof.verify` against `roots[_cycle]`), `RewardPool.sol:210-227` (`claim` with cumulative accounting), plus a signature based `claimFor` meta transaction path at `RewardPool.sol:247-273`.
- PoL is off chain and heuristic: `docs/.../proof-of-location.mdx` (daily recalculation from Location Packets, relocation zeroes the score and resets seniority).
- PoL source absent from public repos: the only algorithm published is `qod/src/obc_sqc/` (data quality); no PoL code is present.

IMPACT. Low to medium. The claim engine is trustless and well built. The word transparent should be read as transparent distribution of a centrally computed and partly closed source result.

Label: CONFIRMED IN CODE (distribution), with an OVERSTATED transparency framing.

## Claim 5: Fixed 100M supply, roughly 52M paid to stations over 10 years

CLAIM. The token doc: "Total supply of the $WXM token is 100M," "52M tokens will be distributed to station owners every day for providing data to the Network," "Distributed over the next 10 years," and "All tokens will be minted on token launch."

REALITY. Confirmed in code with an exact numeric match, which is a strong signal of honesty. The token mints exactly 100M at launch and can never mint again. The station reward tranche of 52M over 10 years implies 52,000,000 divided by 3,650 days equals 14,246.57 WXM per day. The on chain `RewardsVault.maxDailyEmission` is `14246 * 10 ** 18`. The documented schedule and the on chain constant agree to the token.

EVIDENCE.
- Fixed 100M, minted once: `WeatherXM.sol:11`, `:18-19`, `:31-33`; corroborated by live chain state (cap equals totalSupply equals 100M, no mint selector in bytecode).
- 52M over 10 years equals 14,246 per day: `docs/.../the-$WXM-token.mdx` versus `RewardsVault.sol:25`.
- Allocation split in docs: 55 percent station rewards (52M ongoing plus 3M beta), 30 percent supporters (four year linear, one year cliff), 10 percent treasury (five year linear), 5 percent liquidity.

IMPACT. Positive. Emission is predictable, capped, and matches the published tokenomics. Note it is a flat linear emission with no halving, so reward per station falls only as the active station count rises, not by protocol decay.

Label: CONFIRMED IN CODE.

## Claim 6: A real, sizable operating network

CLAIM. WeatherXM presents itself as a live global network of thousands of weather stations.

REALITY. Confirmed as a genuinely operating network, with a framing caveat. The project public API on 2026-08-06 reports 5,595 currently active stations, 9,802 claimed on chain, and 23,753 onboarded, with 4,446 high quality stations, a network average QoD of 86, and network uptime of 56 percent. These are meaningful, non trivial numbers and are consistent with a real DePIN, not vaporware. The caveat is that the largest headline number, onboarded_total of 23,753, is roughly four times the active_total of 5,595. Citing the onboarded figure as the network size overstates operational scale.

EVIDENCE.
- Live API `api.weatherxm.com/api/v1/network/stats` (last_updated 2026-08-06): onboarded_total 23,753, claimed_total 9,802, active_total 5,595, high_quality_stations 4,446, network_avg_qod 86, network_uptime 56.
- Independent corroboration that rewards are flowing: the same API exposes 28 daily reward data points, and the docs point to public Dune analytics for rewards and revenue.

IMPACT. Low. The network is real and active. Marketing that leads with the onboarded count rather than the active count inflates apparent size by about 4x.

Label: CONFIRMED IN CODE (traction real), with an OVERSTATED size framing.

## Positive Findings

- The token is immutable and clean: fixed 100M cap, entire supply minted at launch, no mint path, no owner, no admin roles, no pause, verified both in `WeatherXM.sol` and directly against live mainnet bytecode.
- The Data Quality algorithm is genuinely open source and substantial (3,177 lines, MIT), and it is the exact score that gates and scales rewards. This is rare and creditable transparency.
- The on chain emission ceiling matches the published 52M over 10 years schedule to the token (14,246 WXM per day), and the vault enforces that ceiling on chain regardless of operator behavior.
- Reward withdrawal is trustless: Merkle proof verification with cumulative claim accounting prevents overclaim and double claim, and a `claimFor` path enables gasless claims.
- The smart contracts ship with two independent third party audit reports in repository (`audits/beosin.pdf`, `audits/sayfer.pdf`).
- Real, live traction: thousands of active stations and a network average QoD of 86, served from the project own public API.

## Conclusion

WeatherXM is a legitimate, operating weather DePIN, not a scam and not vaporware. Its strongest claims survive a source read: the data quality mechanism is real and open source, the token is a clean fixed supply immutable ERC20, the on chain emission ceiling matches the published schedule exactly, and thousands of stations are genuinely active. Its weaknesses are honesty of framing rather than fraud. The reward computation is a centralized off chain oracle whose only on chain footprint is a daily Merkle root posted by a single privileged and upgradeable role, so end to end trustlessness is overstated. The Proof of Location mechanism, while real, is off chain and its source is not published, unlike the data quality code. There is no Proof of Backhaul in WeatherXM at all. And headline network size should be quoted as active stations (about 5.6k), not onboarded (about 23.8k).

The central risk to internalize is operator trust and key custody: whoever holds the RewardPool `DISTRIBUTOR_ROLE` and `UPGRADER_ROLE`, and the RewardsVault owner key, controls who gets rewards and can upgrade the distributor, bounded only by the 14,246 WXM per day vault cap and the 100M fixed supply. Within those hard on chain limits the reward allocation is trusted, not trustless.

**Score: 80/100, LOW RISK (Passed).** A real, audited, transparently documented DePIN with a genuine open source quality mechanism and honest tokenomics, marked down for a centralized off chain reward oracle, a closed source Proof of Location, upgradeable and centrally keyed reward contracts, and marketing that can overstate decentralization and network size.
