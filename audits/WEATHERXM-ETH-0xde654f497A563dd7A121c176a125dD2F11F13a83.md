WeatherXM is a weather station DePIN whose WXM token is a plain, immutable, capped and burnable ERC20 living on Ethereum L1 at 0xde654f497A563dd7A121c176a125dD2F11F13a83, with an Arbitrum bridged copy used for trading. On chain, WXM is an OpenZeppelin capped plus burnable ERC20. Total supply and cap both read one hundred million with eighteen decimals, the owner call reverts because no ownership mechanism exists, and none of the access control, mint, pause, or proxy functions are present in the runtime bytecode. Transfers are standard with no fee and no blacklist, and holders retain burn and burnFrom, so supply can only hold steady or fall. Circulating supply is currently around 25.7 million, roughly a quarter of the fixed cap, with remaining allocations unlocking linearly through about 2034, so the primary residual risk is thin secondary market liquidity rather than anything in the token contract itself.

## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A hard 100 million supply fully minted at launch, with rewards from preminted allocations rather than inflation" / Reality: confirmed exactly on chain, since cap equals total supply and no mint selector or minter role exists. The only nuance is that the docs foreground the Arbitrum trading address while the canonical supply and controls live on the Ethereum L1 token.

The official docs describe a hard one hundred million supply that is fully minted at launch and then distributed to stations, supporters, treasury, and liquidity over a ten year vesting plan, and the chain data matches this claim precisely, since cap equals total supply equals one hundred million, and because there is no mint selector and no minter role, no additional tokens can ever be issued. The station rewards emission the site advertises is therefore a release of preminted tokens, not inflation, which is fully consistent with a fixed cap token. The main nuance is that the docs lead with the Arbitrum address as the tradeable venue while the canonical supply and controls sit on the Ethereum L1 token, a relationship the Arbitrum token confirms because its l1 address points back to the L1 contract.
