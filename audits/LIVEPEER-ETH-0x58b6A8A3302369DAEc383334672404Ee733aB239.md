## Executive Summary

Livepeer is a real, long standing decentralized video infrastructure network with long lived, non custodial, governance controlled contracts and established backers. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects economic, not security, risks, plus an AI pivot that is newer than the mature transcoding business:

1. **The contracts are sound:** MEFAI confirms there is no arbitrary admin mint key; new supply is minted algorithmically by the protocol under on chain governance, staking is non custodial, and the contracts are long lived and open source.
2. **But the token is perpetually inflationary,** minting new LPT each round to stakers and node operators at a high annualized rate (on the order of 25 percent, currently easing as participation sits above the 50 percent target), so non staking holders are continuously diluted.
3. **Usage is real but modest and declining,** with quarterly demand side fees that are small in absolute terms and dwarfed by the value of new inflation paid to the supply side.
4. **The AI video pivot is promising but early and unproven,** and stake weight is poorly aligned with actual work, with the top operators capturing a large share of rewards.

The project is legitimate and the contracts are sound; the caution is perpetual inflation dilution, modest usage, operator concentration and an early AI pivot.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The World's Open Video Infrastructure"

> Site: "the world's open video infrastructure", a permissionless global network of independent GPU providers for video transcoding and, in the newer positioning, AI video.

**Reality: real but modest usage, high inflation to the supply side.** Transcoding demand is genuine but modest and recently declining, with quarterly demand side fees that are small in absolute terms and dwarfed by roughly the annualized value of new LPT inflation paid to node operators and stakers. The network is genuinely permissionless to join and staking is non custodial, both positives, but the economics are inflation supported rather than fee sustained, and non stakers are continuously diluted.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: AI Video Pivot, Concentration and Value

- **AI video is newer and less proven:** the AI video subnet launched recently and grew quickly, but off a near zero base; much of the transcoding decline reflects workloads shifting into these newer low latency AI pipelines. The momentum is real; durable large scale AI revenue is not yet demonstrated.
- **Operator concentration:** although running a node is permissionless, stake weight is poorly aligned with work: the top operators by stake capture a large share of rewards while performing a much smaller share of the actual transcoding, so reward capture skews to the largest staked operators.
- **Inflation dilution:** the high annualized inflation (on the order of 25 percent, currently easing as participation sits above the 50 percent target) accrues to bonded holders and operators, so stakers earn a yield above the headline rate while non stakers are diluted, a structural holder consideration rather than a security bug.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99 percent from its 2021 peak.
- **Team and backers (positive):** founded in 2017 with a long track record and established venture backing, which supports legitimacy.

## 8. Conclusion

Livepeer is a real, long standing decentralized video infrastructure network with long lived, non custodial, governance controlled contracts (no arbitrary admin mint key) and established backers, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 70/100. It is held back because the token is perpetually inflationary at a high annualized rate that continuously dilutes non stakers, because usage is real but modest and declining relative to the value of new inflation, because stake weight is poorly aligned with actual work, and because the AI video pivot is promising but early, against a roughly 99 percent drawdown. The contracts are sound; the caution is economic: perpetual inflation, modest usage and operator concentration.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Livepeer team:**
- Present the perpetual inflation and its dilution of non stakers clearly, and continue tuning the participation target.
- Improve the alignment between stake weight and actual transcoding work in reward distribution.
- Distinguish the early AI video business from the mature transcoding business in messaging.

**For users:**
- Understand LPT is perpetually inflationary and that not staking means continuous dilution.
- Treat the contracts as sound and non custodial, and value the real transcoding usage on its own merits while modeling the drawdown.
