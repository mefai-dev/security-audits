## Executive Summary

IoTeX is a legitimate, long running, fully doxxed DePIN focused Layer 1 with a verified token and real, shipped infrastructure. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "DePIN infrastructure layer" branding and modest actual adoption:

1. **The token and team are clean:** MEFAI confirms a verified Ethereum representation with a fixed 10 billion supply and a public, credentialed founding team.
2. **But device adoption is small versus the marketing scale.** Real on chain device identities and daily active wallets are modest, well short of the "DePIN infrastructure layer" framing.
3. **The "blue chip DePIN partners" are explorer listings,** not projects necessarily building on the IoTeX Layer 1, so ecosystem breadth is overstated.
4. **The chain is inflationary and its active block producer set is small** (24), and the project has pivoted narratives repeatedly, against a roughly 99 percent drawdown.

The team and products are real; the caution is modest adoption behind hype heavy DePIN and AI marketing, mild inflation and a small producer set.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The Modular Blockchain for DePIN"

> Site: "the modular blockchain for DePIN", connecting the physical and digital worlds, with verifiable off chain compute middleware, decentralized device identity, and a DePIN explorer.

**Reality: real products, modest adoption.** The verifiable off chain compute middleware, the decentralized device identity system and the DePIN explorer are genuinely shipped, a real strength versus vaporware. But device adoption is small: on the order of low tens of thousands of registered device identities and a few thousand device owners and daily active wallets, well short of the marketing scale. The "modular blockchain for DePIN" is substantive but early.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Partners, Decentralization and Value

- **Partner listings overstated:** the "blue chip DePIN partners" are largely tracked or listed on the project's DePIN explorer, not necessarily running on the IoTeX Layer 1, so explorer listing is conflated with ecosystem integration.
- **Limited decentralization:** permissionless voting, but 24 active block producers is a small set for a self described decentralized Layer 1.
- **Conditional deflation:** the token is mildly inflationary under the minting based IoTeX 2.0 model (newly minted IOTX for staking rewards at a governance set target), only partly offset by burns; net deflation is a future tense promise contingent on adoption that has not materialized.
- **Age and pivots:** founded around 2017 to 2019, the narrative has pivoted repeatedly across IoT, then machine finance, then DePIN, then DePIN and AI, chasing prevailing themes.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99 percent from its 2021 peak, with a thin market capitalization.
- **Team and backers (positive):** a public, credentialed founding team and disclosed backers, with no scam or rug mechanics found.

## 8. Conclusion

IoTeX is a legitimate, long running, fully doxxed DePIN focused Layer 1 with a verified fixed supply token and real, shipped infrastructure, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 68/100. It is held back because actual device adoption is modest versus the DePIN infrastructure layer marketing, because the advertised blue chip partners are largely explorer listings rather than projects building on the chain, because the active block producer set is small and the token mildly inflationary with only conditional net deflation, and because it is down roughly 99 percent from peak after repeated narrative pivots. The team and products are real; the caution is modest adoption behind hype heavy marketing.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the IoTeX team:**
- Report real device adoption and active usage figures, and distinguish projects building on the chain from explorer listings.
- Present the token as mildly inflationary today, and only describe net deflation once burns durably exceed issuance.
- Continue widening the active block producer set.

**For users:**
- Treat the DePIN partner and scale claims cautiously and note the modest verified adoption.
- Understand the token is mildly inflationary and that a small producer set governs block production, and model the drawdown.
