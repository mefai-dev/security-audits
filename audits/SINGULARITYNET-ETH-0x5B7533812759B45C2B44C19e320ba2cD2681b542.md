## Executive Summary

SingularityNET is a real, long standing decentralized AI project, but its AGIX token is now a deprecated migration artifact. The rating is driven by the token's status, not by a malicious contract:

1. **The AGIX contract is legitimate and non malicious:** MEFAI confirms a standard, long standing ERC 20 with 8 decimals; it is not a honeypot.
2. **But AGIX is officially deprecated.** SingularityNET, along with two other projects, merged into an alliance whose unified token trades under a different ticker. AGIX was delisted from major exchanges and converts one way into the merged token at a fixed ratio, and MEFAI's on chain read shows the AGIX supply has collapsed to roughly a tenth of its former size as holders convert away.
3. **The value of legacy AGIX is now a derivative of the merged token,** reachable primarily through a conversion portal with no hard deadline, with only thin decentralized exchange trading otherwise; anyone treating AGIX as the live project token is holding a legacy wrapper.
4. **The AI marketplace usage is thin and the AGI framing is aspirational,** and the token is down heavily from its peak.

The project and contract are legitimate; the caution is that AGIX is a deprecated, delisted, one way convertible migration artifact, which is a real risk for holders.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Live Decentralized AI Marketplace Token"

> Positioning: a decentralized AI marketplace where anyone can publish, buy and sell AI services paid in the token, on a mission toward beneficial decentralized artificial general intelligence.

**Reality: AGIX is deprecated, and the marketplace usage is thin.** AGIX was delisted from major exchanges during the alliance merger and now converts one way into the merged token; the project's own developer materials describe legacy AGIX support as deprecated. Anyone treating AGIX as the live project token is holding a legacy wrapper for the merged token. Separately, the decentralized AI service marketplace has no credible current usage or volume figures; the pitch has shifted toward future infrastructure, and value is driven by AI narrative rather than measured on chain service demand.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Decentralization, AGI and the Alliance

- **Alliance subsumption:** SingularityNET merged into a multi project alliance whose unified token trades under a different ticker, so SingularityNET is effectively a sub brand and AGIX is a legacy input to that merged token.
- **Centralization:** despite decentralization branding, direction is concentrated in the SingularityNET Foundation and its founder; the token architecture is now controlled at the alliance level.
- **AGI is aspirational:** artificial general intelligence is a long horizon research claim with no verifiable product; the framing is heavily narrative weighted.
- **Value:** the token is down heavily from its peak, with a small market capitalization and thin liquidity.

## 8. Conclusion

SingularityNET is a real, long standing decentralized AI project, but its AGIX token scores 50/100 because it is a deprecated migration artifact, not because the contract is malicious. AGIX was delisted from major exchanges, its on chain supply has collapsed to roughly a tenth of its 2 billion maximum as holders convert into the merged alliance token, and its value is now a derivative reachable primarily through a one way conversion portal with no hard deadline. The marketplace usage is thin and the artificial general intelligence framing is aspirational. The contract is legitimate; the caution, and the HIGH risk specifically for legacy AGIX holders, is the deprecated, delisted, one way convertible status.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the SingularityNET team:**
- Prominently and repeatedly warn AGIX holders that the token is deprecated and how to convert, with the conversion ratio and portal clearly documented.
- Publish real, current AI marketplace usage rather than narrative.

**For users:**
- Understand legacy AGIX is a deprecated migration artifact; verify the current conversion path before acquiring or holding it, and convert to the merged token rather than holding AGIX for its own sake.
- Do not treat AGIX as the live project token; the liquid asset is the merged alliance token.
