## Executive Summary

MyShell is a real, venture-backed AI agent platform with a shipping product, an exchange listing and genuinely open-source models. It is not a rug. The MEDIUM rating is driven by a headline claim and a concentration/liquidity profile:

1. The "decentralized AI consumer layer" claim does not survive scrutiny: agent inference runs on **centralized commercial model APIs and MyShell-hosted models**, with no node or validator set. The project's own documentation frames permissionless-blockchain integration as a **future** step.
2. On the Ethereum side, MEFAI's on-chain analysis found a **single wallet holding ~54.72 percent** of supply, with the top few addresses (including two exchange custody wallets) holding the large majority.
3. On-chain liquidity is thin, so price discovery is almost entirely on centralized exchanges.

The token is genuinely tied to a real product; the caution is the overstated "decentralized AI" framing plus concentration and thin on-chain liquidity.

## 2. On-chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's on-chain analysis confirmed the omnichain token identity and the supply split (Ethereum plus BNB Smart Chain summing to exactly 1 billion). The token contract owner is a **controller contract** for the omnichain bridge standard, meaning mint and burn exist via bridge mechanics under owner control (standard for this token type, but not trustless). Freeze-style restrictions were not observed.

---

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized AI Consumer Layer"

> Marketing: "MyShell is a decentralized AI consumer layer".

**Reality: MISLEADING.** The AI is not decentralized. The agent runtime orchestrates **centralized commercial model APIs** (for example large closed models) plus MyShell-hosted models. There is **no public node set, no validator network and no distributed or on-chain inference** in the project's public code. The "decentralization" that exists is economic (token-based ownership and monetization), not the AI compute.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: On-chain / Permissionless Framing

The product is marketed as decentralized AI, but the project's **own documentation** frames blockchain as a future step: it describes benefits that arrive "once the MyShell ecosystem integrates with permissionless blockchain". In other words, permissionless / on-chain operation is aspirational, not shipped, yet the product is already marketed as decentralized AI.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: Open-source Models (Credited)

> Claim: "We've independently developed several open-source AI models."

**Reality: TRUE and verified.** The project's public code hosts genuinely open-source, permissively-licensed voice and text models that are widely used (one voice model has tens of thousands of stars). This claim holds and deserves credit; it is a real contribution.

---

## 6. Holder Concentration and Liquidity

- **Ethereum top holder: a single externally owned account holding ~54.72 percent** of supply. The second and fifth largest are known exchange custody wallets; the top five hold roughly 89 percent.
- On-chain DEX liquidity is thin (a low-six-figure primary pool and a negligible secondary pool), so price discovery is almost entirely on centralized exchanges.

A single wallet controlling more than half of supply is a material concentration risk, and thin on-chain liquidity means large on-chain exits face heavy slippage.

## 10. Conclusion

MyShell is a real, funded product with genuine open-source model contributions, which keeps it out of the high-risk band. The MEDIUM rating (48/100) reflects that its "decentralized AI" headline is not supported (inference is centralized; even the project's docs place permissionless blockchain in the future), combined with a single wallet holding more than half of Ethereum supply and thin on-chain liquidity. Not a scam; the caution is the overstated decentralization claim plus concentration and exit-liquidity risk.

---

## 11. Recommendations

**For the MyShell team:**
- Either deliver decentralized inference (a verifiable node/validator set) or stop marketing the product as "decentralized AI".
- Address the ~54.72 percent single-wallet concentration transparently (lock, vest, or explain).
- Deepen on-chain liquidity so exits are not CEX-dependent.

**For users:**
- Treat "decentralized AI" as economic (token) decentralization, not compute decentralization.
- Note the single-wallet concentration and thin on-chain liquidity when sizing positions.
