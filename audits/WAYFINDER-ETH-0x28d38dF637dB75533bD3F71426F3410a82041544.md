## Executive Summary

Wayfinder is a real, venture backed AI agent project (by the team behind an established card game and token ecosystem) with a verified token. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "autonomous, decentralized agent" branding and a company hosted alpha, company controlled, human gated reality, plus a token safety trap and a severe drawdown:

1. **Autonomy is overstated.** The project's own documentation and terms make clear that agents ("shells") run within user set permissions, pause for user sign off before broadcasting, and may "hallucinate" or "deviate", with the user "solely responsible for verifying" every action. This is assisted automation, not autonomy.
2. **It is a company hosted product in alpha (a closed alpha waitlist, with an open alpha now promoted), not a live decentralized network.** The intelligence and interface layer is company operated; access is via an official portal.
3. **Half the supply is company controlled** (investors, team, foundation, launch partner), and there is a token safety trap: a second Ethereum address is also labeled "Wayfinder / PROMPT."
4. **Severe drawdown:** the token is down roughly 97 percent from its peak, with large investor and team unlocks still ahead.

The team and backers are real; the caution is overstated autonomy, alpha status, company control and the lookalike contract trap.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Autonomous AI Agents that Navigate On chain"

> Docs / marketing: provision a "Shell, your own agent in a sandboxed runtime, persistently running, transacting on your behalf"; "autonomous AI agents, called shells, navigate and interact with blockchain environments"; "decentralized, non custodial, and community governed."

**Reality: assisted automation with a human gate.** The project's own documentation and terms state that agents run "within whatever permissions you set", that the agent "confirms before executing any transaction" so the user reviews the details and approves before a trade executes, that agents "may hallucinate, produce misleading outputs, deviate from user instructions", and that users are "solely responsible for verifying AI generated outputs before acting." So shells are sandboxed runtimes that translate natural language into transactions with simulation, safety checks and **user sign off**, not autonomous agents acting on their own.

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Decentralization, Status and Value

- **Company hosted alpha:** the product is described as live but in alpha (a closed alpha waitlist, with an open alpha now promoted) with infrastructure still being built; access is via a company operated portal. It is non custodial at the wallet layer, but the intelligence and interface layer is company operated, not a live decentralized network.
- **Company control:** roughly **50 percent of supply** is held by investors, team, foundation treasury and a launch partner; the foundation runs token announcements. "Decentralized, community governed" overstates this.
- **Value:** the token peaked around 0.61 dollars at listing and trades near 0.02 dollars, roughly **97 percent below its all time high**, with large investor and team unlocks vesting into 2027 and beyond.
- **Backing:** genuinely venture backed by well known funds and built by an established studio, a real positive versus anonymous projects.

## 8. Conclusion

Wayfinder is a real, venture backed AI agent project with a verified token, so it is not a scam. It scores 54/100 (MEDIUM) because its "autonomous, decentralized, community governed agents" branding overstates a human gated, company hosted alpha product (agents require user sign off and may hallucinate; ~50 percent of supply is company controlled), because a second labeled contract is a trap for holders, and because the token is down roughly 97 percent from peak with heavy insider unlocks ahead. The backers and team are real; the caution is overstated autonomy, alpha status and the lookalike contract risk.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Wayfinder team:**
- Describe shells accurately as assisted, permission gated automation requiring user sign off, not autonomous agents.
- Prominently publish the canonical contract to protect holders from the lookalike address, and clarify the alpha status (closed versus open).
- Disclose the ~50 percent company controlled allocation and the forward unlock schedule.

**For users:**
- Verify the canonical contract (`0x28d38dF637dB75533bD3F71426F3410a82041544`), never the lookalike.
- Understand agents require your sign off and can err; this is assisted automation, not autonomy. Model the unlock overhang.
