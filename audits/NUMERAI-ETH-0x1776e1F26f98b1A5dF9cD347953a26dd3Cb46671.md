## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A fixed 11 million cap, non mintable, deflationary token with relinquished control" / Reality: every point is confirmed on chain, with supply below the cap and trending down and no live owner, minter, or upgrade authority.

Numerai states that supply is capped at eleven million, that no more NMR can ever be minted, that the contract is no longer controlled or upgradeable by Numerai, and that supply is deflationary through burns on Erasure and in the tournament. Public chain data supports every point: the live total supply is about 10.63 million, which is below the eleven million cap and declining, the owner and minter functions are absent or repurposed to burn, and the standard proxy implementation slot is empty. The claims and the observable data align.
