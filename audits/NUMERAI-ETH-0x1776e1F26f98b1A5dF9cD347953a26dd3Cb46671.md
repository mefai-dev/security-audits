## Executive Summary

Numerai is a genuine, long running AI and data science hedge fund whose crowdsourced tournament is live, working, and settling real value in NMR. Unlike many tokens that borrow an AI narrative, here the story and the shipped product line up closely, and the token contract is one of the cleanest classes on Ethereum: fixed supply, non mintable, and no longer controlled by the team.

1. **The product is live and delivering.** The weekly tournament runs at numer.ai, where thousands of models are combined into a single meta model that trades a real, operating hedge fund. Fund assets have grown from roughly 60 million dollars to about 550 million dollars over three years, the 2024 global equity strategy posted a 25.45 percent net return on a 2.75 Sharpe, and J.P. Morgan Asset Management committed up to 500 million dollars of capacity. This is an operating financial business, not a whitepaper.
2. **NMR has real, working utility.** Data scientists stake NMR on their models and either earn more NMR or have staked tokens burned when models underperform, with up to 25 percent of a stake burnable. This is the core incentive engine of the tournament, not a decorative token. Onchain data confirms the deflationary design: live total supply is about 10.63 million against an 11 million hard cap and trending down.
3. **Traction is verifiable.** Cumulative payouts to data scientists have exceeded roughly 43 million dollars, with over 1 million dollars paid in January 2025 alone. The project closed a 30 million dollar Series C at a 500 million dollar valuation in December 2025, executed a 1 million dollar open market NMR buyback in July 2025, and its newer Numerai Crypto meta model surpassed 300 staked models. These are measurable, corroborated signals of use.
4. **The contract is clean and de risked.** Minting was permanently disabled at the NMR 2.0 upgrade in February 2019, the supply cap was cut from 21 million to 11 million, control has been relinquished so there is no live owner or minter, the contract is not an active upgradeable proxy, and there is no transfer fee.

The residual cautions are modest: the token runs on legacy Solidity 0.4.x bytecode using a Backend and Delegate delegatecall pattern, some staking rails are mid migration, and exact circulating figures vary by source. None of these undercut the core finding. Numerai lands at 75 out of 100, Passed, LOW risk.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the NMR contract on Ethereum returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | Numeraire, NMR, 18 decimals, verified |
| Total supply | About 10,628,348 NMR, below the 11,000,000 hard cap |
| Mint authority | Permanently disabled; the mint pathway reverts and was repurposed to burn only at NMR 2.0 |
| Owner / admin | None live; `owner()` is empty and control was relinquished |
| Upgradeable | No active EIP 1967 proxy, the implementation slot reads zero (legacy Backend and Delegate delegatecall design, upgrade control relinquished) |
| Pause authority | Effectively none; the legacy stop flag reads false and is deprecated |
| Transfer fee | None |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level NMR is close to a best case ERC 20 for token holders. Supply is hard capped and cannot be increased because minting is permanently disabled, no live administrative key can mint, pause, or upgrade, and there is no transfer fee. Supply can only decrease, through tournament and Erasure burns, so the token is structurally deflationary. The one contract level nuance is that the code is old, Solidity 0.4.x with a Backend and Delegate delegatecall structure, but that source is verified and audited, and the ability to change it has been given up. This is a low risk contract, and it is backed by a live product rather than a promise.

## 4. Claim vs Reality: "Stake on your model to earn (or burn) cryptocurrency"

> Site: The site invites data scientists to stake on their model with NMR to earn or burn cryptocurrency based on performance.

**Reality: this is the real, working utility of the token.** Staking NMR against a model is the actual mechanism by which the tournament aligns incentives. Models that beat the benchmark earn NMR payouts, and models that underperform have staked NMR burned, up to 25 percent of the stake, which permanently removes it from supply. MEFAI's onchain read is consistent with this design: total supply sits at about 10.63 million, below the 11 million cap and trending downward. NMR is not a speculative bystander to the product, it is the settlement and collateral asset at the center of it.

## 6. Claim vs Reality: Fixed Supply, No Minting, No Team Control

> Site: The documentation states that NMR is capped at eleven million, that no more can ever be minted, that the contract is no longer controlled or upgradeable by Numerai, and that supply is deflationary through burns.

**Reality: every point matches the chain.** MEFAI confirmed a live total supply of about 10.63 million NMR, below the eleven million cap and declining, no live owner or minter, an empty proxy implementation slot, and a mint pathway that now only burns. The cap was genuinely cut from an original twenty one million to eleven million at NMR 2.0, and the deflationary burn mechanism is observable in the falling supply. This is one of the rarer cases where the tokenomics claims are fully supported by onchain evidence.

---

## 7. Claim vs Reality: The Public Website and Frontend

> Site: numer.ai presents live tournament and staking functionality to visitors and connected wallets.

**Reality: the frontend is clean and matches the product.** MEFAI's frontend review found no wallet draining patterns, no hard coded payout or lookalike address, and no auto connecting wallet or surprise signature prompts on the public homepage. The displayed metrics are pulled live from the official Numerai API rather than being static invented numbers, and the web3 code is a conventional ethers plus Privy staking stack calling only well known content, font, video, wallet auth, and RPC hosts, with no obfuscation or untrusted domains. The one scope caveat is that onchain approval targets used inside a signed, logged in staking session were outside this read only public review.

## 12. Conclusion

Numerai is a strong, legitimate project and one of the clearest examples of a token whose utility is real rather than narrative. The tournament, the staking, the burns, and the payouts are all live, and they feed an operating AI hedge fund that has grown to roughly 550 million dollars in assets, posted a 25.45 percent net return in 2024, secured institutional capacity from J.P. Morgan Asset Management, and raised a 30 million dollar Series C at a 500 million dollar valuation. NMR sits at the center of this as the staked and burned asset, and its onchain profile is close to ideal for a token holder: hard capped at eleven million, permanently non mintable, non upgradeable, control relinquished, and free of transfer fees, with supply verifiably below the cap and falling. The only material cautions are the age of the legacy 0.4.x contract code and the ongoing migration of some staking rails. Numerai scores 75 out of 100, Passed, LOW risk.

## 13. Recommendations

**For the Numerai team:**
- Continue publishing clear, source able tournament and payout statistics so external observers can verify traction directly.
- Communicate the staking rail migrations, including Atomic Blockchain Staking and the planned USDC Layer 2 option, with dates and scope so users understand where value settles at each step.
- Consider documenting the legacy 0.4.x contract design and its audit history prominently, since the old bytecode is the main technical caution for new reviewers.

**For users:**
- Understand that NMR utility is real and centered on staking, where up to 25 percent of a stake can be burned if a model underperforms, so participation carries genuine loss risk.
- Treat NMR as a fixed supply, non mintable asset that Numerai can no longer inflate or upgrade, and verify circulating supply against a live source since figures vary.
- When staking in the logged in area, verify the approval and staking contract targets in your wallet before signing, as those were outside this read only public review.
