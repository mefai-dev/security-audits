## 1. Project and Product Reality

Power Ledger is a real, operating company with software that is genuinely deployed, not a whitepaper shell. Its clearest commercial product is TraceX, an environmental commodities marketplace that trades renewable energy certificates and is integrated with real registries including M RETS, the largest voluntary REC registry in North America, and ERCOT in Texas as of July 2025. Alongside TraceX sits Transactive, the peer to peer energy trading engine used across numerous utility pilots. The named engagements span real institutions such as Energie Steiermark in Austria, BCPG MEA and TDED in Thailand, Tata Power DDL and CESC in India, KEPCO in Japan, Elia in Belgium and Metlen in Greece. The important caveat is that the flagship peer to peer energy trading remains largely at pilot, trial or R and D stage, while the fully commercial live product is the REC marketplace.

**Project level flags:** staking deprecated and own blockchain abandoned in 2024; POWR utility weakened after Solana move; flagship peer to peer energy trading still pilot stage roughly eight years after the ICO; client roster mixes old trials with live deployments

## 2. Website and Frontend Integrity

VERDICT: CLEAN | CONFIDENCE: high

The powerledger.io homepage is a conventional WordPress and Elementor marketing site whose HTML and custom JavaScript bundles contain zero Ethereum addresses and zero web3, wallet connect, approve, permit, transfer, or signTypedData logic, so there is no on site wallet interaction and no drainer surface at all. Every script loads from reputable hosts such as wordpress.com, HubSpot, Google Tag Manager, Mailchimp, and Vimeo, with no eval, atob, base64, obfuscation, or untrusted remote loaders present. The only token contract reference lives on the separate stake.powerledger.io page, a static notice that points users to the Ethereum staking contract 0xba33aa06901b7662e17869f588b77c04fb0cd872 on Etherscan (a staking contract, correctly not the POWR token) and instructs them to withdraw via Etherscan Write Contract and MetaMask directly rather than through any site hosted transaction, while linking the official Powerledger GitHub and X account. The official POWR token address is not embedded anywhere, and no lookalike or substitute token address was found. No live price or metrics API is called and no security audit badges are claimed, only legitimate awards and press recognition, so nothing is presented as backed that is not.

## 3. Traction and Claims

The partner and client names check out as genuine, well known energy sector organisations rather than fabricated logos, which is a strong positive. However the client roster mixes live commercial deployments with historical pilots and short trials, for example the RENeW Nexus and CUB projects in Australia, the eight month SEDA trial in Malaysia and early KEPCO phase work, without always flagging which are ongoing. Company messaging leans on superlatives such as the world's largest peer to peer energy trading pilot in India, and the 2025 to 2026 narrative openly frames the goal as converting pilots into commercial deployments, which implies most are not yet commercial. So the traction is verifiable in that the counterparties are real, but marketing forward in how pilot stage work is presented as momentum.

## 4. Token Utility and Economics

The design is a dual token model where POWR is the access and staking token that application hosts must stake or escrow to use the platform, and Sparkz is a fiat pegged stable unit used to settle energy trades locally. That utility is now materially weaker than the branding suggests, because Power Ledger deprecated its own Energy Blockchain and its staking program during the October 2024 migration to Solana, and POWR now exists mainly as an ERC 20 and Solana SPL token with Wormhole bridging. The genuinely revenue generating product, TraceX REC trading, runs on conventional fiat settlement and does not clearly require customers to hold or spend POWR. Utility therefore exists on paper but is limited and loosely coupled to the actual commercial business, leaving POWR closer to a speculative asset than an indispensable platform token.

The powerledger.io site markets a renewable energy technology platform with products such as xGrid, TraceX, PPA Vision, and hydrogen and EV tracking, framed as the democratization of the energy market. It highlights a Solana Mainnet integration and states that POWR holders can onramp, offramp, and trade POWR across both Ethereum and Solana through BitMart, and it exposes an Unstake POWR action implying staking. These are platform and exchange level features rather than properties of the Ethereum token contract itself. The narrative is broadly consistent with an energy focused DePIN positioning, and nothing on the page contradicts the onchain supply of one billion POWR.

## 5. Claim versus Reality

- "Powerledger is now integrated with Solana Mainnet, POWR users can onramp, offramp, access and trade POWR on both Ethereum and Solana" / Reality: a separate Solana token and exchange level bridging do exist, but this Ethereum contract carries its own migration announcement field and that field is empty, so no migration is signaled or enforced by the ERC20 itself.
- "Democratization of the energy market, xGrid, TraceX, PPA Vision, staking and Unstake POWR" / Reality: all energy trading, certificate, and staking logic lives outside this token; the ERC20 is only a plain transfer and approve contract and encodes none of the DePIN or staking behavior.
- Marketed roughly one billion POWR supply / Reality: matches the onchain constant exactly at 1,000,000,000 with circulating near total at about 999.5M per market trackers.

## 6. Contract Security Check (supporting)

- Supply is a hard coded constant of 1,000,000,000 POWR, which is 1000000000000000 base units at 6 decimals, fixed at deployment and unable to ever change; no cap logic is needed because supply is constant.
- No owner, no controller, and no admin function exist; the only privileged role is migrationInfoSetter at 0xd66873c0d33e27a1fd902eea663c285aecd192c1, currently an externally owned account, whose sole power is writing an informational string.
- No mint or emission path exists; the entire supply was assigned to the deployer in the constructor, so inflation is impossible.
- Not a proxy and not upgradeable, not pausable or freezable, no fee on transfer since transfers move value one to one, and decimals is a fixed constant of 6.

Direct calls to the verified contract return name PowerLedger, symbol POWR, decimals 6, version 1.0, and totalSupply 1000000000000000 base units, which equals one billion POWR at 6 decimals. Selectors for owner, controller, admin, implementation, mint, addMinter, pause, and unpause all revert with an invalid opcode, confirming those capabilities simply do not exist. The verified source shows the entire supply was assigned to the deployer in the constructor with no later minting path, and the sole administrative function setMigrationInfo can only record an informational string guarded by migrationInfoSetter. Storage reads show that setter is 0xd66873c0d33e27a1fd902eea663c285aecd192c1, presently an externally owned account, while the migration string slot is empty, so no migration has been announced through this contract. Transfers move balances one to one with no fee, and the contract is not a proxy, so its behavior is fixed for life.

## 7. Findings by Severity

INFO: fixed immutable supply, no mint, no owner over funds, not upgradeable, not pausable, no fee on transfer, decimals 6, and circulating supply sits near the full cap.
