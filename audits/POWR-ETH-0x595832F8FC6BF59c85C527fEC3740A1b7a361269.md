## Why This Report Exists

Power Ledger markets itself as a renewable energy DePIN: a company that runs energy trading (xGrid), renewable energy certificate trading (TraceX), microgrid management (uGrid), an own "Powerledger Energy Blockchain" built on Solana technology, and POWR staking. The POWR token is one of the oldest energy tokens in the market (2017). The question this report answers is narrow and testable: how much of the on chain, blockchain, and staking narrative is verifiable in public code and on chain state, and how much is centralized enterprise software wearing a token. No team, no personalities, no market speculation. Contract is secondary but included. Whole project focus.

## Method

Independent, read only verification against primary sources:
- Ethereum public RPC (`ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com`, cross checked on `eth.drpc.org`): `eth_call` for `name`, `symbol`, `decimals`, `totalSupply`, `owner`, `paused`; `eth_getCode` for bytecode, then a 4 byte function selector scan of the runtime bytecode.
- Solana public RPC (`api.mainnet-beta.solana.com`): `getTokenSupply` and `getAccountInfo` (jsonParsed) on the POWR SPL mint.
- GitHub source: shallow and blobless clones of the PowerLedger org repositories; git logs, fork parentage, README and Anchor manifests, and full text greps for energy specific logic.
- Company claims: powerledger.io platform and blockchain pages, quoted verbatim.

Every load bearing statement below carries either an on chain fact or a `repo/file` reference.

## The Foundation: the 2017 ERC20 is a clean, immutable token

The canonical POWR token on Ethereum is exactly what it is claimed to be, and this is the strongest part of the project. On chain calls return name `PowerLedger`, symbol `POWR`, decimals `6`, and totalSupply `1,000,000,000` POWR. Calls to `owner()` and `paused()` revert with `INVALID` opcode, meaning those functions do not exist. A selector scan of the 4831 byte runtime bytecode finds the standard ERC20 surface (`transfer` `0xa9059cbb`, `transferFrom` `0x23b872dd`, `approve` `0x095ea7b3`, `allowance` `0xdd62ed3e`, `balanceOf` `0x70a08231`, `totalSupply` `0x18160ddd`) and finds NONE of the dangerous ones: no `mint` (`0x40c10f19`), no `burn`, no `pause` (`0x8456cb59`), no `transferOwnership`/`renounceOwnership`/`setOwner`, no `upgrade`/`setUpgradeAgent`, no proxy `upgradeTo`. There is no admin key, no inflation path, no pause, no upgrade proxy. Supply is fixed at 1 billion. This is a plain, honest, immutable 2017 token with no honeypot machinery.

The important corollary: because the contract carries no logic beyond ERC20 transfers, it also carries no utility. Any "utility" of POWR lives entirely off chain, in Power Ledger's enterprise systems, which are the subject of the claims below.

## Claim 1: "The Powerledger Energy Blockchain" is Power Ledger's own Solana based chain

> CLAIM (marketing): Power Ledger built and operated its own energy blockchain on Solana based technology for high frequency energy trading, with validators and delegated staking.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED (and, as a present tense claim, deprecated).**

The public artifact behind this claim is the repo `github.com/PowerLedger/powr`, which the GitHub API reports as `fork: true, parent: solana-labs/solana`. It is a fork of the Solana validator. Of all commits on `master`, the only ones authored by Power Ledger are cosmetic: `Update README.md` (2024-02-09, 2023-09-05), `Updating internal references` and `Reset version to 1.13.7` (2023-09-01, author "Alex Aguilera Vega"). Every other commit is upstream Solana Labs work (Ryo Onodera, Trent Nelson, Jeff Washington, and so on). The pinned version, Solana v1.13.7, is old (Solana Labs shipped that line in late 2022 / early 2023). Last push to the repo was 2024-06-24. The README (`powr/README.md`) reads "POWERLEDGER ENERGY BLOCKCHAIN", "Copyright (c) 2024 Powerledger AG", and gates validator participation behind "Powerledger Validator Node Terms and Conditions", which describes a permissioned consortium network, not a public decentralized chain.

**EVIDENCE:**
- `PowerLedger/powr` fork of `solana-labs/solana`, git log shows only README and "Updating internal references" / "Reset version to 1.13.7" as Power Ledger commits; all substantive commits are upstream Solana.
- `powr/README.md`: "POWERLEDGER ENERGY BLOCKCHAIN", permissioned validator T&Cs, Copyright 2024 Powerledger AG.
- `PowerLedger/powr-explorer` is a fork of `solana-foundation/explorer`; its `app/utils/cluster.ts` still hard codes only the standard public Solana endpoints (`https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com`, `api.testnet.solana.com`, `api.devnet.solana.com`) plus a user supplied Custom URL. There is no published powr chain RPC, genesis hash, or entrypoint anywhere in the public repos, so the powr chain is not independently queryable.
- Company confirmation on powerledger.io/blockchain-technology: "we have evolved from our own blockchain to integrating with Solana mainnet" and "the Powerledger blockchain has been deprecated, and staking is no longer available."

**IMPACT:** The "own energy blockchain" is real only in the weak sense that a rebranded Solana v1.13.7 fork existed; it received near zero protocol level engineering in public, is now deprecated by the company itself, and was permissioned rather than the decentralized network the DePIN framing implies. Present tense marketing of "the Powerledger blockchain" is stale.

## Claim 2: On chain energy market protocol

> CLAIM (repo framing): PowerLedger operates "The on chain Monaco protocol" in `energy-markets-protocol`, an on chain matching engine for energy markets.

**REALITY: FALSE as labeled.** The repo `github.com/PowerLedger/energy-markets-protocol` is `fork: true, parent: BetDexLabs/protocol`. BetDexLabs/protocol is the Monaco Protocol, a Solana decentralized SPORTS BETTING and prediction market exchange. The fork is completely unmodified: a branch comparison shows it is identical to upstream `BetDexLabs/protocol` with zero commits ahead, all commits are authored by BetDex developers (the HEAD commit `chore: audit reports (#248)` is by `pawel-betdex` on 2024-12-17). There are zero Power Ledger commits. `README.md` is still titled "# The Monaco Protocol" and lists the Monaco betting program address `monacoUXKtUi6vKsQwaLyxmXKSievfNWEcYXTgkbCih`. `Anchor.toml` declares `monaco_protocol = "monacoUXKtUi6vKsQwaLyxmXKSievfNWEcYXTgkbCih"`. A full text grep for `energy`, `renewable`, `kwh`, `kilowatt`, `solar`, or `grid` across the Rust sources returns nothing.

**EVIDENCE:**
- Fork parent `BetDexLabs/protocol`; `energy-markets-protocol/README.md` line 1 "# The Monaco Protocol"; `Anchor.toml` program id `monaco_protocol`.
- Sole `develop` commit authored by `pawel-betdex`, 2024-12-17; fork created 2026-02-19 with no Power Ledger commits.
- Zero matches for any energy term in the program source.

**IMPACT:** The one public repository that could plausibly represent an on chain energy market is a bit for bit copy of a betting exchange with the label changed. There is no on chain energy trading logic to audit here. Presenting this as an energy markets protocol is misleading.

## Claim 3: xGrid, TraceX, uGrid, energy trading are the product

> CLAIM: xGrid enables peer to peer solar energy trading; TraceX is "a digital marketplace and exchange for carbon and renewable energy credits"; uGrid manages microgrids. Track and trade the provenance and flow of energy.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED with respect to on chain verifiability; core code is closed and unverifiable.** None of the flagship products appear in any public repository. The seven public repos are: `powr` (Solana fork), `powr-program-library` (an unmodified copy of `solana-labs/solana-program-library`, single 2023-09-01 commit, README still titled "Solana Program Library", no energy strings), `powr-spl-token` (only a logo and `powr_metadata.json`), `powr-explorer` (Solana explorer fork), `energy-markets-protocol` (Monaco betting fork, see Claim 2), `raydium-sdk-V2` (Raydium SDK fork), and `metaplex-program-library` (Metaplex fork). The platform page makes no claim about what runs on chain versus off chain, and names no clients, deployments, or countries in the audited content.

**EVIDENCE:**
- Repo inventory above; `powr-program-library/README.md` is verbatim "Solana Program Library"; grep for `energy|renewable|kwh|kilowatt` returns nothing in that repo.
- powerledger.io/platform: TraceX described as "a digital marketplace and exchange for carbon and renewable energy credits"; "Our platform's track and trade features help you stay on top of the provenance and flow of energy" with no on chain assertion.

**IMPACT:** The energy trading platform is centralized enterprise SaaS with a token attached, not a verifiable on chain marketplace. Per audit policy, closed core code is an explicit transparency finding: unseen product claims cannot be credited. This does not mean the products do not work in the real world (Power Ledger has run real utility pilots historically), only that none of it is auditable in code and none of it is demonstrably on chain.

## Claim 4: Staking as POWR utility

> CLAIM (marketing history): stake POWR, or delegate POWR to a validator that "can charge a commission ... from 0% to 100%".

**REALITY: FALSE as a current utility.** The company states directly: "After the expansion of Powerledger's operations to Solana mainnet, the Powerledger blockchain has been deprecated, and staking is no longer available." The validator commission language still on the site is residual copy for the deprecated chain. The site even surfaces an "Unstake POWR" action, consistent with a wind down rather than an active staking program.

**EVIDENCE:** powerledger.io/blockchain-technology: "the Powerledger blockchain has been deprecated, and staking is no longer available." powerledger.io/platform surfaces "Unstake POWR".

**IMPACT:** Anyone valuing POWR on a staking yield thesis today is valuing a discontinued mechanism. The staking utility that anchored the original token model is gone.

## Claim 5: POWR token utility today

> CLAIM (implicit): POWR is the utility and settlement token of an energy DePIN.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED.** On Ethereum, POWR is a plain immutable ERC20 with no embedded utility (Claim / Foundation above). The chain that would have consumed it is deprecated; staking is off. On Solana, POWR exists as a bridged SPL mint `PowerhfyX6JqFprXzy89fPBSKqmLj8Yzcvr3FkHrQHR` with only 4,109,619.78 tokens in circulation versus the 1 billion Ethereum supply, and that mint still has an ACTIVE mint authority `HCLV1cmh5L79DigmvmMTNUDXBUGBT4fwQKNHC6ys4tnU` (freezeAuthority null). The bridged design is normal, but it means the Solana side supply is centrally mint controlled, in contrast to the fixed Ethereum supply. Present day utility reduces to "a transferable token that Power Ledger's off chain systems may reference."

**EVIDENCE:** Ethereum bytecode selector scan (no utility functions); Solana `getAccountInfo` shows `mintAuthority = HCLV1cmh5L79DigmvmMTNUDXBUGBT4fwQKNHC6ys4tnU`, supply 4,109,619.781393.

**IMPACT:** The energy settlement / access utility narrative is not presently expressed in verifiable on chain code on either chain.

## Positive Findings

- The Ethereum ERC20 at `0x595832F8FC6BF59c85C527fEC3740A1b7a361269` is a genuinely clean, immutable, fixed supply, ownerless token. No mint, no pause, no upgrade proxy, no owner, no honeypot logic. This is a real safety positive and rare among 2017 era tokens. CONFIRMED on chain.
- The Solana POWR mint address published in `powr-spl-token` matches a real, initialized SPL mint on Solana mainnet-beta owned by the SPL Token program. The stated address is accurate. CONFIRMED on chain.
- Power Ledger is transparent on its own site about deprecating its blockchain and ending staking, rather than pretending the chain is still live. That candor is credited.
- Power Ledger is a long lived, real company (operating since 2016) with a documented history of real world energy pilots. This is not a phantom project.

## Conclusion

Power Ledger is a real company with a genuinely clean, immutable, fixed supply ERC20, and that combination keeps the score above the flag line at **60/100, MEDIUM RISK, Passed**. The token itself is not dangerous: no mint, no owner, no pause, no upgrade path, 1 billion fixed supply, verified on chain.

But the DePIN and on chain narrative is substantially overstated. The "Powerledger Energy Blockchain" is a cosmetically rebranded fork of Solana v1.13.7 that received almost no public engineering and has been deprecated by the company. Staking, a core original utility, is discontinued. The flagship products (xGrid, TraceX, uGrid) are closed enterprise SaaS with no public source and no demonstrable on chain footprint, so their claims cannot be credited in code. Most striking, the single public repository that reads like an on chain energy market, `energy-markets-protocol`, is an unmodified fork of the Monaco sports betting exchange with zero energy code and its original "The Monaco Protocol" README intact. The honest summary: POWR is a safe, immutable legacy token bolted to a centralized, largely closed energy software business whose on chain and blockchain claims are mostly legacy or aspirational rather than verifiable today. Core platform code is closed or unverifiable, and per policy the unseen product claims are not credited.
