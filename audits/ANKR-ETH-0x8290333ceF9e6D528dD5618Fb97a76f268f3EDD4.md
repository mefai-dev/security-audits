## Why This Report Exists

Ankr is one of the most recognizable brands in Web3 infrastructure. It markets itself as a "Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) of nodes," advertises 70 plus blockchains, 8 billion daily RPC requests, and a liquid staking suite, and it points to the ANKR token as the coordination and reward asset of a decentralized node network. The interesting question for an auditor is not whether Ankr is a real business. It plainly is: the endpoints answer, the staking tokens trade, the company has years of operating history. The question is whether the on chain, trust minimized, permissionless machinery implied by the words "decentralized" and "DePIN" actually exists in the code, or whether ANKR is a token bolted onto a centrally operated SaaS gateway plus a genuinely on chain liquid staking product. This report separates the two.

## Method

Read only. Everything below is sourced from public artifacts:
1. Direct `eth_call`, `eth_getStorageAt` and `eth_getCode` against public Ethereum and BNB Chain RPC nodes (`publicnode.com`, `bsc-dataseed`) to establish token facts, proxy status, admin keys, and live balances. EIP1967 implementation slot `0x360894...382bbc` and admin slot `0xb531...6103` were read directly.
2. The public source in github.com/Ankr-network. The entire org (about 102 repos across all listing pages) was enumerated. The RPC and coordination code is in `go-jsonrpc-proxy`, the DCCN gateway repos, and `ankr-cli`. The liquid staking contracts are not published in the public org; their verified source was recovered from on chain metadata (Sourcify), which exposes the private repo name `aws-smart-contracts` and the implementation file `AETH_R21.sol`.
3. Ankr's own documentation and blog for the marketed claims, and independent post mortems for the 2022 incident.
No team, identity, or off chain business analysis. Chain and market data are point in time snapshots dated 2026-08-05 and will drift.

## The Foundation: What Is Actually On Chain

Two distinct things share the ANKR brand, and they have very different trust properties.

The Ethereum ANKR token is exemplary. Reading it directly, `owner()` and `paused()` both revert, and the deployed bytecode contains no `mint`, `burn`, `pause`, or `transferOwnership` selector. The EIP1967 implementation slot is zero, so it is not a proxy. Supply is a hard 10,000,000,000 and no address can change it.

```text
# eth_call to 0x8290333ceF9e6D528dD5618Fb97a76f268f3EDD4 (Ethereum mainnet, 2026-08-05)
name()        -> "Ankr Network"
symbol()      -> "ANKR"
decimals()    -> 18
totalSupply() -> 0x204fce5e3e25026110000000  = 10,000,000,000e18
owner()       -> execution reverted   (no Ownable)
paused()      -> execution reverted   (no Pausable)
EIP1967 impl slot -> 0x0              (NOT a proxy)
bytecode scan -> mint/burn/pause/transferOwnership selectors ABSENT
```

The BNB Chain ANKR is a different animal. It is Ownable, its owner is a contract, and its bytecode exposes `mint(uint256)` (selector `0xa0712d68`) plus `transferOwnership`. This is the standard Binance Peg BEP20 template, where a bridge custodian can mint to mirror bridged supply. It is not the immutable object the Ethereum contract is.

```text
# eth_call to 0xf307910A4c7bbc79691fD374889b36d8531B08e3 (BNB Chain, 2026-08-05)
symbol()      -> "ANKR"   decimals() -> 18   totalSupply() -> 10,000,000,000e18
owner()       -> 0xbc17f9422986306ae22875411a5a7c9556217d45  (a CONTRACT)
bytecode scan -> mint(uint256) PRESENT, owner() PRESENT, transferOwnership PRESENT
EIP1967 impl slot -> 0x0   (not a proxy; a normal Ownable BEP20)
```

The liquid staking suite is real and on chain. ankrETH, ankrBNB, the `ethereumPool`, and the `ankrTokenStaking` contract all exist, all resolve, and all are EIP1967 upgradeable proxies:

```text
ankrETH (aETHc)  0xE95A203B1a91a908F9B9CE46459d101078c2c3cb  symbol "ankrETH"
   impl slot -> 0xe672e0e0101a7f58d728751e2a5e6da5ff1fda64   (upgradeable proxy)
   admin slot-> 0xcc4e08a5bfa887621ad68826a0d5913d7a2cb392   (ProxyAdmin)
ankrBNB          0x52F24a5e03aee338Da5fd9Df68D2b6FAe1178827  symbol "ankrBNB"
   impl slot -> 0x2c00ce1a935ff8c9e78580533e2e17c36281c26e   (upgradeable proxy)
ethereumPool     0x84db6eE82b7Cf3b47E8F19270abdE5718B936670
   impl slot -> 0xecce8778214fd9fe37c141a00cff19853ef5bc4a   admin -> 0xcc4e08a5...(same ProxyAdmin)
ankrTokenStaking 0xaB15B0bdDc012092cb23f53953149a7F8C1f9E7f
   impl slot -> 0xd315b7664b99e559c3e209628453de182e77e411   (upgradeable proxy)
```

Who can pull the upgrade lever matters. The ProxyAdmin `0xcc4e08a5...` for ankrETH and `ethereumPool` is owned by `0xa3464e1b86ee6941a600f9f69c61a47322af918f`, which is a Gnosis Safe v1.3.0 with `getThreshold()` of 3 over 5 owners. The ankrETH implementation's own `owner()` returns that very same Safe, so both the upgrade authority and the in contract admin powers (the ratio setter, the mint routing, the pause) are held by the 3 of 5 multisig. That is the correct read of the centralization today: real and sweeping owner power, but reasonably distributed custody of the keys. This is a material change from the pre 2022 posture, when the equivalent authority was a single key, which is exactly what made the December 2022 exploit possible (Claim 3).

## Claim 1: "A Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) of nodes"

> Site (ankr.com): "A world-class DePIN to power your projects." "Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) of nodes" ensuring "the shortest roundtrip path for RPC requests," "70+ blockchains," "8 billion daily RPC requests."

**REALITY: OVERSTATED.** At the code layer the public RPC service is a centrally operated gateway, not an on chain coordinated network of permissionless nodes. The public RPC routing component in Ankr's own GitHub is `go-jsonrpc-proxy`, self described as a "Simple and scalable JSON-RPC reverse proxy based on requested methods." It routes methods to backends defined in a static, off chain JSON config, and the shipped config's catch all upstream is Ankr's own endpoint `https://rpc.ankr.com`. There is no node registry contract, no permissionless operator set, no on chain routing, and no on chain settlement in this path. The original on chain decentralized compute vision, the DCCN (Decentralized Cloud Computing Network), survives only as archived gateway and tooling repos (`dccn-gateway`, `dccn-tools`), that is, it was not carried forward as the live product.

**EVIDENCE**
```json

{
  "methods": [
    { "name": "rpc_getProgramAccounts", "proxy-to": ["http://solana-indexer:38080"], "rate-limit": 10 },
    { "name": ".*",                      "proxy-to": ["https://rpc.ankr.com/solana"], "rate-limit": 100 }
  ]
}
```
```text
// Ankr-network/go-jsonrpc-proxy : README.md
"Simple and scalable JSON-RPC reverse proxy based on requested methods."

// Repo layout: cmd/  config/  server/  Dockerfile  docker-compose.yaml  jsonrpc-proxy.json
// No smart contract, no blockchain interaction, no node registration in the repo. Archived 2024-04-04.

```
```text

// Whole org has only 2 Solidity repos and both are game demos: no node-registry contract exists on chain.
```

**IMPACT.** The word DePIN implies permissionless hardware supply, on chain accounting, and censorship resistance by construction. What ships is a reverse proxy in front of Ankr controlled endpoints, with independent providers added behind Ankr's load balancers at Ankr's discretion. The service is real and heavily used, but it is decentralized in aspiration and marketing language, not in enforced on chain mechanism.

## Claim 2: "Liquid staking, ankrETH and ankrBNB, are real on chain contracts"

> Site: "9+ tokens supported," "$83M+ TVL," reward bearing liquid staking tokens ankrETH and ankrBNB backed by staked ETH and BNB.

**REALITY: CONFIRMED IN CODE.** These are genuine, deployed, live contracts, not vaporware. ankrETH and ankrBNB resolve to the marketed symbols, are upgradeable proxies, and carry real supply. Reading supply directly: ankrETH about 6,908 tokens (roughly $20M at prevailing ETH value) and ankrBNB about 1,012 tokens (roughly $0.6M). ankrETH is genuinely reward bearing: its live `ratio()` is about 0.808, so one ankrETH represents about 1.24 ETH of stake plus accrued rewards. The `ethereumPool` staking entry contract and the withdrawal pool exist and are proxied under the same 3 of 5 Safe. The staking documentation cites external audits by Beosin and Veridise. The important honesty caveat: this real code is NOT in Ankr's public GitHub org. The verified implementation, named `AETH_R21` (revision 21, so heavily upgraded over its life), was recoverable only from on chain verification metadata, which points at a private repo `aws-smart-contracts`. Minting is correctly gated to the pool and bridge operator contracts, but the owner retains sweeping powers including an unbounded ratio overwrite.

**EVIDENCE**
```solidity

// L123-127  mint is gated to the pool/bridge operator, not open:
function mint(address account, uint256 amount) external returns (uint256) {
    require(msg.sender == address(_bscBridgeContract) || msg.sender == address(_globalPoolContract), 'Not allowed');
    _mint(account, amount); ... }
// L39-42   modifier onlyOperator() { require(msg.sender == owner() || msg.sender == _operator, "Operator: not allowed"); _; }
// L103-106 repairRatio bypasses the 12h/threshold guards on the normal updateRatio path:

// L200 togglePause onlyOwner ; L112 updateGlobalPoolContract onlyOwner ; L155 changeOperator onlyOwner
```
```text
totalSupply(ankrETH 0xE95A203B...) = 0x0176758684ecc036860d  ~= 6,908 ankrETH   (live TVL, ratio ~0.808)
totalSupply(ankrBNB 0x52F24a5e...) = 0x36dabef135cab5b5e6     ~= 1,012 ankrBNB
ethereumPool 0x84db6eE8... : EIP1967 proxy, admin 0xcc4e08a5..., owner Safe 0xa3464e1b... (3-of-5)
```

**IMPACT.** This is the strongest, most real part of the project: the liquid staking product does what it says on chain, and it is live. Three caveats an auditor must attach: (a) the contracts are upgradeable and the owner can overwrite the exchange ratio with no bounds, so users trust the 3 of 5 multisig not to swap malicious logic or misprice the token; (b) the source is closed and only discoverable by chain forensics, which undercuts the "read the code" ethos; and (c) the BNB side is tiny today, the visible scar of Claim 3 below.

## Claim 3: "Ankr's staking contracts are secure"

> Implied by the liquid staking product and reinforced by audit references. The relevant history tests it.

**REALITY: OVERSTATED, with a documented catastrophic failure.** On 2 December 2022 the aBNBc (the earlier BNB liquid staking token) contract suffered an infinite mint. A compromised deployer key was used to deploy a malicious upgrade whose function bypassed the `onlyMinter` access control, allowing arbitrary minting. Attackers minted trillions of aBNBc across several transactions, drained about $5M, crashed the token 99 percent, and triggered contagion into Helio Protocol. Ankr subsequently deprecated aBNBc, relaunched as the current ankrBNB, and reimbursed affected users. The current ankrBNB proxy is the post incident redeployment, which is why its supply is small.

**EVIDENCE**
```text
Incident 2022-12-02 (aBNBc, BNB Chain):
- root cause: compromised deployer key was used to UPGRADE the proxy to a malicious implementation
  in which the mint approval path (analogue of AETH_R21.sol:129 mintApprovedTo) dropped its
  require(msg.sender == _globalPoolContract) guard, enabling arbitrary mint.
- attacker minted ~trillions of aBNBc, ~$5M extracted, aBNBc -99%, contagion to Helio (HAY)
- remediation: aBNBc deprecated -> ankrBNB relaunched (current proxy 0x52F24a5e...), users reimbursed,
  admin/owner authority moved to the 3-of-5 Gnosis Safe 0xa3464e1b... that controls the suite today
```

**IMPACT.** The failure was in exactly the surface an auditor cares most about: mint authorization under an upgradeable contract with privileged keys. Ankr's response (relaunch plus reimbursement) was responsible, and the current contracts moved to multisig control, but the episode is proof that "audited" did not equal "safe," and that key and upgrade governance are the live risk in this suite.

## Claim 4: "Independent node operators earn ANKR rewards; stakers earn a 49% share of fees"

> Blog/press: "Independent node operators will begin earning ANKR rewards for the work they do in securing the network," and stakers receive "a 49% share of the user fees spent on Ankr Network."

**REALITY: FALSE as an on chain claim.** There is no on chain proof of service and no on chain reward contract that measures RPC work by independent node operators and pays them trustlessly. What exists on chain is ANKR delegation to "full nodes" through `ankrTokenStaking`, which distributes staking rewards, but the underlying RPC service quality is not proven or settled on chain. Node providers are onboarded as legal entities registered in Ankr's permissioned Validator Hub, not through a permissionless on chain registry, and the 49 percent fee share is a stated economic policy rather than a modifier enforced by a public contract. In other words, the reward narrative is real as a business arrangement and partly real as ANKR staking, but the "independent operators paid on chain for proven RPC service" framing is not implemented in code.

**EVIDENCE**
```text
docs (ankr.com/docs, Ethereum Delegated Staking / Validator Hub):
"A node provider is a legal entity ... registered in Ankr's Validator Hub."   -> permissioned, off chain onboarding
ankrTokenStaking 0xaB15B0bd... : on chain ANKR staking to "full nodes" (proxy, audited Beosin/Veridise)
No public contract measuring RPC uptime/throughput per operator; no on chain settlement of the "49% of fees".
```

**IMPACT.** The distinction is the whole DePIN thesis. Ankr pays and admits providers by policy and off chain agreement. The ANKR staking layer is real, but it does not constitute on chain proof of node service.

## Claim 5: "ANKR is the token that powers a decentralized network"

> Site: "ANKR token, reforged." Token positioned as staking, governance (Ankr DAO), and payment asset.

**REALITY: CONFIRMED IN CODE for on chain staking; OVERSTATED for decentralization of the token itself.** The `ankrTokenStaking` proxy is live and holds about 59.3M ANKR staked, so the staking utility is not fictional. Governance is described as an Ankr DAO tool for treasury and pricing decisions. However participation is thin (about 0.6 percent of supply staked), and the token's decentralization is chain dependent: immutable on Ethereum, but owner mintable on BNB Chain (Claim 6). Payment for RPC is a fiat and credit product; ANKR is not the mandatory settlement unit of the gateway.

**EVIDENCE**
```text
balanceOf(ankrTokenStaking) on ANKR(ETH) = 0x310ba92b92768505de4188 = 59,292,432 ANKR  (~0.59% of 10B)
ankrTokenStaking 0xaB15B0bd... = upgradeable proxy, live, external audits (Beosin, Veridise)
GitHub org search for governance/dao/vote/Governor contracts -> ZERO repos.
   -> DAO voting is off chain Snapshot style signaling, not an on chain Governor with binding execution.
```

**IMPACT.** ANKR has genuine on chain staking utility and a governance story, but it functions more as an incentive and governance token around a centrally run service than as the trust minimized coordination asset the branding implies.

## Claim 6: "ANKR is a fixed supply token"

> Implied by the 10B fixed supply widely published across market data sites.

**REALITY: CONFIRMED on Ethereum, OVERSTATED across chains.** The Ethereum canonical ANKR is genuinely immutable with no mint path (see The Foundation). The BNB Chain ANKR, however, is an Ownable Binance Peg style BEP20 that exposes `mint(uint256)` to its owner, a contract at `0xbc17f9...`. Supply parity is a custodial promise on BSC, not a code guarantee.

**EVIDENCE**
```text
ETH ANKR 0x8290333c... : bytecode has NO mint selector, owner() reverts -> immutable
BSC ANKR 0xf307910A... : bytecode HAS mint(uint256) 0xa0712d68, owner() -> contract 0xbc17f9...
```

**IMPACT.** For holders on Ethereum, supply is trustless. For holders and integrators on BNB Chain, supply integrity depends on the bridge custodian behaving, which is the normal but often unstated property of pegged tokens.

## Positive Findings

1. The Ethereum ANKR token is a model of restraint: no owner, no mint, no pause, no proxy, fixed 10B supply, verified directly on chain. Many "infrastructure" tokens fail this basic test; ANKR passes it cleanly on its home chain. (ANK 07)
2. The liquid staking product is genuinely on chain and live. ankrETH, ankrBNB, `ethereumPool`, and `ankrTokenStaking` all resolve and carry real balances, with external audits by Beosin and Veridise cited. (ANK 02)
3. Both the upgrade authority and the in contract owner powers over the liquid staking suite sit behind the same Gnosis Safe 3 of 5 multisig (v1.3.0) at `0xa3464e1b...`, not a single EOA, a meaningful improvement over the pre 2022 single key posture that enabled the exploit.
4. Post exploit conduct was responsible: after the December 2022 aBNBc infinite mint, Ankr deprecated the token, relaunched ankrBNB, moved to multisig control, and reimbursed users.
5. The product is unambiguously real and used: the RPC service, the staking tokens, and a liquid market for ANKR (roughly $34M cap, actively traded on major venues) all exist. This is not a phantom project.

## Conclusion

Ankr is a real, operating Web3 infrastructure business with two clearly separable halves. The liquid staking half is honest: real upgradeable contracts on chain, real (if modest) TVL, multisig upgrade control, and a responsible track record after a serious 2022 exploit. The RPC and node network half is where the marketing outruns the code. The public "decentralized DePIN" RPC is, at the source level, a method based reverse proxy in front of Ankr controlled endpoints, with providers admitted through a permissioned Validator Hub and rewarded by policy rather than by any on chain proof of service. The ANKR token has genuine on chain staking utility and is beautifully clean on Ethereum, but it is an incentive and governance wrapper around a centrally operated service, not the trust minimized coordination layer the DePIN framing implies, and its supply guarantee does not extend to the owner mintable BNB Chain peg.

Netting the immutable token and real liquid staking against the overstated decentralization, the historical mint exploit, upgradeable proxies, and the owner mintable BSC token, this lands as a Passed project with medium risk. It is not a scam and not vaporware; it is a legitimate centralized infrastructure company wearing more decentralization language than its code supports.
