## Executive Summary

Aleph Cloud (ALEPH) is a real, long running decentralized cloud network with a genuine node fleet, a fixed non inflationary token, and real confidential compute technology. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects modest, subsidized usage and multi chain transparency gaps:

1. **The token is clean and fixed:** MEFAI confirms a fixed 500 million supply on the Ethereum contract, with no mint function, so there is no inflation.
2. **The network is genuinely operating,** with roughly a hundred core channel nodes and several hundred compute resource nodes running decentralized storage and compute for years.
3. **But the node economy is subsidized.** Rewards are paid from a pre allocated reserve rather than paid usage, and there is no disclosed proof that paid demand sustains the nodes.
4. **The multi chain supply reporting is inconsistent,** though Ethereum is the canonical chain (its ERC 20 holds the full 500 million supply), a real transparency and holder confusion risk.

The network, technology and clean supply are real strengths; the caution is subsidized economics, semi permissioned nodes, multi chain supply confusion and a deep drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized Cloud"

> Site: a decentralized cloud for compute, storage and indexing, with core channel nodes and compute resource nodes, confidential virtual machines, and a Web3 alternative to centralized cloud, plus an AI angle.

**Reality: real network, subsidized economics, partial decentralization.** Aleph Cloud has run since 2020 with a real, globally distributed node set and genuinely functioning decentralized storage and compute, and its confidential virtual machines using hardware trusted execution are a real, documented capability rather than a slogan, which distinguishes it from typical projects. But the node economy is subsidized: participation is primarily motivated by reward emissions from the reserve, and there is no disclosed proof that paid usage covers node economics. Onboarding is only partly permissionless (core channel nodes require large stakes and compute resource nodes must be sponsored by a core channel node), and the AI and confidential cloud framing runs ahead of demonstrated large scale paid workloads.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Economics and Value

- **Clean, fixed supply (positive):** the 500 million supply is fixed and non mintable, so there is no inflation; rewards are a redistributive subsidy from a reserve rather than new emission.
- **Subsidized economics:** node participation depends on reward emissions rather than disclosed paid demand, the key sustainability question.
- **Multi chain supply confusion:** the token spans several chains as bridged, non additive representations though Ethereum is the canonical chain (its ERC 20 holds the full 500 million supply), and trackers disagree on circulating supply, a transparency and holder confusion risk.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99 percent from its 2022 peak, a micro capitalization with thin liquidity.

## 8. Conclusion

Aleph Cloud (ALEPH) is a real, long running decentralized cloud network with a genuine node fleet, a fixed non mintable 500 million token, and real confidential compute technology, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 60/100. It is held back because the node economy is subsidized from a reserve with no disclosed proof that paid demand sustains it, because the multi chain supply is reported inconsistently though Ethereum is the canonical chain (its ERC 20 holds the full 500 million supply), because the node operator set is semi permissioned, and because the token is down roughly 99 percent from its 2022 peak with thin liquidity. The network, technology and clean supply are real strengths; the caution is subsidized economics, multi chain transparency gaps, partial decentralization and the drawdown.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Aleph Cloud team:**
- Disclose real paid usage revenue against the reward subsidy, so node sustainability is clear.
- Clearly document Ethereum as the canonical chain and reconcile the multi chain, non additive supply reporting, so market capitalization cannot be overstated.
- Publish node operator concentration and continue widening permissionless participation.

**For users:**
- Understand the node economy is reward subsidized and the multi chain supply is not additive, and reconcile the circulating figure carefully.
- Value the real, long running network and confidential compute technology on their merits, and model the drawdown and thin liquidity.
