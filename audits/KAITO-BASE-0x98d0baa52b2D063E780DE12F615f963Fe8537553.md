## Executive Summary

Kaito is a real, well backed artificial intelligence information platform with an unusually clean, immutable token contract. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "information layer for crypto" framing and a centralized company product, plus heavy insider allocation and unlock dilution:

1. **The token contract is clean:** MEFAI confirms the Base contract is a fixed one billion supply, not mintable, has no owner or admin keys, is not an upgradeable proxy and has no pause, freeze or blacklist, so contract level rug and backdoor risk is very low.
2. **But the platform is a centralized, for profit software product with a token attached.** The models, data pipeline, indexing and the headline attention metric are entirely company run and closed, not a decentralized protocol.
3. **The headline attention metric is a proprietary, off chain, changeable score,** not verifiable on chain data, and the content reward program is an attention farming incentive.
4. **Token utility is thin, insider allocation is heavy, and only a small share is circulating** with dilution running for years, against a drawdown of roughly 85 percent from its launch peak.

The company and contract are real and well backed; the caution is a centralized closed product, thin token utility and heavy unlock dilution.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The Information Layer for Crypto"

> Site: an intelligence and financial markets platform for the attention economy, positioned as the information layer for crypto, with attention leaderboards, content rewards and an analysis engine over many sources.

**Reality: a centralized, for profit software product with a token attached.** The platform is run by a company; the analysis models, data pipeline, indexing and the headline attention metric are entirely company operated and closed source. The token is attached to a centralized product, not a decentralized protocol. The headline attention metric is a proprietary, off chain algorithm over social feeds that is opaque, changeable at will and not auditable or reproducible by third parties, so it is a company score, not verifiable on chain data. The content reward program rewards volume and engagement of favorable posting, which is effectively a gamified attention farming incentive prone to manipulation.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Token Utility, Concentration and Value

- **Thin token utility:** the token's real utility is access tiers, staking, launchpad participation and incentive distribution, largely reflexive rather than fee backed cash flow to holders; company revenue does not accrue to the token by any verified mechanism.
- **Heavy insider allocation and unlocks:** roughly a quarter of supply is allocated to core contributors and a further share to backers, only about 24 percent is circulating, and vesting runs for years, so future dilution is significant.
- **Backers (positive):** the company is genuinely well funded by top tier venture backers and its founder has a credible finance background, so it is a real, well capitalized project, not a scam.
- **Value:** the token launched in early 2025, peaked shortly after, and is down roughly 85 percent from that peak.

## 8. Conclusion

Kaito is a real, well backed artificial intelligence information platform with an unusually clean, immutable and non mintable token (no owner, no pause, no blacklist), which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 66/100. It is held back because the platform is a centralized, for profit closed product marketed as a decentralized information layer, because the headline attention metric is a proprietary off chain score presented alongside on chain data, because token utility is thin and insider allocation is heavy with only about 24 percent circulating and multi year dilution, and because the token is down roughly 85 percent from its launch peak. The contract and company are real; the caution is centralization, thin utility and unlock dilution.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Kaito team:**
- Describe the product accurately as a centralized analysis service with a token for access and incentives, not a decentralized information layer.
- Disclose the attention metric methodology and label it as a proprietary social score distinct from on chain data.
- Publish the unlock schedule prominently so dilution is transparent.

**For users:**
- Value the clean, immutable contract, but understand the product is a centralized company service and the token utility is thin.
- Model the heavy insider allocation, the multi year unlock dilution and the drawdown.
