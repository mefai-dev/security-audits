## Executive Summary

Autonomys Network (AI3) is a technically real, novel Layer 1 with a running mainnet, but the token is a low float, unlock heavy, deeply drawn down micro capitalization whose AI framing runs well ahead of substance. It is not a scam. The rating, and the HIGH risk for current holders, is driven by that supply and valuation reality, not a malicious contract:

1. **The chain and consensus are genuinely novel and live:** MEFAI confirms a running Substrate Layer 1 with a proof of archival storage consensus and an EVM domain (Auto EVM, chain id 870).
2. **But the float is very low and the unlocks are heavy.** Only about a tenth of the 1 billion maximum circulates, roughly three quarters of the initial supply is insider held on a cliff and vesting schedule now unlocking, and farming rewards add continuous emissions.
3. **The AI framing is largely aspirational.** The proven substance is decentralized storage plus a new Layer 1; the agent identity and AI agent products are early with no evidence of real usage at scale.
4. **The valuation has collapsed** to a thin, near illiquid micro capitalization at all time lows.

The consensus and mainnet are real; the caution, and the HIGH risk for current holders, is a severe unlock overhang into a collapsed, illiquid price, inflationary farming, and an AI narrative ahead of substance.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "The Foundation Layer for AI"

> Site: the foundation layer for a decentralized AI future, with decentralized storage via proof of archival storage, an agent identity framework, on chain AI agents, and super scalable throughput.

**Reality: real storage Layer 1, aspirational AI.** The core technology is genuine: a running Substrate Layer 1 with a novel proof of archival storage consensus, permissionless farming (anyone can pledge disk space), a live EVM domain, and a credible engineering lineage, which distinguishes it from typical projects. But the AI framing is largely a re brand narrative layered on what is fundamentally a decentralized storage and new Layer 1 stack. The agent identity and AI agent products are framework and toolkit stage, with no evidence of meaningful production usage or agent volume, and the super scalable throughput is roadmap rather than delivered. Real economic usage is thin, with negligible fee revenue and token volume in the low tens of thousands of dollars, and network health metrics are largely self reported.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Float and Value

- **Very low float with heavy unlocks:** only about a tenth of the maximum circulates, roughly three quarters of the initial supply is insider held on a cliff and vesting schedule that is unlocking now, a severe structural overhang.
- **Inflationary farming:** roughly 35 percent of supply is still to be farmed out over decades, adding continuous sell side supply on top of the unlocks, even under the fixed 1 billion cap.
- **AI narrative ahead of substance:** the entire foundation layer for AI thesis is not backed by live AI usage; the proven substance is storage and a new Layer 1.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 95 percent or more from its 2025 peak, printing all time lows around the report date, a thin and near illiquid micro capitalization.

## 9. Recommendations

- Present the very low float, the insider unlock schedule and the multi decade farming emissions prominently, so the overhang is clear.

- Understand only about a tenth of supply circulates, that roughly three quarters of the initial supply is insider held and unlocking now, and that farming adds continuous emissions.
- Treat the AI framing as aspirational, and model the severe unlock overhang, the thin liquidity and the collapse to all time lows; the legitimate technology does not offset the current supply and valuation risk.
