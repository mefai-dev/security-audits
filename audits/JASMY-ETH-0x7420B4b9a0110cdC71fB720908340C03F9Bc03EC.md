## Executive Summary

JasmyCoin (JASMY) is a legitimate, Japan based personal data and IoT project with a real registered company, a credible team and a clean, fully circulating token. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects thin proven utility behind a strong narrative:

1. **The token is clean and fully circulating:** MEFAI confirms a fixed 50 billion supply with no mint function and no owner on the contract, and the token is roughly 99 percent circulating, so there is no unlock overhang and no inflation.
2. **The company and team are real.** Jasmy is a registered Tokyo company founded by former senior technology executives, and the token cleared a high regulatory bar to list on a licensed Japanese exchange.
3. **But the flagship data ownership platform has unproven adoption.** On chain activity is dominated by trading and transfers rather than data locker settlement, and headline partnerships read largely as pilots and announcements.
4. **The token is a Japan and Korea retail heavy speculative asset** with utility thin relative to its valuation, down heavily from its launch peak.

The company, team and clean supply are real strengths; the caution is thin proven utility, speculative retail concentration and a heavy drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Data Democracy" and the Personal Data Platform

> Site: a data democracy where users own and control their personal data through a personal data locker and IoT device integrations, positioned as a Japan led data sovereignty platform.

**Reality: real company, unproven platform adoption.** Jasmy is a genuine registered Tokyo company with a credible team of former senior technology executives, and it cleared an unusually high compliance bar to list on a licensed Japanese exchange, which distinguishes it from anonymous projects. But the flagship own your data platform has no demonstrable on chain adoption: there is no verifiable personal data locker user count, data marketplace volume or IoT device throughput at scale, and named corporate tie ups read largely as pilots and announcements rather than proven, revenue generating usage. A newer application chain and native gas framing is a claim, not demonstrated economic activity.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Utility and Value

- **Clean, fully circulating supply (positive):** the 50 billion supply is fixed and non mintable, and roughly 99 percent circulates, so there is no unlock overhang and no inflation, a real structural strength.
- **Thin utility versus valuation:** observable token utility is thin relative to the valuation, which rests on narrative and Japan and Korea retail demand more than usage.
- **Speculative retail concentration:** trading is heavily retail momentum driven, so volatility is high and hype cycle dependent.
- **Value:** the token is down heavily from its launch era peak; its later cycle secondary peak was also well above current levels.

## 8. Conclusion

JasmyCoin (JASMY) is a legitimate, Japan based personal data and IoT project with a real registered company, a credible former technology executive team, and a clean, fixed, non mintable and effectively fully circulating token, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 62/100. It is held back because the flagship own your data platform has unproven on chain adoption, because token utility is thin relative to a valuation that rests on narrative and retail demand, because it is a Japan and Korea retail heavy speculative asset, and because it is down heavily from its launch era peak. The company, team and clean supply are real strengths; the caution is thin proven utility, speculative retail concentration and the drawdown.

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Jasmy team:**
- Publish verifiable personal data locker user counts, data marketplace volume and application chain activity, so the utility is grounded in data.
- Distinguish pilots and announcements from live, revenue generating usage.
- Present the fully circulating, non mintable supply as the genuine strength it is.

**For users:**
- Value the real company, credible team and clean fixed supply on their merits.
- Understand token utility is thin relative to valuation, that the price is largely retail and narrative driven, and model the drawdown.
