## Executive Summary

Ocean Protocol is a real, long standing data for AI project with a standard, non malicious token, but the OCEAN token now sits in a contested, litigation shadowed migration limbo. The rating is driven by that status:

1. **The OCEAN contract is legitimate:** MEFAI confirms a standard ERC 20 with 18 decimals; it is not a honeypot.
2. **But the token is a stranded migration artifact.** OCEAN was folded into a multi project alliance and converted toward the merged token, then the project publicly withdrew from that alliance, triggering a dispute. The conversion route for ordinary holders is now disputed and disrupted, and MEFAI's read shows that roughly 81 percent of the supply has been sent to the burn address in the migration, leaving only about 268 million outside it.
3. **Exchange support is degraded:** at least one major exchange suspended OCEAN deposits and another declined to process the conversion, so holders can be left with a deprecated token and an uncertain redemption path.
4. **Marketplace usage is thin, headline activity is incentive driven, and control of the disputed assets is centralized** under a founder controlled entity; the token is down roughly 95 percent from its peak.

The technology and team are real; the caution, and the HIGH risk for holders, is the stranded, disputed migration and the degraded redemption path.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: The Migration and Holder Position

- **Subsumed then divorced:** OCEAN's independent thesis was folded into the merged alliance token at a fixed ratio, and the project then withdrew from the alliance, wanting to control its own tokenomics again, triggering a public dispute and litigation.
- **Disputed, disrupted redemption:** the alliance parties are in dispute over alleged unauthorized conversion and mismanagement of community funds; the practical result is that the OCEAN to merged token redemption route for ordinary holders is contested and disrupted, not a clean fixed ratio swap.
- **Degraded exchange support:** a major exchange suspended OCEAN deposits during the dispute, and another exchange declined to support the migration, so holders face a materially worse position than the clean swap the merger originally promised.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Marketplace, Usage and Control

> Site: a decentralized data marketplace to unlock data for artificial intelligence, with privacy preserving compute, data collectives and on chain prediction feeds.

- **Thin marketplace:** the data marketplace usefulness scales with participant density, and it has historically been thin, with limited datasets and limited liquidity, so marketing outran organic data commerce.
- **Privacy preserving compute is real but not plug and play:** the compute on private data design is genuinely differentiated but requires provider side infrastructure, which throttled adoption; it is more cited as a concept than measurably adopted at scale.
- **Incentive driven volume:** the large cited prediction feed volume is largely produced by incentivized prediction bots chasing token rewards, closer to subsidized reflexive activity than external paying demand.
- **Centralized control of disputed assets:** community assets were moved into a founder controlled offshore entity with the founder as sole director and a key holder on the treasury, which is the crux of the dispute and a concrete centralization and key holder control concern. The project has been founder led since 2017.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 95 percent from its 2021 peak, with a market capitalization on the order of tens of millions of dollars.

## 8. Conclusion

Ocean Protocol is a real, long standing data for AI project with genuine privacy preserving compute technology and a standard, non malicious token, but the OCEAN token scores 48/100 because it is a stranded, litigation shadowed migration artifact. OCEAN was subsumed into a merged alliance token, the project then withdrew from the alliance, and the holder redemption path is now disputed and disrupted, with a major exchange suspending deposits and another declining to support the conversion. Marketplace usage is thin, headline volume is incentive driven, and the disputed community assets sit in a founder controlled offshore entity, against a roughly 95 percent drawdown. The technology is real; the caution, and the HIGH risk for holders, is the contested, frozen migration.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Ocean Protocol team:**
- Publish a clear, current and reliable redemption path for OCEAN holders and resolve the disputed conversion transparently.
- Disclose the control and custody of the community assets and the litigation status plainly.

**For users:**
- Treat OCEAN as a contested migration artifact; verify the current redemption path before acquiring or holding it, and understand deposits may be suspended on some venues.
- Do not assume a clean fixed ratio swap into the merged token is available.
