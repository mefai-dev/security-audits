## Executive Summary

Silencio is a real noise mapping DePIN with a genuinely functional mobile app, but the audit still lands it at Flagged because the token that anchors the network sits behind a single externally owned key and because there is real confusion about where the token actually lives.

1. **The product is real and working.** The Silencio mobile app is live on both the App Store and Google Play, measures ambient sound intensity in decibels without recording audio, and feeds a live public noise map. This is a shipped consumer product, not a mockup, and it is credited accordingly.
2. **The primary flag is contract control.** The canonical SLC token on Base is a LayerZero omnichain fungible token whose `owner()` resolves to a single externally owned account, `0x82ae4f07a69fd588625ef7c48c9b4ed9aae73e3d`, with no bytecode. That one key governs the LayerZero peers and delegate, meaning cross chain issuance and configuration authority sit behind a single signer rather than a multisig.
3. **There is chain listing confusion.** SLC is commonly assumed to trade on Polygon. It does not. The canonical contract is on Base, the token is native on peaq, and it is also present on Ethereum. No Polygon deployment exists, and buying a lookalike on the wrong chain is a real user risk.
4. **Token utility is limited and the messaging has drifted.** The whitepaper describes earning SLC for contributions, yet the current homepage reframes the network as voice and audio data for AI, says contributors are paid in stablecoin, and does not name SLC at all. Onchain, Base value and demand side utility are thin.

The contract is clean at the code level, not a proxy, not upgradeable, not pausable, and with no fee on transfer, and the app is a legitimate working product. The reason Silencio is Flagged at 50 out of 100 is the concentration of cross chain authority in one externally owned key, compounded by chain confusion and a token whose real utility is thin. This is a real project with a concentrated admin key, not a scam.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the SLC contract on Base, cross checked on two independent Base RPC providers, returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | Silencio, SLC, 18 decimals, verified live |
| Base total supply | 5,682,879,234.34 SLC (via `eth_call` on `0x18160ddd`) |
| Onchain hard cap | None; `cap()` and `maxSupply()` both revert |
| Token standard | LayerZero OFT (`oftVersion` responds, `token()` returns itself), standard OZ ERC 20 base |
| Mint authority | No public mint function; SLC is minted or burned on Base only when bridged, governed by the OFT owner |
| Owner | `0x82ae4f07a69fd588625ef7c48c9b4ed9aae73e3d`; `eth_getCode` returns `0x`, so a single externally owned account, not a multisig contract |
| Upgradeable | No; EIP 1967 implementation and admin storage slots are both zero, so no proxy |
| Pausable | No; `paused()` reverts |
| Transfer fee | None |

**Interpretation.** At the pure code level SLC is reasonable: a standard, non upgradeable, non pausable LayerZero OFT with no fee on transfer. The material weakness is not a broken contract, it is who holds the keys. Because the owner is a single externally owned account rather than a multisig, one signer controls the peers and delegate that define cross chain issuance and routing. A lost or compromised key could configure a hostile peer, which is why this contract carries HIGH residual risk despite otherwise clean code. The concerns for this project, contract control, chain confusion, and thin token utility, sit alongside a product that genuinely works.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Teaching AI every language on Earth" and the noise mapping app

> Site: "Teaching AI every language on Earth." "The voice and audio data network for AI. Real voices from millions of people in 180+ countries." "2.5M+ Contributors."

**Reality: the underlying product is genuinely live and working, and it is credited.** The core of Silencio is a real noise mapping DePIN. The mobile app is published on both the App Store (rated about 4.8 stars over roughly 1.6K ratings) and Google Play (500K plus downloads, about 18.5K reviews at 3.66 stars), it measures ambient sound intensity in decibels without recording audio, lets users explore geographic hexagons and check into venues, and it feeds a live interactive noise map. This is a shipped consumer product with real adoption, and MEFAI treats that as a genuine positive. The caveats are that the largest headline figures, for example billion scale data point totals and seven figure contributor counts, are self reported and vary widely by source and date, and that several analytics and AI overlays on the map are still marked coming soon.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Contract Control and the Omnichain Owner

> Site: "Auditable end to end, by design." The token is presented as the decentralized reward and incentive layer of the network.

**Reality: a single externally owned key owns the omnichain OFT.** This is the heart of the flag. MEFAI read `owner()` on the Base contract and it resolves to `0x82ae4f07a69fd588625ef7c48c9b4ed9aae73e3d`, an address whose `eth_getCode` returns `0x` on two independent RPCs, so it is a single externally owned account with no smart contract logic behind it, not a Gnosis Safe or any multisig. For a LayerZero OFT, the owner governs the peers and the delegate, which is precisely the machinery that authorizes cross chain mint and burn and defines which remote contracts are trusted counterparties. Concentrating that authority in one key means a lost, phished, or malicious signer could set a hostile peer or misroute cross chain issuance. An end to end auditable design that terminates in a single unmultisigned key is a real centralization exposure, and it is the main reason Silencio is Flagged rather than passed.

## 5. Claim vs Reality: Where SLC Actually Lives (Base and peaq, not Polygon)

> Site and market perception: SLC is widely assumed to be a Polygon token.

**Reality: the canonical contract is on Base, and the token is native on peaq.** MEFAI found no Polygon deployment of SLC. The canonical trading contract is the Base LayerZero OFT at `0x6bd83abc39391af1e24826e90237c4bd3468b5d2`, the token is native on peaq at `0x5c3126bfB9A68a7021d461230127470b3824886B` following the December 2024 migration, and it is also present on Ethereum. Users who go looking for SLC on Polygon will not find the official asset, and any Polygon token bearing the name would be a lookalike. This chain confusion is a practical safety issue for buyers even though it is not itself evidence of wrongdoing by the team.

## 6. Claim vs Reality: "Paid in Stablecoin" and SLC Utility

> Site: contributors are "Paid in stablecoin," and the SLC symbol does not appear on the current homepage.

**Reality: real reward utility in the docs, thin demand and a messaging gap in practice.** The whitepaper is consistent that users earn Noise Coins that convert to SLC and that on chain staking is live, with roughly 2 billion SLC reported staked, and SLC trades on KuCoin, Gate, MEXC, BitMart and BingX. So the token does have a real reward and staking loop. The problem is twofold. First, the value side is tiny: price near 0.00004 USD, market cap around 713K USD, only about 1,657 holders on Base, and dust tier user earnings, with one review reporting about 1.86 USD earned over eight months. The noise data marketplace and API revenue that would give SLC durable demand remain largely aspirational. Second, the current homepage says contributors are paid in stablecoin and never names SLC, which drifts from the earn SLC narrative that runs through the whitepaper and exchange listings. That inconsistency does not indicate fraud, but it muddies what the token is actually for.

---

## 7. Claim vs Reality: "Auditable End to End, by Design"

> Site: "Auditable end to end, by design."

**Reality: a marketing line, not a security audit badge.** MEFAI reviewed the homepage, which is a static Framer marketing site. It contains no wallet connect, no `window.ethereum` usage, and no approve, permit, or transfer calls, so there is no drainer surface, and no `0x` address of any kind is published in the page or its main bundle, which also means there is no wrong chain or lookalike address embedded on the site. That is a clean frontend and is credited. However the "Auditable end to end, by design" wording is a design claim about traceable data provenance, not a published third party security audit of the token contract. Readers should not mistake it for an audit certification.

## 12. Conclusion

Silencio is a real DePIN with a working product, which is why this report opens by crediting it: the mobile app is live on both stores, measures ambient noise without recording audio, and feeds a public noise map with genuine adoption. As a token, however, SLC scores 50 out of 100 and is Flagged, driven mainly by contract control. The canonical Base contract is a clean, non upgradeable, non pausable LayerZero OFT, but its owner is a single externally owned key that governs cross chain peers and delegate, so cross chain issuance authority sits behind one signer. That concentration, compounded by real chain confusion (Base and peaq, not the Polygon many assume), a token whose onchain utility and demand are thin, and a homepage that has drifted to a voice AI framing that no longer names SLC, is what earns the flag. The caution here is not a contract exploit or a fake product, it is a legitimate project whose most important key is centralized and whose token story is muddier than it should be.

---

## 13. Recommendations

**For the Silencio team:**
- Move OFT ownership and the LayerZero delegate from a single externally owned key to a multisig or timelock, and publish the signer set, so cross chain authority is not one key.
- Clearly state the canonical chains, Base and peaq, on the website and in listings, and explicitly warn that there is no Polygon deployment, to prevent lookalike purchases.
- Reconcile the reward messaging: if contributors are paid in stablecoin, say how SLC still fits, and if they earn SLC, name it on the homepage rather than only in the docs.
- Ship and evidence the data marketplace and API revenue that would give SLC durable demand, and label self reported metrics as estimates.

**For users:**
- Buy SLC only on Base (`0x6bd83abc39391af1e24826e90237c4bd3468b5d2`) or peaq; there is no official Polygon token, so treat any Polygon SLC as a lookalike.
- Understand that a single externally owned key controls the omnichain OFT peers and delegate, which is a real centralization risk despite the clean contract code.
- Enjoy the app on its own merits, but treat SLC utility as limited today, with dust tier earnings and marketplace demand still unproven.
