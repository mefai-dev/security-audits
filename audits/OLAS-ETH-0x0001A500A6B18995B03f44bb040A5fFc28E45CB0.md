## Executive Summary

Autonolas (Olas) is a real, working autonomous AI agent protocol with genuine on chain agents and a verified token. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "co owned network of autonomous agents" branding and an incentive driven, inflationary, drawdown heavy reality:

1. **The token and agents are real:** MEFAI confirms a verified Ethereum token governed by a DAO timelock, with genuinely working agents and marketplaces deployed across several chains.
2. **But much on chain agent activity appears incentive driven.** Agents earn rewards by hitting staking activity thresholds, so MEFAI assesses that a significant share of the agent transaction volume is farming those thresholds rather than clearly organic paying demand.
3. **The token is inflationary** via a bonding and developer rewards model, and the value driver depends on staking incentives.
4. **Deep drawdown and thin liquidity** temper the picture despite a genuine product.

The product and agents are real; the caution is incentive driven activity, inflation and a deep drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "A Co Owned Network of Autonomous AI Agents"

> Site: a network of autonomous AI agents, an agent launcher and a task marketplace, with co owned AI and autonomous services running continuously on chain.

**Reality: real agents, incentive driven activity.** The agent launcher and the task and prediction marketplaces are genuinely live and running, a real strength. But agents earn OLAS by meeting staking activity thresholds (for example performing a required number of transactions per period), so MEFAI assesses that a meaningful share of the on chain agent transaction volume is farming those thresholds rather than clearly organic paying demand. The autonomous agent economy is real but is currently bootstrapped and sustained primarily by staking incentives.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Inflation, Governance and Value

- **Inflationary emission:** OLAS is minted via bonding for protocol liquidity and via developer and staking rewards, so supply grows on a scheduled curve, and value accrual depends on the incentive programs continuing.
- **Governance is DAO based (positive):** control sits with a DAO timelock and locked OLAS voting rather than an anonymous single key, a real decentralization positive.
- **Value and liquidity:** the token is down roughly 99.7 percent from its early 2024 peak, near all time lows, with a small market capitalization (on the order of 7 million dollars) and thin daily volume, so sizable positions face slippage.

## 8. Conclusion

Autonolas (Olas) is a real, working autonomous AI agent protocol with a verified token under DAO timelock governance and genuinely deployed agents and marketplaces, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 62/100. It is held back because a large share of the on chain agent activity is driven by staking activity thresholds rather than organic paying demand, because the token is inflationary via bonding and developer and staking rewards with value accrual dependent on those incentives, and because it is down roughly 99.7 percent from peak near all time lows with a small market capitalization and thin liquidity. The product and agents are real; the caution is incentive driven activity, inflation and a deep drawdown.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the Olas team:**
- Report organic paying agent demand separately from staking incentive driven activity, so usage is not overstated.
- Present the inflationary emission and the dependence on incentive programs clearly.
- Deepen liquidity to reduce slippage risk.

**For users:**
- Understand that much agent activity is staking incentive driven, and that emission is inflationary.
- Value the real DAO governance and deployed agents on their merits, and model the drawdown and liquidity risk.
