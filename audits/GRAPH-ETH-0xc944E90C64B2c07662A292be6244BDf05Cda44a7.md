## Executive Summary

The Graph is a genuine, long running, audited decentralized indexing protocol with a verified token and a real, working network. It is not vaporware. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "decentralized" and "AI" framing and a more centrally governed, subsidy driven, modestly used reality:

1. **The network and product are real:** MEFAI confirms a functioning decentralized indexing network serving subgraph queries, with a completed hosted service sunset and a completed migration of the live protocol to Arbitrum.
2. **But protocol control sits in a small multisig.** Mint authority, protocol parameters and the upgradeable Arbitrum contracts are governed by The Graph Council, a 6 of 11 multisig with no on chain timelock, so upgrades and issuance changes can execute immediately at 6 signatures.
3. **Supply is inflationary, not fixed,** and query fees are small, so indexer economics are still subsidized by roughly 3 percent gross issuance, not fee sustained.
4. **The AI and knowledge graph framing outruns substance,** and the token is a deep drawdown small cap that printed a new all time low around the report date.

The protocol integrity is solid; the caution is small multisig control, ongoing dilution, modest usage and an AI narrative ahead of delivery.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized Indexing / The Google of Blockchains"

> Site / docs: an "indexing protocol for organizing and accessing blockchain data" via subgraphs, coordinated by Indexers, Curators and Delegators; framed as indexing chain data "similarly to how Google indexes the web."

**Reality: a real but modest, subsidy driven network.** The decentralized network genuinely serves queries (on the order of a couple of billion per month) and the hosted service was actually sunset, a real achievement. But the active indexer set is on the order of low hundreds, not thousands, query fees are small in absolute terms, and roughly 3 percent gross issuance still subsidizes indexers, so the economics are emission supported rather than fee sustained. The scale is genuine infrastructure, not the "Google" the framing implies.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Decentralization and Governance

- **Council multisig control:** protocol upgrades, parameters and minter roles are controlled by The Graph Council, a 6 of 11 multisig with no on chain timelock. This is multi stakeholder but still a small signer set that can execute immediately, a meaningful trust assumption rather than trustless governance.
- **Upgradeable L2 protocol contracts:** the canonical protocol has run on Arbitrum since the 2023 migration (the hosted service was separately sunset in June 2024). The L2 GRT token itself has its upgrade authority burned, so the token code is immutable, but the protocol's other Arbitrum contracts (staking, rewards) are upgradeable under the Council, so those live economic contracts are mutable by the same multisig.
- **Migrations delivered (positive):** the hosted service sunset and the L2 migration were actually completed, which is a genuine execution positive versus many projects that only promise decentralization.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: "AI / Knowledge Graph" and Value

- **AI framing is early:** the newer knowledge graph and AI agent additions (structured data standards, a token data API, agent payment tooling) are early stage add ons; the core business remains subgraph indexing, so the AI narrative is ahead of demonstrated substance.
- **Inflation and dilution:** issuance targets ~3 percent gross with burns (query fee, curation and delegation taxes) offsetting roughly one third, for ~2 percent net per year under the current model (the realized average since 2020 has been closer to 1.4 percent). Modest and honest, but non staking holders are continuously diluted.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99 percent from its 2021 peak and printed a new all time low around the report date, so market risk is elevated even though protocol integrity is solid.

## 9. Conclusion

The Graph is a real, audited, long running decentralized indexing protocol with a verified token, a working network and two genuinely delivered migrations, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 68/100. It is held back because protocol control (issuance, parameters, upgradeable Arbitrum contracts) rests in a 6 of 11 Council multisig with no on chain timelock, because supply is inflationary with subsidy driven indexer economics, because usage is modest versus the "Google of blockchains" framing, and because the AI and knowledge graph narrative outruns delivery. The protocol is solid; the caution is multisig control, dilution and an AI narrative ahead of substance.

---

## 10. Recommendations

**For The Graph team:**
- Add an on chain timelock over the Council multisig for issuance and upgrade actions, and continue widening the signer set.
- Present indexer economics transparently as subsidy supported until query fees sustain the network.
- Distinguish the early AI and knowledge graph work from the mature indexing business in messaging.

**For users:**
- Understand GRT is inflationary and that protocol upgrades and issuance are controlled by a 6 of 11 multisig that can act immediately.
- Treat the network as real but modest, and model ongoing dilution and the deep drawdown.
