## Executive Summary

Arbitrum is a real, leading Ethereum layer 2 rollup with enormous total value locked and a verified, deeply liquid governance token. It is legitimate and technically strong, and to its credit it markets itself as "progressive decentralization" rather than a finished decentralized system. The MEDIUM rating reflects the gap between "community governed" and the current control reality:

1. **The sequencer is centralized.** The project's own documentation states the sequencers for Arbitrum One and Nova "are currently maintained by the Arbitrum Foundation." A single operator orders every transaction; a decentralized sequencer is explicitly a future goal.
2. **A Security Council multisig can override the DAO instantly.** A 9 of 12 Security Council multisig "can skip delays and directly access all admin and upgrade functions of all smart contracts," with no DAO vote and no timelock. A 7 of 12 can schedule routine upgrades without a governance vote.
3. **ARB is a governance token with no direct claim on Arbitrum One revenue.** MEFAI's on chain read confirms ~10 billion ARB. Arbitrum One's own sequencer revenue still flows to the operating company, not to ARB holders or, by default, the DAO. The DAO treasury does receive an 8 percent share of a 10 percent Arbitrum Expansion Program license fee (2 percent to the Arbitrum Developer Guild) paid by separate third party Orbit chains that settle outside Arbitrum One, a license fee on other chains, not a share of Arbitrum One's own fees. Team and investors (~44 percent combined) continue to unlock monthly into 2027.

To its credit, permissionless validation (BOLD) went live in early 2025, a genuine decentralization milestone. Arbitrum is not a scam; it is a real rollup whose decentralization and token value accrual are still maturing.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized" Governance

> Docs: "The distribution of the $ARB governance token decentralizes governance of these protocols"; "$ARB holders... collectively shape the future of Arbitrum protocols." Framing: "progressive decentralization."

**Reality: real DAO, hard limits on its power.** Governance genuinely runs on chain through the ARB DAO, and Arbitrum is honest in calling this "progressive." But two control points sit above the DAO:
- The **sequencer** is a single Foundation operated node. If it acts maliciously it "could reorder or censor certain transactions before they achieve hard finality," mitigated by a roughly 24 hour force inclusion backstop. Decentralized sequencing is not live.
- A **9 of 12 Security Council** can instantly access all upgrade functions of all contracts, bypassing any DAO vote or delay; a 7 of 12 can push routine upgrades without a vote. Ultimate emergency control is a small multisig, not token holders.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Token Value and the AIP 1 Episode

ARB is a **governance token with no direct claim on the sequencer revenue it helps generate**. Arbitrum One's own sequencer surplus still flows to the operating company, not to ARB holders; the DAO has only discussed routing it. The only DAO level revenue share is the **Arbitrum Expansion Program**: separate third party Orbit chains that settle outside Arbitrum One pay a 10 percent license fee on their own net revenue (8 percent to the DAO treasury, 2 percent to the Arbitrum Developer Guild). That is a license fee on other chains, it predates 2026, and it is not a share of Arbitrum One's own fees.

The canonical governance credibility gap is **AIP 1** (early 2023), the DAO's first proposal, which sought "ratification" of 750 million ARB (about 1 billion dollars) to the Foundation, framed "as a ratification and not a request", after the tokens had already been moved to a Foundation controlled wallet and selling had begun before the vote closed. Over 76 percent of voters opposed it; the Foundation subsequently split the proposal and pledged a vesting schedule. Governance has matured since, but the episode showed insiders acting first and seeking approval after the fact.

## 9. Conclusion

Arbitrum is a legitimate, leading Ethereum layer 2 with a verified, deeply liquid governance token, real usage, and a genuine decentralization milestone in BOLD permissionless validation. It scores 68/100 (MEDIUM) because a single Foundation operated sequencer orders all transactions, a 9 of 12 Security Council can instantly upgrade every contract with no timelock, and ARB confers no direct claim on the sequencer revenue it helps generate. The network is real and decentralizing; the caution is centralized sequencing, emergency upgrade trust and weak direct value accrual.

---

## 10. Recommendations

**For the Arbitrum team:**
- Ship decentralized sequencing and communicate the timeline; disclose the Security Council members and the exact 9 of 12 / 7 of 12 powers prominently.
- Be explicit that ARB has no direct claim on Arbitrum One revenue (that revenue flows to the operating company) and that the only DAO revenue share is an Expansion Program license fee from separate third party chains; publish forward unlock schedules.

**For users:**
- Understand a single sequencer orders transactions today and a small multisig can instantly upgrade contracts.
- Model monthly insider unlocks to 2027; treat ARB value accrual as indirect.
