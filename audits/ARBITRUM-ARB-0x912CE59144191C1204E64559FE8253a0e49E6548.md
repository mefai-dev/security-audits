## Executive Summary

Arbitrum is a real, leading Ethereum layer 2 rollup with enormous total value locked and a verified, deeply liquid governance token. It is legitimate and technically strong, and to its credit it markets itself as "progressive decentralization" rather than a finished decentralized system. The MEDIUM rating reflects the gap between "community governed" and the current control reality:

1. **The sequencer is centralized.** The project's own documentation states the sequencers for Arbitrum One and Nova are currently maintained by the Arbitrum Foundation. A single operator orders every transaction; a decentralized sequencer is explicitly a future goal.
2. **A Security Council multisig can override the DAO instantly.** A 9 of 12 Security Council multisig can skip delays and directly access all admin and upgrade functions of all smart contracts, with no DAO vote and no timelock. A 7 of 12 can schedule routine upgrades without a governance vote.
3. **ARB is a governance token with no personal dividend, though the treasury it governs does capture revenue.** MEFAI's on chain read confirms ~10 billion ARB. Per Arbitrum's own fee distribution documentation, Arbitrum One's sequencer surplus (the L2 base fee and the surplus fees) accrues to the Arbitrum DAO treasury, which ARB holders collectively govern; only the L1 base fee, a pass through reimbursement for posting batch data to Ethereum, goes to the sequencer operator (the Arbitrum Foundation). ARB confers governance over that treasury, not a personal cash flow. Team and investors (~44 percent combined) continue to unlock monthly into 2027.

To its credit, permissionless validation (BOLD) went live in early 2025, a genuine decentralization milestone. Arbitrum is not a scam; it is a real rollup whose sequencing and upgrade decentralization are still maturing.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "Decentralized" Governance

> Docs: "The distribution of the $ARB governance token decentralizes governance of these protocols"; "$ARB holders... collectively shape the future of Arbitrum protocols." Framing: "progressive decentralization."

**Reality: real DAO, hard limits on its power.** Governance genuinely runs on chain through the ARB DAO, and Arbitrum is honest in calling this "progressive." But two control points sit above the DAO:
- The **sequencer** is a single Foundation operated node. If it acts maliciously it could reorder or censor certain transactions before they achieve hard finality, mitigated by a roughly 24 hour force inclusion backstop. Decentralized sequencing is not live.
- A **9 of 12 Security Council** can instantly access all upgrade functions of all contracts, bypassing any DAO vote or delay; a 7 of 12 can push routine upgrades without a vote. Ultimate emergency control is a small multisig, not token holders.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Token Value and the AIP 1 Episode

ARB is a **governance token**: it confers no direct, personal cash flow (no dividend or distribution) on network revenue. What it does confer is collective governance of the Arbitrum DAO treasury, and that treasury genuinely captures Arbitrum One's revenue. Per Arbitrum's own fee distribution documentation, on Arbitrum One the L2 base fee and the surplus fees flow to the Arbitrum DAO treasury (which ARB holders govern); only the L1 base fee, a pass through reimbursement for the cost of posting batch data to Ethereum, goes to the sequencer operator (the Arbitrum Foundation). Auctioned priority ordering (Timeboost) is a further DAO revenue stream. Separately, the Arbitrum Expansion Program routes a 10 percent license fee from third party Orbit chains that settle outside Arbitrum One, 8 percent to the DAO treasury and 2 percent to the Arbitrum Developer Guild.

So the honest framing is not that ARB captures nothing: Arbitrum One's sequencer surplus accrues to the ARB governed treasury. The nuance is that it accrues to a collectively governed treasury, not to individual holders as a dividend, and turning that value into holder benefit depends on governance decisions.

The canonical governance credibility gap is **AIP 1** (early 2023), the DAO's first proposal, which sought "ratification" of 750 million ARB (about 1 billion dollars) to the Foundation, framed "as a ratification and not a request", after the tokens had already been moved to a Foundation controlled wallet and selling had begun before the vote closed. Over 76 percent of voters opposed it; the Foundation subsequently split the proposal and pledged a vesting schedule. Governance has matured since, but the episode showed insiders acting first and seeking approval after the fact.

## 9. Conclusion

Arbitrum is a legitimate, leading Ethereum layer 2 with a verified, deeply liquid governance token, real usage, and a genuine decentralization milestone in BOLD permissionless validation. It scores 68/100 (MEDIUM) because a single Foundation operated sequencer orders all transactions, a 9 of 12 Security Council can instantly upgrade every contract with no timelock, and while Arbitrum One's sequencer surplus accrues to the ARB governed DAO treasury, ARB confers governance over that treasury rather than a personal dividend. The network is real and decentralizing; the caution is centralized sequencing and emergency upgrade trust.

## 10. Recommendations

**For the Arbitrum team:**
- Ship decentralized sequencing and communicate the timeline; disclose the Security Council members and the exact 9 of 12 / 7 of 12 powers prominently.
- Continue to make the flow of sequencer surplus to the DAO treasury transparent, and clarify how holders benefit from a collectively governed treasury; publish forward unlock schedules.

**For users:**
- Understand a single sequencer orders transactions today and a small multisig can instantly upgrade contracts.
- ARB is governance over a treasury that does capture sequencer surplus, not a personal dividend; model monthly insider unlocks to 2027.
