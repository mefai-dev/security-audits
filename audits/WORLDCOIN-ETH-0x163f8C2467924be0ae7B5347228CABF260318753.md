## Executive Summary

World (Worldcoin) is a large, well funded proof of personhood project with a real, verified, capped supply token and genuine technology (the Orb, World ID). It is not a scam. It is rated MEDIUM to HIGH because two of its central claims sit in sharp tension with the public record:

1. **The "private, anonymous, owned by everyone" framing is contradicted by regulators and by holder concentration.** Multiple national data protection authorities have suspended, banned or ordered deletion of its biometric collection, and public analysis has placed the large majority of circulating WLD in a very small number of wallets.
2. **The "decentralized" framing overstates a centrally controlled system:** the Orb hardware, firmware and verification pipeline, and the majority of token custody, remain controlled by a private company and its foundation, with community run Orbs and community governance still early stage.
3. The token is genuinely capped at 10 billion with zero inflation for 15 years, a real positive, but roughly a quarter of supply sits with insiders on multi year unlocks.

The technology and funding are real; the caution is the regulatory, privacy and concentration reality behind the "owned by everyone, private" narrative.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: World ID Privacy and "Proof of Human"

> Whitepaper / site: World ID "securely and anonymously proves that every user is a real and unique human online"; "Proof of Human"; images "are only analyzed in local memory, and are deleted after verification."

**Reality: multiple regulators have found the biometric collection unlawful or non consensual.** The public record includes suspensions, bans, fines or deletion orders from data protection authorities in multiple jurisdictions, including Spain (the AEPD), Portugal (the CNPD), Germany (the Bavarian authority BayLDA), Kenya, Hong Kong (the PCPD), South Korea (the PIPC), Brazil (the ANPD) and Indonesia, on grounds including invalid consent (for example, paying people to scan their irises undermining "free and informed" consent) and data protection violations. Regulators have repeatedly judged the "deleted on device" assurances insufficient for lawful biometric collection. The privacy claim is presented as settled; the regulatory reality is contested and, in several jurisdictions, adverse.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: "Owned by Everyone" vs Concentration

> Site: World is "designed to be owned by everyone"; the whitepaper describes the majority of WLD as intended to be claimed by individuals simply for being verified unique humans (paraphrased).

**Reality: circulating supply has been highly concentrated.** Public analysis (including a securities filing) has indicated that roughly the top 100 wallets controlled about 90.44 percent of circulating WLD, a Gini coefficient near 0.9985, and that at launch the bulk of circulating tokens sat with market makers. One caveat: at least one of the largest addresses is the Ethereum to World Chain bridge, which aggregates many users' deposits rather than a single holder, so the raw figure overstates true single holder concentration. Roughly 25 percent of total supply is allocated to insiders (investors and team) on multi year unlocks. The "free and fair, owned by everyone" framing overstates the actual, concentrated float.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: Decentralization

The Orb is manufactured and its firmware and verification pipeline controlled by a private company; community operators only recently began to be able to run their own Orbs, and the majority of token custody and governance remain with the company and its foundation, which describes itself as only "progressively decentralizing." Hardware, verification and majority custody are centralized; user run Orbs and community governance are aspirational and early stage.

## 9. Conclusion

World (Worldcoin) is a real, well funded project with genuine technology and a verified, hard capped token, so it is not a scam. It scores 48/100 (MEDIUM to HIGH) because its two central claims collide with the public record: "private, anonymous proof of human" against multiple national regulators suspending, banning or ordering deletion of its biometric collection; and "owned by everyone" against a highly concentrated float and a centralized control model over hardware, verification and majority custody. The capped, non inflationary token is a genuine positive; the caution is the regulatory, privacy and concentration reality.

---

## 10. Recommendations

**For the World team:**
- Reconcile the "private, anonymous" claim with the regulatory findings, and disclose the jurisdictions where collection is suspended or banned prominently.
- Publish transparent holder distribution data rather than "owned by everyone" framing.
- Deliver and disclose genuine decentralization of the Orb, verification and governance, or qualify the "decentralized" claim.

**For users:**
- Understand the biometric collection model faces active regulatory challenges in multiple countries, and that the float is concentrated.
- Treat the capped, non inflationary supply as a real positive, distinct from the privacy and decentralization claims.
