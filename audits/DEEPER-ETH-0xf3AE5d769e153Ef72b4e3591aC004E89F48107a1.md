## Executive Summary

Claim vs reality audit of Deeper Network (DPR). An established privacy DePIN whose Ethereum token enforces a real 10 billion mint cap and carries no transfer fee, but whose owner, a Gnosis Safe multisig, retains strong and undisclosed powers to mint up to the cap, pause all transfers, and freeze any account, and where a widely circulated contract address actually resolves to a different token entirely.

Deeper Network is an established privacy DePIN project whose DPR token is native on the Deeper Chain and mirrored as an ERC20 on Ethereum. The single most important due diligence finding is that the contract address often circulated for it maps to a completely different asset. A direct read against the public node shows that 0x3597bfD533a99c9aa083587B074434E61Eb0A258 returns the name and symbol DENT with eight decimals and a roughly one hundred billion supply, which is the Dent Wireless token. The real, canonical DPR contract is 0xf3AE5d769e153Ef72b4e3591aC004E89F48107a1, confirmed by its onchain name Deeper Network, symbol DPR, and eighteen decimals, and corroborated by CoinGecko and Deeper Network support documentation.

## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A fixed 10 billion supply privacy DePIN" / Reality: the 10 billion cap is genuinely enforced in the verified source, but the live Ethereum supply of about 6.6 billion is lower than aggregator figures because emission is ongoing and DPR also lives natively on the Deeper Chain.
- What the marketing does not surface is that the owner multisig can still mint up to the cap, pause transfers, and freeze accounts.

The project claims a fixed ten billion maximum supply with sixty percent set aside for mining rewards, and the verified ERC20 source does enforce a hard ten billion mint ceiling, so that core claim holds up. The live Ethereum total supply reads about 6.6 billion, which is lower than the roughly ten billion that market aggregators display, a gap explained by ongoing emission and by DPR circulating natively on the Deeper Chain rather than only on Ethereum. What the public marketing does not surface is that the owner retains strong controls, since the same verified source exposes an owner only mint function, a pause switch that can halt all transfers, and a freeze account function that can block any address from sending or receiving.

## 4. Website and Frontend Integrity

The site is reachable and advertises more than two hundred thousand nodes, and it does not print the token address on the homepage. This matters because a widely circulated wrong address resolves to a completely different token, so a visitor must confirm the official DPR contract from the project docs before interacting.
