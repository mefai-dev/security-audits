# Deeper Network (DPR): Whitepaper Claims vs Code Reality

**Score: 46/100, RISK: HIGH (Flagged)**

Date: 2026-08-06

Token (live): Ethereum ERC20 `0xf3AE5d769e153Ef72b4e3591aC004E89F48107a1`, name "Deeper Network", symbol DPR, 18 decimals, verified source (Solidity 0.4.24), contract name `Token`. Live total supply on Ethereum is 6,616,479,552.71 DPR against a hard cap of 10,000,000,000 DPR coded in the contract. A second bridged ERC20 lives on BNB Chain at `0xa0a2ee912caf7921eaabc866c6ef6fec8f7e90a4`. The mining and staking economy runs on the native Deeper Chain (a Substrate appchain), so the Ethereum token is a tradeable, bridged representation rather than the token that the product actually consumes.

Websites: deeper.network, shop.deeper.network, deepernetwork.ph (regional storefront), explorer and staking dashboards on the Deeper Chain appchain.

GitHub: github.com/deeper-chain/deeper-chain (Substrate node, Apache 2.0, default branch `master`, last push 2025-04-04). All file and line references below are against that branch.

Data as of 2026-08-06. Chain state read live from Ethereum mainnet public RPC; market data from CoinGecko; source read from raw GitHub blobs.

## Why This Report Exists

Deeper Network is marketed as a decentralized privacy and bandwidth sharing DePIN: buy a Deeper Connect hardware box, share your unused bandwidth into a "decentralized private network" (DPN), and earn DPR through a novel "Proof of Credit" consensus, with credit scores grown "organically" from real bandwidth contribution. A prior review flagged that the Ethereum DPR contract carries multisig controlled mint, pause and freeze powers. This report independently confirms the token state on chain and, more importantly, reads the actual Substrate runtime to test whether the flagship decentralization and Proof of Credit claims survive contact with the code. The contract is secondary; the central question is whether the product does what the marketing says.

## Method

Read only and public. Ethereum token facts were read live from mainnet public RPC (`eth_call` for `name`, `symbol`, `decimals`, `totalSupply`, `paused`, `viewOwner`; `eth_getCode` for bytecode and for the owner contract; `eth_getStorageAt` for the EIP 1967 proxy slot and the Gnosis Safe singleton slot). The verified Solidity source was pulled via a public block explorer source endpoint and read in full. The owner was resolved and probed for Gnosis Safe interfaces (`getThreshold`, `getOwners`, `VERSION`). The Substrate side was audited by downloading raw source for the `credit`, `credit-accumulation`, `deeper-node`, `micropayment`, `staking`, `user-privileges` pallets and the `primitives` privilege enum, then reading the extrinsics, authorization checks, signature verification and reward payout paths directly. Runtime files were pinned to the master HEAD commit `ce700b14a5830e363ba08ec51483598d97d32419` after a branch name fetch of `runtime/src/lib.rs` returned a stale, unrelated blob; the pinned runtime is the genuine `spec_name: "deeper-chain"`, and the staking pallet was byte identical between the branch and the pinned commit. Market and traction figures came from CoinGecko and public project pages.

## The Foundation: A Real Chain and Real Hardware, Wrapped in an Overstated Decentralization Story

Deeper Network is not vaporware. There is a working Substrate chain (`deeper-chain`) with genuine pallets, real cryptographic payment channels, a shipped hardware product line (Deeper Connect), and a documented crowdfunding history of more than 2.72 million USD across Indiegogo and Kickstarter. That distinguishes it from projects that are pure narrative.

The problem is the gap between the story and the mechanism. The story is trustless: share bandwidth, prove it on chain, earn from a decentralized network. The mechanism in code is permissioned and stake driven: an off chain oracle key that root appoints signs a periodic heartbeat, that heartbeat bumps a credit counter by one regardless of how much bandwidth moved, and the size of your reward is then set by how much DPR you have staked, paid out as freshly minted inflation. The word the code itself uses is instructive. The primary credit setting extrinsic is annotated `/// This operation requires sudo now and it will be decentralized in future` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:480). The decentralization is aspirational, not shipped.

## Claim 1: The Decentralized Private Network is "decentralized traffic sharing with on chain incentives"

> CLAIM (marketing): "The Proof of Credit (PoCr) consensus algorithm allows users to earn passive income by sharing their unused internet bandwidth" through a "decentralized private network (DPN)."

REALITY: OVERSTATED, and FALSE on the specific word "decentralized". Bandwidth contribution is attested by a single off chain oracle key that the sudo/root origin appoints. The chain does not measure, prove, or verify any traffic volume. It verifies one signature from the "atmos" server key and then rate limits a credit counter.

EVIDENCE:
- The only extrinsic that grows credit from traffic is `add_credit_by_traffic`, which is `ensure_signed` but only accrues credit if a signature verifies against a stored oracle account: `Self::verify_atomos_signature(nonce, &signature, server.clone())?;` then `T::CreditInterface::update_credit_by_traffic(server);` (pallets/credit-accumulation/src/lib.rs:115-131).
- That oracle account is set by root, not by peers: `ensure_root(origin)?;` inside `set_atmos_pubkey` (pallets/credit-accumulation/src/lib.rs:141), loaded at line 166 and verified with `sr25519_verify` at line 202.
- Device registration stores no traffic and discards the IP address entirely. The stored node is `Node { account_id, country, expire }` (pallets/deeper-node/src/lib.rs:44-48), and the registration argument is the ignored `_ip: IpV4` (pallets/deeper-node/src/lib.rs:253-255). "Online" is a self reported heartbeat: `im_online` just inserts the current block (pallets/deeper-node/src/lib.rs:357-360).
- Where a traffic number does appear, `report_credit_proof` stores `daily_sharing_traffic` as a raw value gated by the same oracle signature (pallets/deeper-node/src/lib.rs:371-392), but that number is not what drives credit.

IMPACT: The "decentralized" traffic accounting is a centralized oracle attestation. Whoever controls the atmos key and the root origin controls who earns and how much. A single compromised or dishonest oracle key can fabricate credit for arbitrary accounts, and users cannot independently prove their contribution on chain. This is the core marketing claim, and it is not supported by the runtime.

## Claim 2: "Proof of Credit" is a novel consensus where organic credit is earned and proven

> CLAIM (marketing): Basic miners "start with a credit score of 0 and must grow their organic credit score to 100 before DPR can be earned," growing credit "organically by contributing bandwidth."

REALITY: OVERSTATED. Proof of Credit is not a consensus algorithm in the runtime; consensus is ordinary Substrate staking (Aura/GRANDPA style validator set). "Credit" is an off chain scored number written on chain, and the on chain increment is a flat plus one per time interval that never reads how much bandwidth was actually shared. Credit can also be set outright by privileged accounts.

EVIDENCE:
- The traffic driven increment ignores volume and adds exactly one credit once an interval elapses: `if time_eras >= CREDIT_CAP_ONE_ERAS { ... let new_credit = Self::get_credit_score(&server_id).unwrap_or(0).saturating_add(One::one()); }` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:1508-1530).
- Credit can be written wholesale by root: `add_or_update_credit_data(... ) { ensure_root(origin)?; ... }` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:540-548), and credit history can be force rewritten by root in `force_modify_credit_history` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:495-500).
- Credit can be written by a `CreditAdmin` privilege holder: `external_set_credit_data(...) { let admin = ensure_signed(origin)?; ensure!(Self::is_admin(&admin), Error::<T>::NotAdmin); ... }` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:772-782), where `is_admin` checks `Privilege::CreditAdmin` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:891).
- The one genuinely user driven path to raise credit is to burn DPR: `burn_for_add_credit` withdraws and burns DPR to add credit, capped at the user historical maximum (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:553-600). That is pay to reinstate credit, not proof of bandwidth.

IMPACT: The flagship "Proof of Credit" is a permissioned scoring system with a trustless veneer. The term describes a database of admin writable scores plus a heartbeat, not a cryptographic consensus over bandwidth work.

## Claim 3: You earn DPR by sharing bandwidth, "as high as 30,700+ DPR per month"

> CLAIM (marketing): "Users can earn DPR tokens through their contribution to the Deeper Network," with genesis mining "as high as 30,700+ DPR per month," framed as reward for sharing bandwidth.

REALITY: OVERSTATED and misleading. The reward magnitude is a staking yield on locked DPR, not a function of bandwidth shared. Bandwidth only matters to reach a nonzero credit level; once there, your daily reward equals an annual percentage yield times the DPR you staked, divided by 365. This is stake to earn dressed as share to earn.

EVIDENCE:
- Credit levels are literally staking tiers with fixed APYs. Level One requires a `staking_balance` of 5,000 DPR at `base_apy` 10 percent; Level Two 10,000 DPR at 15 percent; Level Three 20,000 DPR at 18 percent; Level Four 30,000 DPR at 20 percent; Level Five 50,000 DPR (pallets/credit/src/credit_setting.rs:28-84).
- The daily reward is computed straight from APY times stake: `base_total_reward = Perbill::from_rational(270, 365) * (credit_setting.base_apy * credit_setting.staking_balance)` and `base_daily_poc_reward = Perbill::from_rational(1, 270) * base_total_reward` (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:1095-1108), i.e. reward per day is approximately `base_apy * staking_balance / 365`.
- For the USDT campaign the same shape applies to a staked USD value, and non stakers with credit above level one are handed a virtual balance of just 25 USD: `daily_poc_reward = credit_setting.base_apy * dpr_amount / 365` with the "25 USD is virtual balance for those who not staking" fallback (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:1205-1238, comment at 1214-1216).

IMPACT: A user who shares a great deal of bandwidth but stakes little earns almost nothing, while a user who stakes 100,000 DPR and merely keeps a device online (heartbeat) earns the headline numbers. The advertised earnings are yield on capital locked, not payment for bandwidth delivered. The "30,700 DPR per month" figure is achievable only at large stake and elevated genesis APYs, and it is denominated in an asset that has lost more than 99.9 percent of its value.

## Claim 4: DPR is the token of a decentralized network (Ethereum ERC20 controls)

> CLAIM (implicit): DPR is a standard, decentralized network token. A prior review flagged multisig controlled mint, pause and freeze.

REALITY: CONFIRMED IN CODE. The Ethereum DPR contract grants its owner the power to mint new supply up to the 10 billion cap, to pause every transfer, and to freeze any individual account. The owner is a 3 of 5 Gnosis Safe multisig, which is a meaningful mitigant over a single key but leaves the powers fully intact. "Decentralized token" is therefore FALSE for the Ethereum asset.

EVIDENCE:
- Mint to owner, capped at 10 billion: `function mint(uint256 amount) public onlyOwner ... require(newSupply <= MAX_TOTAL_SUPPLY * 10 **uint256(decimals), "ERC20: exceed maximum total supply"); _totalSupply = newSupply; balances[owner] += amount;` with `MAX_TOTAL_SUPPLY = 10000000000` (dpr Token source, lines 525-538 and 345). Live supply is 6,616,479,552.71 DPR, so the owner can still mint roughly 3.38 billion DPR (about 51 percent of current supply) to itself.
- Pause halts everything: `function pause() onlyOwner whenNotPaused public { paused = true; ... }` (lines 197-205); `transfer`, `approve`, `transferFrom`, `burn` and `lock` all carry `whenNotPaused` (lines 402-441, 540-543, 555-560).
- Per account freeze/blacklist: `function freezeAccount(address _address, bool freeze) public onlyOwner ...` (lines 503-513); `_transfer` enforces `require(!frozenAccount[_from]); require(!frozenAccount[_to]);` (lines 388-389).
- Owner is a multisig: `viewOwner()` returns `0xb90182c7a8639de5ffd3f831009365d98971270f`, which has contract code whose storage slot 0 points to the Gnosis Safe 1.3.0 singleton `0xd9db270c1b5e3bd161e8c8503c55ceabee709552`; `getThreshold()` returns 3 and `getOwners()` returns 5 signer addresses; `VERSION()` returns "1.3.0" (live RPC reads, 2026-08-06).
- The contract is not a proxy: the EIP 1967 implementation slot reads zero, and the deployed bytecode is a full 15,626 byte implementation, so the logic is immutable.

IMPACT: A token listed on public exchanges can be frozen at the account level, halted network wide, and inflated by more than half again its current supply, at the discretion of five signers. These are custodial grade controls on a token sold as the fuel of a decentralized network.

## Claim 5: Real device traction and a bandwidth "sharing economy" that funds rewards

> CLAIM (marketing): a large, growing network of Deeper Connect devices sharing bandwidth, with mining rewards flowing from that shared economy.

REALITY: OVERSTATED. Hardware and crowdfunding are real, but the current decentralized node count is not independently verifiable from public on chain data, and the rewards are not funded by a bandwidth economy. Mining rewards are newly minted DPR (inflation) drawn from a soft, root refillable emission counter, plus privileged mint extrinsics.

EVIDENCE:
- Rewards are minted, not transferred from a revenue pool. The routine per device payout `pay_delegator` calls `T::Currency::deposit_creating(delegator, reward)` (pallets/staking/src/lib.rs:2788), the referral payout mints the same way (:2828), and validator payouts call `deposit_creating(&controller, amount)` (:2658). Each creates a `PositiveImbalance`, i.e. new tokens; no source account is ever debited. Both flows are paid automatically by the pallet block hooks (`on_initialize` at :2150 for devices, `end_era` at :2860 for validators), not by an offchain worker or a per payout root call.
- The emission is bounded only by a soft, refillable counter, not a hard supply cap. `RemainderMiningReward` (pallets/staking/src/lib.rs:887) is seeded from the `TotalMiningReward` constant (about 249,994,914 DPR; runtime/src/constants.rs:36-37) and decremented on each payout, halting with a `PoolEmpty` event when it reaches zero (:2688-2690). But root can raise the ceiling at will through `increase_mining_reward` (ensure_root, :1692-1698), and there is no check against `total_issuance` anywhere, so no immutable total supply cap exists on the appchain. The scheduled halving is disabled: the only halving line is commented out (:2201).
- There are direct privileged mint paths: `npow_mint` mints via `deposit_creating` gated by the `NpowMint` privilege (pallets/staking/src/lib.rs:2068-2090) and `difference_compensation` mints via `deposit_creating` gated by `ReleaseSetter` (:1962-1998).
- Traction proxy from market data: DPR trades near 0.00015 USD with about 1,766 USD of 24 hour volume, down 99.96 percent from the April 2021 all time high of 0.33 USD (CoinGecko, 2026-08-06). Documented hardware crowdfunding is 2.72 million USD historically.

IMPACT: Miners are paid by diluting a fixed emission schedule, not by end users purchasing bandwidth. Combined with an oracle gated heartbeat that does not measure traffic, the "sharing economy" that supposedly backs the token is not visible in the reward accounting.

## Positive Findings

- The Substrate codebase is real, non trivial, and internally coherent. The pallets compile a genuine credit, staking, micropayment and node registration system rather than empty scaffolding.
- Micropayment channels are cryptographically real. `open_channel` locks funds (pallets/micropayment/src/lib.rs:212-236), `close_channel` enforces expiry rules (line 263), and `claim_payment` verifies a client signature over server, nonce, session and amount before settling (lines 381, 420, 488-518). This is a legitimate unidirectional payment channel, even if the amount is negotiated off chain.
- The mining emission is accounted against a `TotalMiningReward` budget (about 250 million DPR) via a depleting `RemainderMiningReward` counter that halts payouts when empty (pallets/staking/src/lib.rs:887, 2688-2690). That is better than silent unbounded inflation, though the ceiling is soft: root can refill it via `increase_mining_reward` (:1692-1698), and no hard `total_issuance` cap is enforced.
- The Ethereum token has a coded hard cap of 10 billion enforced inside `mint` (line 532), and it is a plain immutable contract, not an upgradeable proxy, so its logic cannot be silently swapped.
- Token control sits behind a 3 of 5 Gnosis Safe rather than a single externally owned account, reducing single key compromise risk.
- The code is honest in at least one place about its own centralization, annotating the root only credit setter as something to "be decentralized in future" (pallets/credit/src/lib.rs:480).

## Conclusion

Deeper Network is a real product with a real chain, real hardware, and real payment channel plumbing, but its headline identity as a decentralized, trustless, bandwidth proven mining network is not supported by its own source. Bandwidth is attested by a single root appointed oracle key, the on chain credit increment is a flat plus one heartbeat that ignores traffic volume, and the reward you actually receive is a staking APY on locked DPR paid as minted inflation from a soft emission counter that root can refill, with no hard supply cap on the appchain. Credit and price can be overwritten directly by sudo/root, `CreditAdmin` and `OracleWorker` privileges. On Ethereum, a 3 of 5 multisig can mint roughly 3.38 billion more DPR to itself, pause all transfers, and freeze any account, on an asset that has lost more than 99.9 percent of its value and trades a few thousand dollars a day.

None of this makes Deeper a pure scam; the engineering is more substantial than most DePIN tokens flagged here. But the distance between the "Proof of Credit, decentralized private network" marketing and the permissioned, stake weighted, oracle gated reality is large enough that the flagship claims are OVERSTATED at best and FALSE where they assert decentralization and trustless bandwidth proof. Combined with severe token level central controls and a nearly dead market, the whole project verdict is Flagged.

Verdict tally: 4 core mechanisms CONFIRMED IN CODE (multisig mint/pause/freeze token controls, real micropayment channels, minted inflation emission bounded only by a soft root refillable counter, hardware plus crowdfunding), 3 flagship claims OVERSTATED (Proof of Credit consensus, share bandwidth to earn, bandwidth funded reward economy and device scale), and 2 claims FALSE (decentralized trustless traffic accounting, organic on chain proven credit).

**Score: 46/100, RISK: HIGH (Flagged).**
