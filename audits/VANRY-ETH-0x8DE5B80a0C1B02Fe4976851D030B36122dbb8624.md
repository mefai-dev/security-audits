## Executive Summary

The starting address is correct and verified live on Ethereum: name and symbol both read VANRY, decimals read 18, and the token is the new ERC20 issued in the one for one swap that rebranded Terra Virtua Kolect (TVK) into Vanar. Onchain reality is an OpenZeppelin style token that is mintable up to a fixed hard cap of 2.4 billion, transfer pausable, and governed by AccessControl roles rather than a single owner. Every privileged role (admin, minter, pauser) resolves to one Gnosis Safe multisig requiring three of four signers, and the contract is not a proxy, so its logic cannot be silently upgraded. The advertised maximum supply of 2.4 billion matches the onchain cap exactly, which is a strong positive. The main caveats are that a compromised or captured multisig could mint the roughly 63.7 million tokens of remaining headroom and could freeze all transfers, and that the homepage now redirects to a repositioned product while marketing claims about an AI focused L1, low fees, and carbon neutrality describe the separate Vanar L1 rather than this Ethereum ERC20.

## 1. Project and Product Reality

The Vanar mainnet is genuinely live and its Blockscout explorer reports about 24.6 million blocks, roughly 44.6 million lifetime transactions, a 3 second block time, and VANRY priced near 0.006 dollars as the native coin, so a working chain plainly exists. However present activity is very low, with only 786 transactions on 2 August 2026, a steady band of roughly 786 to 1150 transactions per day over the prior month, network utilization near 1.55 percent, only 109 verified contracts out of 1222 total, and zero new smart contracts deployed in the last 24 hours. The advertised AI products look shaky in practice, since myneutron.ai returned HTTP 502 on repeated checks and the vanarchain.com/vanar neutron page redirects to an onvanar.com URL that returns 404. The public site has itself pivoted to an AI Organizations narrative under a new Organization Commerce Protocol. On balance the L1 is real infrastructure but reads as a lightly used chain wrapped in a heavy and shifting AI marketing layer.

**Project level flags:** low chain activity around 786 to 1150 transactions per day and about 1.55 percent network utilization; zero new smart contracts in the last 24 hours and only 109 verified contracts; flagship myneutron.ai returns HTTP 502 and vanarchain.com/vanar neutron redirects to a 404 page; vanity claims such as first blockchain to abolish external storage and 500 to 1 compression; new flagship AI Organizations product settles value in USDC on Base rather than in VANRY; no DefiLlama chain listing; several partnerships framed as announcements of intent (Worldpay) rather than shipped integrations; multiple rebrands Terra Virtua to Virtua to Vanar plus repeated product repositioning

## 2. Website and Frontend Integrity

VERDICT: CLEAN | CONFIDENCE: high

The homepage at vanarchain.com redirects to onvanar.com, the project's own rebranded AI Organizations site operated by VANRY Technology DMCC, and it is a purely static marketing page with no wallet connect, no web3 or ethereum provider code, and no approve, permit, transfer, or signTypedData flows, so there is no drainer surface at all. I found zero contract addresses of any kind in the HTML or in every fetched script (app.js, the ocp video header scripts), meaning there is no lookalike or mismatched VANRY address to worry about; the token is referenced only as the plain text label $VANRY in navigation. The JavaScript is readable and unobfuscated, contains no eval, atob, base64, or fromCharCode tricks, and pulls no live metrics API, so all content is hardcoded rather than fetched. The only third party scripts are standard Google Tag Manager and gtag analytics, and other external hosts (veilprotocol.org, xbpp.org, myneutron.ai, cdn.onvanar.com, staking.vanarchain.com) are ordinary ecosystem links opened with rel noopener. The word audit appears solely in product marketing copy, not as an unbacked security audit badge, so nothing here misrepresents a third party attestation.

## 3. Traction and Claims

Vanar names credible partners including Google Cloud, NVIDIA, Emirates Digital Wallet, Worldpay and Movement Labs, and Google Cloud does host a customer page and a fellowship program in Pakistan. The depth of these ties is thin, as the Worldpay item is explicitly an announcement of intent to explore Web3 payments rather than a shipped integration, and the Google relationship centers on cloud hosting and an education program rather than protocol usage. The chain is not listed as a DeFi chain on DefiLlama, so there is no independently tracked TVL, and the homepage discloses no user, transaction, or revenue figures. Marketing language leans on strong vanity claims such as being the first blockchain to abolish external storage and achieving 500 to 1 compression, which are hard to verify. Traction is therefore a mix of real but shallow partnerships and unverified usage.

## 4. Token Utility and Economics

VANRY is a real utility token in the narrow sense that it is the native gas coin of the Vanar L1, confirmed by the explorer showing it as the fee currency at about 1.25 gwei. Yet the utility is limited because on chain activity is very thin and the market cap sits near 13.7 million dollars against a circulating supply around 2.15 billion. Notably the projects newest flagship, the AI Organizations and Organization Commerce Protocol, is described as settling in USDC on Base rather than in VANRY on Vanar, which routes economic value away from the token. So VANRY has genuine but modest gas utility that the latest product direction does not reinforce.

The listed website vanarchain.com issues a 301 redirect to onvanar.com, which has clearly repositioned around AI Orgs, an organization commerce protocol, and a Neutron memory layer, and it even names Base as a settlement layer. The Vanar documentation and whitepaper still present the classic pitch of an EVM compatible Layer 1 for gaming and entertainment with VANRY as native gas, fixed fees near 0.0005 dollars, carbon neutral operations, and a five layer AI stack of Neutron, Kayon, Axon and Flows. These are marketing and product claims about the L1 network and its offchain services. They are not properties of the Ethereum ERC20 being traded, so a buyer should not read L1 fee or AI features into this token.

## 5. Claim versus Reality

- "AI focused EVM Layer 1" / Reality: a Vanar L1 does exist and uses VANRY as its native gas coin, but this audited asset is the Ethereum ERC20 representation, not the L1 and not the native gas coin. Nothing AI related is provable from this token contract; the AI stack is an offchain product claim.
- "Neutron AI" / Reality: Neutron is an offchain product now described as a semantic memory layer for AI agents. The token contract holds no Neutron logic. Branding has drifted from Neutron AI toward a memory layer narrative, and the homepage now redirects to onvanar.com which pivots to AI Orgs.
- "Low fees near 0.0005 dollars" / Reality: that fixed fee applies to Vanar L1 gas, not to this Ethereum ERC20, whose transfers pay ordinary Ethereum gas in ETH. Not verifiable from this contract.
- "Carbon neutral" / Reality: an operational and offchain claim about the L1 that cannot be confirmed or denied from onchain token data.
- "Maximum supply 2.4 billion" / Reality: confirmed. The onchain cap() equals 2,400,000,000, so the disclosed max supply is honest and enforced by code.

## 6. Contract Security Check (supporting)

- Supply and cap: onchain Ethereum totalSupply reads 2,336,316,616 VANRY; ERC20 cap() reads exactly 2,400,000,000 VANRY, matching the advertised max supply; CoinGecko lists circulating near 2.15 billion. Remaining mint headroom to the cap is about 63.7 million (roughly 2.6 percent of the cap).
- Owner and admin: there is no Ownable owner (owner() reverts). Control is via AccessControlEnumerable. DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE, MINTER_ROLE and PAUSER_ROLE each have exactly one member, and it is the same address 0x8a908ce240a547ce837ca28b3cca7a31f6251b12, which is a Gnosis Safe multisig version 1.3.0 with a threshold of three out of four owners.
- Mint and emission: mintable up to the fixed cap only. A holder of MINTER_ROLE can mint new tokens until totalSupply reaches 2.4 billion, after which minting reverts. No infinite inflation, no rebase, no hidden emission function found.
- Upgradeable, pausable, fee, decimals: not a proxy (EIP1967 implementation and admin storage slots are empty and implementation() reverts), so the logic is immutable. Pausable is present (paused() returns false today but PAUSER_ROLE can halt all transfers). No fee on transfer and no blacklist function were found (blacklist selectors revert). Decimals are 18.

Live reads over public RPC confirm name VANRY, symbol VANRY, 18 decimals, total supply 2,336,316,616, and a cap of 2,400,000,000. The token uses AccessControlEnumerable, and DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE, MINTER_ROLE and PAUSER_ROLE each have a single holder that is a Gnosis Safe multisig at 0x8a908ce240a547ce837ca28b3cca7a31f6251b12 with a threshold of three of four. Minting is allowed only until the cap is reached, transfers are pausable, and no fee on transfer or blacklist logic was detected. The EIP1967 proxy slots are empty and implementation() reverts, confirming the logic is not upgradeable. This is the new ERC20 from the TVK rebrand at 0xd084b83c305dafd76ae3e1b4e1f1fe2ecccb3988, and the same address is also deployed on Polygon and on the Vanar L1 where VANRY additionally serves as the separate native gas coin.
