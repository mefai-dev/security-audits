## 3. Claim versus Reality

- "A 1 billion fixed supply with daily rewards and an 80 percent revenue buy back and burn" / Reality: confirmed on chain, with rewards distributed from the premined supply rather than minted (the minter role has no members and the cap is reached) and burns executed as dead address transfers.

The docs describe a one billion fixed supply, daily miner rewards on a halving schedule, and an eighty percent revenue buy back and burn, and the public data agrees. The onchain cap equals the onchain total supply at exactly one billion, the minter role holds no members, and about 36.7 million GEOD are verifiably held at the dead burn address, which is why trackers report a lower net total against a raw onchain figure of one billion. Circulating supply near 454 million, about 45 percent of max, is consistent with the multi year vesting and reward release the project describes.

## 4. Website and Frontend Integrity

VERDICT: CLEAN | CONFIDENCE: high
The public GEODNET homepage and its main JavaScript bundles are clean from a frontend security standpoint. It is a prerendered marketing site that never asks visitors to connect a wallet, sign a message, or approve a token, so there is no drainer surface at all, and it contains no embedded wallet or contract addresses that could be swapped for a lookalike. Live network figures such as miner and console counts come from the project's own API and a WebSocket at websiteapi.geodnet.com rather than being faked as static text, and every outbound link points either to a first party geodnet.com subdomain or to well established analytics and social platforms. The only slightly unusual code, a base64 decode paired with a document write, is a routine image export helper that opens a new window, not obfuscated remote execution, and the site loads no untrusted third party scripts beyond standard analytics. Overall the marketing and the code agree, and the frontend reads as safe for end users, with the caveat that the review covered the homepage and its main bundles rather than every internal subdomain app such as the store and console.
