## Executive Summary

GEODNET markets itself as the world's largest decentralized Real Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS network, a DePIN that pays base station operators in GEOD and burns GEOD from real data revenue. The audit finds that, unusually for the category, the marketing and the reality line up well. The network is genuinely large and in use, the revenue is real, and the token is doing real work.

1. **The RTK DePIN is real and at scale.** Independent coverage from Messari and DePIN trackers places the network at roughly twenty thousand active satellite miners, over twenty thousand RTK base stations, across about 145 countries, which is consistent with the project's claim to run the largest RTK network in the world. A live console and coverage map let anyone verify station density.
2. **Traction is measurable and growing.** RTK data service revenue rose across 2025 from about 812 thousand dollars in the first quarter to about 1.2 million dollars in the third quarter, with annualized run rate reported of about five million dollars by year end. The website presents these figures through its own live API and a WebSocket rather than as static text.
3. **The token is actually used.** Miners earn GEOD daily for contributing data, data customers pay protocol service fees in GEOD to consume the RTK correction streams, and eighty percent of data revenue funds a buy back and burn. About 36.7 million GEOD, roughly 3.67 percent of supply, is verifiably sitting at the dead burn address onchain.
4. **The contract is effectively immutable and clean.** GEOD is a hard capped one billion supply token that is fully minted, source verified, not a proxy, and carries no fee on transfer. Every privileged role has been renounced, so no party can mint, pause, upgrade, or reassign control.

The residual concerns are modest and sit at the transparency and centralization layer, not at the contract or delivery layer. The premined allocation percentages are not filled in the docs, the liquidity lock is not chain verified, and the network operations and reward issuance are run by a single company. None of these undercut the core finding. GEODNET is a real DePIN that delivers and whose token is genuinely used, which lands it at 76 out of 100, Passed.

## 2. On chain Security Assessment (MEFAI analysis)

MEFAI's direct read of the GEOD contract on Polygon returned:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Token identity | Geodnet Token, GEOD, 18 decimals, source verified (Sourcify, September 2024) |
| Max supply | 1,000,000,000, cap equals total supply, fully minted |
| Mint authority | mint() and MINTER_ROLE exist in bytecode, but MINTER_ROLE has zero members and the cap is reached, so no new tokens can be created |
| Admin authority | AccessControlEnumerable with DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE member count of zero, control renounced |
| Pause authority | Pause code present but the pause role has zero members, so it is inert; paused() reads false |
| Upgradeable | No, implementation and admin proxy slots are empty |
| Transfer fee | None |
| Burn evidence | About 36.7 million GEOD, roughly 3.67 percent, held at the 0xdEaD dead address |

**Interpretation.** At the contract level GEOD is among the cleaner tokens in the DePIN category. It is a hard capped, fully minted, non upgradeable, source verified ERC 20 with no fee on transfer, and crucially every privileged role has been renounced, so the token is effectively immutable. There is no live mint key, no active pause key, and no proxy admin, which removes the usual centralization red flags at the token layer. One presentational nuance is worth noting: the buy back and burn moves tokens to the dead address rather than calling burn(), so the reported total supply stays a flat one billion and a reader has to inspect the dead address balance to see the real reduction. This is a transparency point, not a safety defect. The remaining project level considerations are discussed in the claim sections below.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "World's Largest RTK GPS DePIN"

> Site: GEODNET presents itself as the world's largest blockchain powered navigation network, a decentralized RTK network of base stations that correct standard GPS for robots, drones, and machines.

**Reality: the network is real, large, and independently corroborated.** Independent coverage from Messari and DePIN trackers places GEODNET at roughly twenty thousand active satellite miners and over twenty thousand RTK base stations spread across about 145 countries, which matches the largest RTK network claim rather than inflating it. The project exposes a live console and a coverage map where station density and individual miner health, for example signal to noise ratio, can be inspected directly. This is not a whitepaper network. It is deployed hardware in the field producing a real positioning service, and the scale is verifiable through third parties.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Live Network Metrics and Traction

> Site: GEODNET advertises a growing network and rising data revenue, presenting live miner counts, country coverage, RTK usage, and annual recurring revenue on its homepage.

**Reality: the metrics are live and the revenue is real and growing.** MEFAI's frontend review confirmed that the homepage figures for miner and console counts are pulled from the project's own API and a WebSocket at websiteapi.geodnet.com, not hard coded as static marketing text, so the numbers move with the actual network. On traction, RTK data service revenue climbed through 2025 from about 812 thousand dollars in the first quarter to about 963 thousand in the second and about 1.2 million in the third, with annualized run rate reported of about five million dollars toward year end. Revenue of this kind, paid by real customers for a positioning service, is exactly the sort of traction most DePIN marketing implies but cannot show. Here it is documented in quarterly third party research.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: GEOD Utility, Miner Rewards and Data Payments

> Site: GEOD is described as the utility token of the network, earned daily by station operators and used to pay for RTK data service.

**Reality: the token is genuinely used on both sides of the market.** Station operators earn GEOD daily for contributing quality data, on a reward schedule whose base emission halves each year, and the rewards are paid out of the premined supply rather than freshly minted, which is consistent with the renounced minter role and the reached cap. On the demand side, data customers pay protocol service fees in GEOD to access the RTK correction streams, so the token is the actual medium of the service, not a decorative asset bolted onto an unrelated product. This two sided use, earn to supply and pay to consume, is a materially stronger utility story than most tokens in this space can substantiate.

---

## 6. Claim vs Reality: Eighty Percent Revenue Buy Back and Burn

> Site: GEODNET states that eighty percent of its data revenue is used to buy back and burn GEOD, permanently reducing supply as the network grows.

**Reality: the burn is real and visible onchain.** Third party research records rising quarterly burns through 2025, roughly 2.25 million GEOD in the first quarter, about 3.8 million in the second, and about 6 million in the third, and MEFAI confirmed about 36.7 million GEOD, roughly 3.67 percent of the fixed supply, sitting at the dead address as the all time total. The mechanism ties token demand to actual data revenue, which is a coherent value capture design rather than an arbitrary deflation gimmick. The one caveat, already noted, is that the burn is executed as transfers to the dead address, so the reported ERC 20 total supply stays a flat one billion and the reduction is only visible when the dead address balance is checked.

## 11. Conclusion

GEODNET is a rare case in the DePIN category where the marketing and the reality substantially match. The RTK GPS network is real and one of the largest of its kind, roughly twenty thousand miners across about 145 countries, verifiable through a live console and independent research. The traction is measurable, with data revenue growing quarter over quarter and an annualized run rate in the millions, and the token is genuinely used on both sides of the market, earned by operators and spent by data customers, with eighty percent of revenue funding a burn that is visible onchain. The contract reinforces this: hard capped, fully minted, source verified, non upgradeable, and with every privileged role renounced, so it is effectively immutable. The remaining concerns are transparency and operational centralization, the blank premine allocation, the unverified liquidity lock, the single operator, and burns presented as dead address transfers, which temper the score but do not undermine the core reality. GEODNET scores 76 out of 100, Passed, as a real network that delivers and whose token is actually used.

## 12. Recommendations

**For the GEODNET team:**
- Fill in the premined allocation percentages in the public docs and publish the liquidity lock so supply and float can be verified end to end.
- Consider executing the buy back and burn through an ERC 20 burn() call, or clearly document the dead address method, so the reported total supply reflects the real reduction.
- Continue publishing quarterly revenue and burn figures through independent researchers to keep the traction story auditable.
- Progressively decentralize network operations and reward issuance beyond a single operating company where feasible.

**For users:**
- Understand that the reported total supply stays a flat one billion onchain because burns are dead address transfers; check the dead address balance to see the real reduction.
- Recognize that while the token contract is effectively immutable, the network, console, and reward issuance are operated by a single company, which is an off chain centralization to weigh.
- Verify current miner counts, revenue, and burn totals through the live console and independent trackers rather than relying on any single figure.
