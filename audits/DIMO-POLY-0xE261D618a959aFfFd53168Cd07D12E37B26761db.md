## Executive Summary

DIMO is a real connected vehicle DePIN with genuine hardware and software integrations and a verified token on Polygon. It is not a scam. The MEDIUM rating reflects an emission driven, admin upgradeable, control heavy reality behind the user owned framing:

1. **The token is verified but mintable and upgradeable:** MEFAI confirms a verified Polygon token at roughly 647 million current supply, but the token is an upgradeable proxy and is mintable toward a policy cap of about 1 billion via a baseline emission controlled by DIMO governance, so supply grows and the logic is admin upgradeable.
2. **The hardware and integrations are real,** with drivers connecting vehicles through a device or app to share and monetize data.
3. **But usage is emission subsidized.** Rewards are paid largely from token emissions rather than clearly organic data buyer revenue, and connection counts are self reported.
4. **Governance and the minter are controlled by the project,** against the user owned decentralization framing.

The hardware and integrations are real; the caution is emission driven dilution, an upgradeable and mint controlled token, self reported metrics and a drawdown.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "User Owned Vehicle Data"

> Site: a user owned connected vehicle network where drivers plug in a device or connect an app to share and monetize their car data and earn DIMO, a mobility DePIN.

**Reality: real hardware, emission subsidized usage, controlled token.** DIMO has a genuine hardware and software product with real connected vehicle integrations and verifiable on chain vehicle definitions, which distinguishes it from typical projects. But the economy is emission driven: contributor rewards are paid largely from a preset token emission, and while a paid data marketplace does exist (data credits settled in DIMO), its revenue is small and unaudited relative to those emissions. Vehicles are minted as on chain NFTs but active connection status is self attested, and buyer side demand is early. The user owned framing understates that the token is mintable toward a cap under governance control, is an admin upgradeable and pausable proxy, and that emissions rather than paid demand sustain the rewards.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Supply, Control and Value

- **Emission driven dilution:** the token is mintable toward a policy cap of about 1 billion via a baseline emission, so supply grows and non staker holders are diluted.
- **Upgradeable and mint controlled:** the token is an upgradeable proxy and the minter and emission are controlled by DIMO governance, a real centralization consideration against the user owned framing.
- **Emission subsidized usage:** rewards come largely from emissions, and real data buyer revenue is early relative to the subsidy.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 99 percent from its December 2023 peak, a thin, near illiquid micro capitalization.

## 8. Conclusion

DIMO is a real connected vehicle DePIN with genuine hardware, software integrations and verifiable on chain vehicle definitions, and a verified Polygon token, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 58/100. It is held back because the token is mintable toward a policy cap of about 1 billion via a governance controlled emission and is an admin upgradeable proxy, because rewards are emission subsidized rather than clearly organic data buyer revenue with self reported connection counts, because governance and the minter are project controlled against the user owned framing, and because it is down roughly 99 percent from its December 2023 peak to a thin, near illiquid micro capitalization. The hardware and integrations are real; the caution is emission driven dilution, a controlled and upgradeable token, self reported metrics and the drawdown.

---

## 9. Recommendations

**For the DIMO team:**
- Present the emission schedule, the policy cap and the minter and upgrade controls transparently.
- Report real data buyer revenue separately from emission driven reward activity, and publish verifiable connected vehicle counts.
- Deliver a clear path toward less centralized minter and upgrade control.

**For users:**
- Understand the token is mintable toward a cap under governance control and is an upgradeable proxy, and that rewards are emission subsidized.
- Value the real hardware and integrations on their merits, and model the dilution and the drawdown.
