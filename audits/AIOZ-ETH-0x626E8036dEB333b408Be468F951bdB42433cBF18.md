## Executive Summary

AIOZ Network is a real, long running decentralized infrastructure network with a verified token, a public founding team and its own Layer 1. It is not vaporware. The MEDIUM rating reflects a gap between the "Web3 AI, storage and streaming DePIN" branding and a storage and content delivery core with an inflationary token:

1. **The project is legitimate and long running:** MEFAI confirms a standard, non exotic token contract, a public founder, multiple years of activity and a native Cosmos based, EVM compatible Layer 1.
2. **But the proven substance is storage and content delivery, not AI.** The AI compute layer is heavily marketed but still largely aspirational and early, while the demonstrated core is decentralized storage and a content delivery network.
3. **The headline node count is self reported,** and only the roughly 50 chain validators are cryptographically verifiable; the large edge node figures are off chain devices without a published geographic breakdown.
4. **The token is uncapped and inflationary,** though supply is roughly 99.9 percent circulating so unlock risk is low, and the token is down roughly 98 percent from its 2021 peak.

The team and network are real; the caution is uncapped inflation, self reported node and usage claims, and an AI narrative ahead of a storage and delivery core.

## 3. Claim vs Reality: "DePIN for Web3 AI, Storage and Streaming"

> Site: a decentralized physical infrastructure network for Web3 AI, storage and streaming, powered by a large global set of edge nodes.

**Reality: a storage and content delivery core with an aspirational AI layer.** The demonstrated products are decentralized storage and a content delivery network (an S3 style storage layer and a peer to peer streaming and delivery layer, with a video app as a demonstrator). The AI compute layer is heavily marketed but still largely a roadmap and early stage, not a demonstrated at scale AI business. So AI is the narrative and storage and delivery are the substance.

---

## 4. Claim vs Reality: Node Count, Decentralization and Usage

- **Node count is self reported:** the large edge node figure is an off chain device count, not cryptographically verifiable, and only the roughly 50 chain validators can be verified on chain. There is no published country by country geographic breakdown despite the "global" framing.
- **Node onboarding is permissionless (positive):** anyone with a computer and internet can run the node app and earn AIOZ, with no gatekeeping, while the validator set remains permissioned by stake.
- **Usage and revenue are self reported:** the burn mechanics reference infrastructure and application revenue, implying real revenue, but no audited or on chain revenue figures are disclosed.
- **Team and backers:** the founding team is public (a named chief executive) and the project was self funded for years before raising, which is a positive on transparency.

---

## 5. Claim vs Reality: Supply and Value

- **Uncapped inflation:** AIOZ has no hard cap; issuance runs around 6 percent declining toward 5 percent, split between stakers and the treasury and partly offset by burns. Dilutive but modest given the near fully circulating supply.
- **Low unlock risk:** with roughly 99.9 percent already circulating, there is very little future unlock or cliff dump risk.
- **Value:** the token is down roughly 98 percent from its 2021 peak and well below a 2024 secondary peak, though the project has survived multiple cycles.

## 9. Conclusion

AIOZ Network is a real, long running decentralized infrastructure network with a verified token, a public founder and its own EVM compatible Layer 1, which keeps it in the MEDIUM band at 68/100. It is held back because the marketed AI compute layer is still largely aspirational while the proven substance is storage and content delivery, because the large edge node count and usage and revenue figures are self reported and only the roughly 50 validators are on chain verifiable, and because the token is uncapped and inflationary against a roughly 98 percent drawdown. The team and network are real; the caution is uncapped inflation, self reported claims and an AI narrative ahead of the core.

---

## 10. Recommendations

**For the AIOZ team:**
- Distinguish the demonstrated storage and content delivery business from the aspirational AI compute layer in messaging.
- Publish a verifiable node count with a geographic breakdown and independently verifiable usage and revenue figures.
- Present the uncapped inflation clearly.

**For users:**
- Understand AI is the narrative and storage and delivery are the current substance.
- Treat node, usage and revenue claims as self reported; note the uncapped inflation is mitigated by near full circulation, and model the drawdown.
